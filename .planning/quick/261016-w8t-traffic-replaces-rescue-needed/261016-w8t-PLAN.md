<objective>
Stop offering the Rescue needed report category for new reports and add a Traffic category in its
place (owner decision, 2026-10-16). Traffic means a slow or jammed road a person can still get
through; Road blocked stays for a road nobody can use. Critical severity still covers danger to life.

Reports already stored with rescue_needed keep every current rule until they fade: the critical or
rescue_needed visibility bypass in visibility.go, the finished rescue blur in coarsen.go, the serious
rate limit bucket, the screening rules, their label, their icon and the Rescue chip. Only the path
that accepts a NEW report refuses rescue_needed.

Purpose: another Claude session (pinalert-4a, Phase 3.4 app layout) builds its new report form on
top of exactly this behaviour, so the design below is locked and must not drift.

Output: a split category list in Go and in the browser, a Traffic tile, label and icon, contract
tests for the refusal and for old rows, regenerated Swagger, and a dated note in docs/TRUST-MODEL.md.
</objective>

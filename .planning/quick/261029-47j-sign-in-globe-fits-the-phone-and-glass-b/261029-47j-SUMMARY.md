# Quick 261029-47j: sign in Earth fits the phone, glass boxes

The whole Earth now sits in a CSS reserved box above the sign in card on phones (sphere = box / 1.06, box 140px to 300px), the Earth is at full strength, and the card, Check your inbox and the Low data panel are 65 percent frosted glass while the globe is on, with solid fallbacks. Laptop sizing and placement are unchanged (764px sphere in a 809px box at 1470x830, identical to the base run).

## What was built

- **login-globe.css**: `--globe-dim: 1` in all four theme blocks; a glass section scoped under `.login-gate.has-globe` (column transparent; card, `#check-inbox`, `.login-ways` 65 percent surface plus 4px blur; the three panel buttons 65 percent page colour, not on hover, no blur; red error line solid surface backing); a phone only `@media (max-width: 899.98px)` block (`--g` with 100vh value, nested `@supports (height: 100svh)` value, globe-area `display: contents`, relative box above the card, card margin-top `calc(var(--g) * -0.22)`, credit centred in flow); three solid fallbacks (no backdrop-filter, no color-mix, reduced transparency).

A box of 0 returns diameter 0, `startGlobe` returns quietly before any download and `buildMap` gives up with "the reserved box has no size". Laptop branch is the old code.
- **Tests**: five new Go pins (reserved phone box, glass fallbacks, full strength, glass only with the globe, contrast floor) and six node cases (amended 390x844, box not area, box 0, zoom from box, laptop branch at 890 with phone false, 1470x830 pin).

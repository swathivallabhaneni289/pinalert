## Decisions the owner should see

- **R3, one person extends once (Claude's call).** It follows the independence rule behind "confirmed by N nearby" and the promise already in TRUST-MODEL.md that walking around cannot add places. A confirm, dispute, confirm toggle never extends twice, and a second account in a taken back spot does not extend either, because the whole append only log is the reference.
- **R7 as reversed by A1: a finished report is never extended.** A resolved report, a faded one or one in any other finished state gets Extend false. A finished report has no life left to extend, and extending it would delay the reliability judging that waits for expiry. CastVote reads the current votes once before the write (only for a content confirm), runs `Resolve`, and passes the answer through the shared helper `mayExtend` (built on `ViewOf`), which `ConfirmExtends` also uses. A reporter who reopens a resolved report makes it extendable again (pinned). The accepted race, stated in TRUST-MODEL.md under Honest limits: a resolve landing between the pre read and the write can still let that one confirm extend; the report stays resolved either way.
- The Policy and `CurrentTermsVersion` were not touched (still 2026-10-29). The Disclaimer sentence was edited.

Step 1 of 5

Welcome to Pinalert

  • Know what is happening around you. People nearby post short reports: a flooded road, an open shelter, a power cut, a jammed road.
  • See how far to trust each report. Others nearby confirm or dispute it. The more different places confirm a report, the more likely it is true, but a report can still be wrong.
  • Pinalert cannot send help. It tells you a situation is happening near you. If someone is in danger, call 112 first.

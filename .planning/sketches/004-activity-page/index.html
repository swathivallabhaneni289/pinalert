Where it differs from your picture, on purpose

  • The typeface is the system font, because the app loads no web fonts, so the letters differ a little.
  • Every row has the height of rows 1 to 3. The picture draws rows 5 to 7 shorter, which looks like the image generator running out of room.
  • One title weight on every row (the picture mixes bold and medium), and the thin bar on the left of each card takes the colour of its round icon (the picture mixes grey, amber and black).
  • The descriptions in the right box line up in one column.
  • The 127.0.0.1 box in the top bar is the browser's own address bar in the picture. It is drawn, and it does nothing.

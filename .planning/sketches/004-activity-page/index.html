What this is

The Activity page with the look of your picture, in Pinalert's own colours, sizes and corners. Each row reuses the trust block you chose in sketch 003 (variant H), so the confirmed counts, the state word and the reporter words are the real parts and not drawings. The page and its tabs use the app's real stylesheets, so the tokens, sizes and states are the app's own.

What to look for

  • At 360 wide, does a row read in one pass: who did what (the small label), how serious (the severity label), what it is (the title), when (the line under it), how well backed (the state word, the reporter words and the counts)?
  • Switch "Counts, state word and reporter words" to "Like the picture". The page is calmer, but nothing says a report is Disputed or Resolved, the counts are gone, and the Phase 3 rule that both trust signals show on every report without opening anything would need a recorded change. Recommended: keep them.
  • All is your posts and your votes, newest first. My posts has the Active and Resolved filter. Your disputes sit inside My votes as "Your dispute". At 320 the three tabs still fit.
  • The dashed rows (All tab only) are updates that Phase 3.2 adds. They are drawn here only to show where they would sit.
  • Press a row or its arrow. It says where it would go: the report on the map, through a link Phase 3.1 adds. The trust block, the Reopen button and the back arrow keep their own presses.
  • Try the text size at 200%. The tab strip scrolls sideways instead of wrapping, and the rows grow taller.

What your picture has that this leaves out

  • The distance on each row. This page does not know where you are.
  • The left side menu and the top bar. That is the separate design pass for the whole app.
  • Six tabs. Disputes sit inside My votes, and Resolved and Active are a filter inside My posts, as your second picture recommends.
  • Round icons coloured by the kind of event. The round icon keeps its colour for severity, so colour keeps one meaning.
  • A "System update" row with its own chip on the right. Here an update is a normal row with the label Update, dashed, arriving with Phase 3.2.

What changes from the Activity page that ships today

  • Rows are neutral cards (page colour, hairline, 12px corners) with the 4px severity bar, not flat rows tinted by severity. The map list rows keep their tint for now.
  • The small label and the severity label share the first line, and the title has an arrow.
  • The email line is gone from the page. The account menu already shows it.
  • Tabs, count chips and the Active and Resolved filter are new. The selected tab is underlined with a 1px line, not the picture's 2px, because the design rules allow hairlines and the 4px severity bar only.

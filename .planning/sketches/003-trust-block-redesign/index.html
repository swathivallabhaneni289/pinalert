Known weaknesses

    What to look for

    • Move the pointer over the trust block, then press it (or use Show state). Is anything underlined anywhere? Without hover, as on a phone, is it clear that the block opens?
    • Where do the reporter words sit, and do they read as plain words and not as a tag?
    • Open the panel and use Panel wording to compare the proposed sentences with today's. Is it short enough now?
    • Try the 320 width on the Activity row (the narrowest, 188px of text) at 150%, 175% and 200% text size. Does anything clip, push sideways or split a count into "7" and "confirmed"? From about 145% the chevron moves under the text on that row. Does it still read as the end of the button?

    The surfaces use the real stylesheets from web/static/css, so the tokens, sizes and states are the app's own. All names, numbers and sentences are sample data. The vote buttons are only there for context: Confirm and Dispute flip their pressed look and never change a count, because the app never predicts a vote. Activity states 7 and 8 only show on the Activity page in the app.

# Phase 3.4 Plan 19: Take back on Ended nearby Summary

**The person who marked a report resolved can take the mark back from its My activity, Ended nearby row (Undo, or the used both undos sentence), through one new votes.js builder over the existing take back block, state decision and handler, with the server still the only judge of who may and of the limit of two.**

## What was built

- **votes.js.** `createTakeBackFor(report, options)` builds `createTakeBackBlock(report.id)`, drops the statement and the poster Reopen from it, adds the error line, decides the visible part with the existing `takeBackState` (`undo` shows the button, `used` shows the sentence, every other state returns null), and wires the Undo button to the existing `onTakeBackClick` with its labels, toasts and error mapping. It returns `{ el, update(report), message(), showMessage(text) }`. `onTakeBackClick` now reaches its refetch through two small helpers, `readAfterWrite(block)` and `readFailed(block)`: a block with `readAgain` uses it, any other block still calls `Pinalert.fetchReports({ afterWrite: true })` and checks `Pinalert.state.status` as before. `takeBackState`, `castVote` and `createVoteBlock` bodies are untouched (the diff hunks all sit after line 1661 apart from the three changed lines inside `onTakeBackClick` and the export line).
- **ended-cards.js.** `updateEndedRow` calls `mountTakeBack`, which asks the builder once per row, appends its element as the last line of the What happened cell, and calls `update` on later refreshes. No fetch, no limit literal, no field name of the trust or vote state.
- **ended-nearby.js.** `load(quiet)` and `refresh(reportId)`: a re-read with no skeleton, no toast, no scroll, rows left as they are if the read fails. A refusal line is carried to the same report's new row; when a redraw took focus it goes to the new Undo button, else the row, else the part title.
- **activity.css.** Layout for the take back inside the What happened cell, in tokens only (44px, no opacity, no new colour).
- **profile.html.tmpl.** votes.js moved ahead of ended-cards.js and ended-nearby.js.

# Phase 3.4 Plan 43: Settings as a grouped list Summary

Settings now reads as a settings screen on both sizes: a short heading over each group, its one line under it, one rounded filled surface per group with inset hairlines between rows, the current value in the text colour, and Log out in its own last group in the critical colour, now also on the laptop.

## What changed

- `settings.html.tmpl`: `<main>` gets the modifier `place-page--settings`; the Account section loses `place-section--phone` (owner addition, below). No row, id, href, form or window changed.
- `places.css`: a Settings block scoped to `.place-page--settings` (phone base) and a laptop block (centred column, max 640px, heading above, no section grid or top borders). The phone only hide `.place-section--phone` is removed. Official alerts shares the file and is pixel identical before and after (PNG `cmp` at 390 and 1280).
- Found on the way: the old hairline rule `.place-box > * + *` never drew, because `.place-row { border: 0 }` has the same weight and comes later. Measured before: every row `border-top 0px`. Now each row after the first draws a 1px inset line through `::before` under a heavier selector.

## Owner addition during the run (2026-10-21)

"I don't see a logout option on my laptop version, so I think we should add that."

- What I found: the laptop has a person menu in the top bar (the round person button at the top right, 44 by 44 at x 1220, y 8 at 1280 wide). Clicking it opens a menu with exactly Settings and Log out, and Log out is visible and reachable there. The Settings page, though, hid its Account section on the laptop (`.place-section--phone { display: none }` at 900px and up, D-25), so the laptop Settings page had no Log out.
- Done: the Account section with the same `POST /auth/logout` form is drawn on both sizes as the last group, in the critical colour at weight 600. No new endpoint. The person menu is unchanged and keeps its Log out.
- Checked at 1280 wide: the row is visible (592 by 52), its label is rgb(192, 57, 43), and clicking it lands on `/login`. After that, `/settings` answers 302.
- UI-SPEC Surface 6 still says the laptop has no Account section. I did not edit the UI-SPEC, because plan 42 is running in parallel. That line needs an owner amendment note.

## App defects found (not fixed here; for a gap closure plan)

Every FAIL in the reported run is one of these two. Each is marked `[KNOWN APP DEFECT: ...]` in the results file.

1. **The sheet has no wheel form of its scroll rule** (T18.6d2, 6 per engine: 320, 360 and 390 px, light and dark; Chromium and WebKit). UI-SPEC 2b words the rule "upward scroll (finger up, wheel down) while the sheet is not Full first grows it to Full, then the list scrolls". `web/static/js/sheet.js` has touch listeners only. Evidence at 390x844: a 160px mouse wheel turn over a row at Half leaves `data-snap` half, the sheet top at 324px and the list scrolled 160px (320x640: top 232px, 360x740: top 277px, same list scroll). The touch form passes (T18.6d). New finding; the fix is either a wheel listener on the sheet or a change of the spec text to "finger up" only. A person on a laptop at a narrow window, or a phone with a mouse, is the only one who sees it.
2. **Laptop Nearby shell is 2px taller than the window** (T18.2b at 1280x800, light and dark; Chromium and WebKit): `scrollHeight - innerHeight` is 2. This is plan 17's finding 1, already assigned to gap plan 21; recorded here with the same reason, never weakened.

Other findings of plan 17 (the 2h row heights, the phone search answer over the chips, the ResizeObserver error at large text, the My activity label breaking at 320px and 200% text) were not met by T18: the run raised no page error note in either engine, the walk does not search a place and it never sets large text.

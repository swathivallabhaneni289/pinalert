## What was built

- **Sheet (D-16).** `index.html.tmpl`: the list pane is `section#report-sheet` (`role="region"`, `aria-label="Reports near you"`, `data-snap`), with a sticky `div.sheet__handle-row` holding `button#sheet-handle.sheet__handle` (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="report-sheet"`, name "Show more of the list" or "Show the map again" at full) and `span.sheet__grabber`. No `aria-modal`, `inert` or trap. The switch button and `data-view` are gone. `sheet.js` loads before `map.js` and `feed.js`.
- **Positions.** `snapTops(H)` gives full 0, half round(0.45 H), short max(0, H - 208), half clamped to short so the order always holds. `nearestSnap` picks the closest top, and a move of more than 32px that lands on the start position is a one step flick. `gestureOwner` hands a finger up at half or short to the sheet (it grows to full first), a finger down at the top of the list to the sheet (full shrinks to half), everything else to the list.
- **Gestures.** Pointer drag on the handle row and the list head (document level listeners, `touch-action: none` on those two only, a 6px tap threshold, the click after a drag swallowed for 60ms, Show menu items never start a drag). Touch on the list body: `touchstart` records, a non passive `touchmove` asks `gestureOwner` on the first 3px and calls `preventDefault` only when the sheet owns the gesture. Handle: click toggles half and full, ArrowUp and ArrowDown use `stepSnap`. Focus by keyboard on a row a short sheet clips grows it to half (the handle and the Show button leave it). Every load ends at half.
- **Frame and CSS (phone.css, section `/* 03.4 nearby sheet */`).** The shell is `position: relative; display: block` (height rule with the bar token kept), the map pane fills it, the sheet is absolute with `top: var(--sheet-top, 45%)`, 16px top radius, `--shadow-sheet`, `overflow-y: auto`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`, z-index 1020. `#map` height is `calc(var(--sheet-top, 45%) + 16px)`; sheet top and map height ease 0.3s `cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1)`, none while dragging (`data-dragging` on the shell) and none under reduced motion (CSS media block and the script). The page frame does not scroll as a document (`overflow: hidden`, `overscroll-behavior: none`). The handle row and the list head are sticky (44px and under it). The alert banner slot is ordered after the head. The attribution stays 16px above the sheet edge.
- **Map.** `PinalertMap.resizeToSheet(sheetTop)`: `invalidateSize({ animate: false, pan: false })`, then `panInside` the chosen pin with the tools row as top padding. Skipped at full (no map shows) and while the phone detail is open (`centerPinInStrip` already placed the pin). `flyTo` now returns whether a marker was found.
- **Choosing (D-17, D-04).** Row tap: select, soft fill, `flyTo` opens the report as built, then `PinalertSheet.remember()` and `setSnap('short')`; `onPopupClose` calls `restore()` first, so the sheet returns to where it was. Pin tap: the row takes the fill, the sheet goes to half (not remembered), `revealRowInListPane` scrolls only the sheet's own element (`sheet.scrollTo`, smooth unless reduced motion, nearest: a row already whole under the head stays) with an offset for the sticky head. The chosen state stays until another is chosen.
- **Chosen row.** `.report-row--selected` is `--color-surface` with no outline; heads in feed.css outrank the white provisional and hidden cards; muted lines switch to `--color-text` while chosen (laptop hidden trust line, phone provisional and hidden card meta, distance, trust line, reporter words). The good tone is kept (4.58 to 1 light, 9.25 to 1 dark on `--color-surface`, measured). Laptop row padding is `12px 16px` (phone card unchanged).
- **Chosen pin.** `map-pin--chosen` on the badge, set in `buildBadgeElement` from the shared selection (so the 30 second `setIcon` keeps it) and moved by `applyChosenPin` on select; the raise is `marker.setZIndexOffset(1000)`. Laptop badge `scale(1.35)` with a 3px white spread edge and `--shadow-pin-chosen`; phone dot `translate(-50%, -50%) scale(1.35)` with a white border. 0.2s, none under reduced motion (inside main.css's one existing reduced motion block). Two tokens added once in main.css: `--shadow-pin-chosen`, `--color-pin-edge`. The 3px `--color-text` open outline and the brief `map-pin--highlight` ring (and `PinalertMap.highlight`) are removed.
- **Disputed pin.** `icon-badge--disputed` on the badge when `report.view === 'disputed'` (the row's own predicate). `#map .icon-badge--pin.icon-badge--disputed`: `--color-age-stale` fill with a `--color-text` glyph on the laptop badge (trust.css), a plain grey dot on the phone (phone.css), no opacity. The shared `.icon-badge.vis-hidden` head is untouched (the card's glyph and the Activity page use it). Contrast of the pair: 10.58 to 1 light, 7.49 to 1 dark (held to the 3.0 floor in `TestBadgeGlyphContrastAcrossAgeStagesAndThemes`).

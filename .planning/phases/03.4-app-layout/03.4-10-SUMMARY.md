# Phase 3.4 Plan 10: The first-time flow Summary

**/welcome is a four step page of its own (welcome, the shared Disclaimer and Policy with Agree and Disagree, location, optional name and home area) and the onboarding gate is switched on, so every account sees it once; consent is recorded only on Agree, the profile only on Continue, and the device position never leaves the browser.**

## What was built

- **Script commit** (`4d7c7e1`): `position.js` (`PinalertPosition.read` and `save` over `pinalert.lowLastPosition`, range and finite checks, guarded storage), `welcome.js` (`PinalertWelcome.goTo`, `agree`, `disagree`, `allowLocation`, `finish`), `map-tools.js` (the one shot focus when the session flag `pinalert.focusSearch` is set), and the node tests (`position_test.mjs` 6 tests, `welcome_flow_test.mjs` 24 tests including the three named cases). `welcome.js` has one `makeSearch` factory used twice: the step 3 forward search `GET /api/geocode?q=` and the step 4 place search `GET /api/geocode/place?q=`; a result is the only way a place can become a home area, and typing after a pick forgets the pick.

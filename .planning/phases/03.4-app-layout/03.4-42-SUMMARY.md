## Deviations from Plan

1. **16 declarations, not 23.** The grep found 16 underline declarations. The browser sweep found one more, the default underline on `a.btn`. Nothing else is underlined at rest.
2. **The strip wrap band is fixed (02329a1).**
   - **What went wrong:** the first version widened "Read the disclaimer" by 18px. The strip text wrapped below 1262px instead of 1244px, so between those widths the strip and everything under it sat 8px lower.
   - **The fix:**
     - The link has 4px side padding.
     - An end margin of minus 10px uses 10px of the strip's 16px right padding, leaving 6px to the window edge.
     - The box is centred 2px from the strip top, with the words kept in place.
   - **Measured:** both builds reach one line at 1244px, and the text widths are identical.
   - **Picture:** /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-swathivallabhaneni-code-pinalert/856a45ac-f4b6-4779-8968-4e1c629e2967/scratchpad/w42/sim/pw-strip-light-1280.png shows the outlined box centred in the strip.
3. **A new colour token, and a second one for the red error.** The plan says "the link colour", but no link colour existed. A hue would break D-10, so `--color-link` is a neutral grey, and `--color-link-in-error` covers the one red sentence. Both are declared in all four theme blocks of main.css.
4. **Hover-only underlines became fills.** The plan's "at rest" wording allowed them (rows 10, 15 and 16). They were changed anyway, because the owner said "no underlined text anywhere" and iOS keeps `:hover` after a tap.
5. **Remove home area keeps its own width.** It is not full width like the filled button above it, because the plan allows only the link's own size to change.
6. **Left as is, and a question for the owner:**
   - The laptop top bar marks the current place with a short bottom bar under Nearby or My activity (app-bar.css, UI-SPEC 1c).
   - The phone bottom bar marks it with a 4px bar above the item.
   - Both are border or pseudo element bars, not text underlines. Both are owner decided and allowed in layout_rules_contract_test.go.
7. **Leaflet's attribution links** (Leaflet, OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap) are not underlined at rest (Leaflet's own CSS). They underline on hover, which Leaflet's stylesheet draws. Not changed.

# Phase 3.4 Plan 23: The wheel form of the sheet scroll rule Summary

**sheet.js now has a non passive wheel listener that grows the sheet to Full on a wheel turned down and shrinks it to Half on a wheel turned up at the top of a Full list; T18.6d2 goes from 12 FAIL to 12 PASS and the whole T18 run is 382 PASS and 0 FAIL in each engine.**

The tests and the code are in one commit (the tests were written first and then the code; they were not committed separately).

## The change

`web/static/js/sheet.js`:

- `WHEEL_JITTER_PX = 4` (the mockup's threshold) and `wheelLockedUntil`.
- `onWheel(evt)`: returns unless the phone query matches (`active`), inside the lock, or for an absolute deltaY under 4. It asks `gestureOwner(snap, sheetEl.scrollTop, -evt.deltaY)`; when the sheet owns the turn it grows to Full (wheel down, not Full) or shrinks to Half (wheel up, Full, list at top), calls `preventDefault` (if cancelable), sets the lock to `now() + SETTLE_MS` and calls `setSnap`. Any other turn is left to the list with no `preventDefault`.
- `sheetEl.addEventListener('wheel', onWheel, { passive: false })` in `init`, next to the touch listeners.

Nothing else changed: touch, pointer drag, handle, keyboard and the laptop path are as before. On the laptop `active` is false, so the listener returns before touching the event.

## Tests

- `web/sheet_glue_test.mjs` (7 new cases, all through the real sheet.js in the vm sandbox): wheel down at Half and at Short grows to Full and prevents default; at Full a wheel down is left to the list; at Full with scrollTop 0 a wheel up of -40 shrinks to Half and prevents default, with scrollTop 30 it does nothing; at Half and Short a wheel up does nothing; deltaY of 2 and -2 do nothing; a second event 100ms after a wheel snap is ignored and one 400ms after works; on a laptop nothing happens.
- `web/sheet_contract_test.go` (in `TestSheetNoPageScroll`): pins the `{ passive: false }` wheel listener, the `gestureOwner(snap, sheetEl.scrollTop, -evt.deltaY)` call, the order inside `onWheel` (active check, gestureOwner, preventDefault, setSnap) and that `gestureOwner(` appears exactly three times in code (its definition and the touch and wheel callers), so there is no second copy of the rule.

Final gates: `go build ./...`, `go vet ./...`, `go test ./web -count=1` pass; `node --test web/*.mjs` is 337 pass, 0 fail.

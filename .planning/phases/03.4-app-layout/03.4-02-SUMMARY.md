## The dispute majority choice (for the owner)

Built as the plan's default, as one named rule in `ConfirmedByPeople` plus `disputeMajority` in `internal/service/confirmed.go`, with a truth table row in `TestConfirmedByPeople`:

- A confirmed Critical report (confirmed by 2 or more distinct spots) is in Show: Confirmed.
- It is not in Confirmed when disputes are a majority at the resolver's own threshold (2 or more dispute spots and more than confirm spots). It stays visible in All because the critical bypass keeps it Live.
- An unconfirmed Critical report (0 or 1 confirm spots) is in All at once and is not in Confirmed.

Question to put to the owner in plain words: should a Critical report that people have confirmed appear under Confirmed? If the answer is no, `ConfirmedByPeople` takes the report's `ReportMeta` and returns false when `meta.Serious()`, and the Critical rows of the truth table flip (a small edit, not a one line one); if yes, nothing changes.

## What was built

- **ended-nearby.js.** An IIFE that returns null when `#ended-nearby` is absent. `load()` reads `PinalertPosition.read()`; with none it sends nothing and shows the location state; with one it sends a single read (`/api/reports?lat&lon&radius_km&view=past[&report=ID]`, 10 second limit through `Pinalert.requestTimeout`). The radius comes from `data-default-radius-km`, fallback 10. A failed or timed out read shows the error state; Try again sends exactly one new read, and an older answer that lands late changes nothing.
- **Groups.** The answer is split by `history.state` and drawn in the server's order (newest first) with the server's `cut` flags. A group with no rows is not drawn unless its partial flag is set. The hub count (and the laptop title count) equals the rows shown; zero rows without a partial flag shows the empty state.
- **Rows (ended-cards.js).** `createEndedRow` and `updateEndedRow` produce the My activity row: disc (severity tint, grey when expired), Report (text or "Rescue needed"; category or "Approximate location", plus the Written line), Status chip (Resolved or Expired), What happened (the server's closed_text or outcome_label, then the shared trust line counters; the confirm counter is left to the label for a corroborated outcome), When ("Resolved 2 days ago", "Faded 2 days ago, after 6 hrs") and Distance (the server's `distance_km`; "about N km" for a finished Rescue report). The Past helpers `cutLineText`, `groupLineText` and `PARTIAL_LINE_TEXT` are unchanged.
- **Distances.** `fillDistances(position)` writes "{d} km" (one decimal under 10 km, whole from 10 km, "under 100 m" under a tenth of a kilometre) into every cell the server drew with coordinates, from a display only great circle helper; Unknown with no position or no usable coordinates.
- **Link landing.** `?part=ended&report=ID` (plain digits only) adds `report=ID` to the read, chooses the row (`activity-card--chosen`, the soft grey fill), and scrolls the nearest scrolling ancestor by its `scrollTop` (the phone's content region), never the window; on a laptop where the page itself scrolls the row takes focus instead. A focus report that is not in the groups is drawn at the top of its group. `too_old` and `unavailable` are told once with the Phase 3.1 toast words.
- **Page.** profile.html.tmpl: hidden count elements for the hub row and the title, a column head row (hidden until rows exist), ids on the group lists and cut lines, `profile-report-list` on the group lists so the row and card grids apply, the retry button id, `data-default-radius-km`, and the three scripts after app.js. Go: `DefaultRadiusKm` on `AuthConfig` and `profileViewModel`, set by the router from `deps.Page.DefaultRadiusKm`.
- **activity.css.** Group label and cut line (flush on a phone, box padding in the 900px block, hairline between groups on a laptop), What happened, the trust line with a middle dot, the chosen row fill and focus ring.

## Known gaps

- **Take back inside Ended nearby is not built.** The UI-SPEC says rows are read only "apart from the take back for the person who marked one resolved". The plan's tasks and behaviour list do not include it, the old Past card did not carry it (it was reached by opening the report), and nothing opens from a row now. Reopen and the take back still work in Posted by you for the person's own posts. If the owner wants the take back on someone else's resolved report from Ended nearby, it is a follow up that needs the vote controls and the location step on this page.
- **Laptop link landing.** On a laptop the page itself scrolls, and the plan forbids scrolling the window, so the chosen row is brought into view by giving it focus (a native scroll, no scroll call). On a phone the content region is scrolled by `scrollTop`. Neither was checked in a browser.
- **No live refresh.** Ended nearby is read once per page load and on Try again (no poll).

# Phase 3.4 Plan 45: The whole laptop screen, a filled Report button and a calm Flag row

**On a laptop the Low data view now uses the whole width: a sticky 360px left column (Back, Reload, the intro with Report an emergency, the position line, the queue strip and the stale line) and a grid of equal height report cards filling the rest. Report an emergency is the app's filled button with the plus glyph, 52px tall and full width on both sizes. Flag is no longer a third box wrapping under Confirm: it ends every card as a calm full width row with a hairline and the alert glyph.**

## What was built

**Template (grouping only).**
- Two wrappers: `.low-side` (the top row and `.low-page__head`) and `.low-reports` (the Active reports heading, the list, the skeleton, the empty state and the error state).
- The heading left `.low-page__head` so it can head the reports area; its exact markup `<h2 id="low-list-heading">Active reports</h2>` is unchanged.
- No id, class, text, link or button was added or removed, and low.js is untouched.

**Laptop, 900px and wider (inside one min-width 900px block).**
- `.low-page` is a grid with columns `360px minmax(0, 1fr)` and a 32px gap.
- Padding is 32px on the left. On the right it is `calc(44px + 24px + 16px)` = 84px, so cards end 16px left of the light and dark button.
- `.low-side` is `position: sticky; top: 0; max-height: 100vh; overflow-y: auto`. It has 4px of inline padding and a matching negative margin, so focus rings at its edge are not clipped (the Back ring starts at x 28, exactly the column edge).
- The Active reports heading is 52px, level with the Back and Reload row. The first cards start at y 68, level with the intro block.
- `#low-list` (and the skeleton) is `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(100%, 22.5rem), 1fr))`, which gives 2 columns at 1280 and 1440 and 3 at 1920. Cards in a row are the same height, and their action rows sit at the bottom.

**Phone, under 900px.** Still plan 44's one column. Only the button and the Flag row changed: every x position is unchanged, and blocks below the intro moved down 8px (the button grew from 44 to 52).

**Report an emergency, both sizes.**
- `.btn--primary` fill: text colour fill, page colour label, semibold.
- 8px corners, at least 52px tall, `flex: 1 1 100%` (full width of its block).
- The plus glyph is a 20px `::before` mask of `/static/icons/plus.svg`, the same mask pattern `.app-icon--plus` uses.
- Same words, same id `low-report`, same behaviour.

**Flag, both sizes.**
- Rule `.report-row--low .flag-control:not([hidden])`, with the phone report view's Flag row declarations copied.
- `display: flex`, full width, 48px tall, 24px above, no padding, no box, no radius, no underline, semibold text colour, left aligned.
- A 1px hairline above in `--color-text-muted`, not `--color-border`, which is under 3 to 1.
- The 20px circle-alert glyph as a `::before` mask.
- A `--color-border` fill on hover, except when Flagged.
- The global 2px focus ring.
- Forced colours: both glyphs opt out and paint ButtonText.

**Confirm and Dispute.** Unchanged: plan 42's outlined look until pressed, equal width, side by side (trust.css two column grid).

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Judgment] Two small local icon requests.** The plan asks for "no new requests" and also for the plus glyph and the circle-alert glyph through the mask pattern.
   - The Low page asset policy test (TestLowDataAssetPolicy in low_contract_test.go) requires every url() in its stylesheets to be a shipped `/static/icons/*.svg`. That rules out an inline data: glyph without weakening a test.
   - So /low now fetches plus.svg (376 bytes) and circle-alert.svg (about 400 bytes) from its own origin, once, and then from cache.
   - Nothing external was added. If the owner wants zero extra fetches, the alternative is a text "+" like plan 44's arrows, but that is not the Post a report glyph.
2. **[Fact] The reader's own report shows no Flag.** flag.js hides Flag on the viewer's own report, so that card has no Confirm, Dispute or Flag at all, not "Flag alone". It was not changed (JS is out of scope, and hiding Flag there is intended behaviour, P4-A6).
3. **[Fact] Reopen cannot appear in the Low view's Active list.** Reopen shows only for the poster's own report in the resolved view, and the Active list never holds a resolved report. The layout was checked with a DOM probe that showed the hidden Reopen button on a card: it sits under Confirm and Dispute and Flag stays last. Undo (the reader's waiting resolve mark) does appear and was checked live.
4. **[Judgment] 84px right inset rather than 32px.** The person button (fixed at the top right) and the light and dark button (fixed at the bottom right) cover x 1372 to 1424 at 1440 wide. A 32px inset would let every card scroll under them. The 32px is kept on the left.
5. **[Judgment] 360px left column (the top of the plan's 320 to 360 range).** At 340px the intro note wrapped and left "default." alone on a line.
6. **[Judgment] Action rows.** In each grid row the Flag rows line up exactly. The Confirm and Dispute rows line up too, except when one card also carries the Undo block. Undo lives inside the vote controls (votes.js), so that card's Confirm sits higher by the Undo block's height.
7. **[Rule 1] 200 percent text.** The first build used a fixed 360px card minimum, and at 200 percent text on a 1280 laptop it broke Confirm and Dispute mid word ("Confir m"). The minimum is now `min(100%, 22.5rem)`, which is one column at that size.

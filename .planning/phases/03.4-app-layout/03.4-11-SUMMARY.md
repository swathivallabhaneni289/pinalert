## What was built

- **Show menu.** `show-menu.js`: aria-expanded written only in open and close, focus to the checked item on open, ArrowUp/Down/Home/End, Enter chooses (browser click stopped so it is made once), Space and touch use the item click, Escape returns focus to the button, outside click closes. The boot alias (`/?view=disputed`, older `show_disputed=true`) is read once in `readAlias` and writes no history.
- **List.** `feed.js` draws `Pinalert.visibleReports()`; the count line is "{N} reports, {R} km" ("1 report"), N being the cards shown; empty states chosen in the order filter, disputed, confirmed, history, general; status "3 reports, Show Confirmed. - **Disputed cues.** Row class `report-row--disputed` (category glyph shown at 20px, grey dot `--color-age-stale`), the Disputed state word from the visibility tag, the bold critical line "Disputed: may not be true" above the counters, a visually hidden lead "Disputed, may not be true." - **Moved code.** `ended-cards.js` (`window.PinalertEnded`) holds createPastCard, buildPastMeta, updatePastCard, cutLineText, groupLineText, PARTIAL_LINE_TEXT, placeInList and the two Rescue words, behaviour unchanged.

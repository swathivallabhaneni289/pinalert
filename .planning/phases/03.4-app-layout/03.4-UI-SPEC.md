# Phase 3.4: UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 3.4 (App layout): the main navigation, Nearby with the phone sheet, My activity, Official alerts, Post a report, Settings, the first-time flow, and how the disclaimer is shown. Built from the owner's approved mockup, sketch 007 (approved 2026-10-16), plus `03.4-CONTEXT.md`. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, to be verified by gsd-ui-checker.

## Source of Truth and Scope

**Visual source of truth:** `.planning/sketches/007-app-layout/index.html`. Every value below was read from its final cascade (the file stacks rounds v4 to v18 and keeps dead rules from rejected rounds: the tab strips, the card grids, the phone call box, the amber strip, the filled Report block). Computed styles were probed in a headless browser at 390 px and 1280 px, light and dark, and mapped to the app's tokens. Where the mockup draws a value the app has no token for, this file says how it is snapped and lists it under "Departures from the mockup and the template".

**Decisions treated as the contract:** D-01 to D-32 of `03.4-CONTEXT.md`, including its "Where the build departs from the mockup" section. Nothing was put to the owner while writing this file. Anything not decided anywhere is in the last two sections: "Open items for the planner and the owner" (storage and cross phase questions, inside Hand Off) and "For the owner's viewing session" (placement, look and copy).

**Token source of truth:** `web/static/css/main.css`. The mockup's `--bg`, `--surface`, `--border`, `--text`, `--muted`, `--low`, `--medium`, `--critical`, `--low-bg`, `--medium-bg`, `--critical-bg`, `--stale` are the app's `--color-bg`, `--color-surface`, `--color-border`, `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-severity-low`, `--color-severity-medium`, `--color-severity-critical`, `--color-severity-*-bg` and `--color-age-stale`, with identical light and dark values. The mockup's `--water`, `--land`, `--road`, `--park` and the SVG map are mockup scenery only: the real map is Leaflet with the MapLibre basemap.

**Baselines this contract amends** (identifiers quoted, not copied): `03.1-UI-SPEC.md` (Surfaces 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and departures R1 to R10), `04-UI-SPEC.md` (the disclaimer bar, P4-A1 to P4-A4, P4-A11, P4-A25, the notice card, the account menu), sketch 005 (phone layout) and quick tasks 261013-lrw and 261013-wm5. Where this file is silent, those stay in force. The report popup, the phone detail sheet and the trust block stay exactly as built (`.claude/skills/sketch-findings-pinalert`, variant d4).

**Sample data rule:** every name, number, district and sentence in the mockup is sample data (2024 Vijayawada floods). Real values come from the server, the page config and the numbers setting (`Chrome.Numbers`). The browser computes no trust figure, no outcome and no count of history.

**Stack:** Go `html/template`, vanilla JS, hand written CSS, Leaflet 1.9.4 with the MapLibre basemap. No framework, no build step, no new library. Tool: none. The shadcn gate does not apply (no `components.json`, no `package.json`).

**What this phase builds:** places, layout, navigation and empty states. It builds no feature that belongs to a later phase (see "What renders in this phase and what waits").

**Not designed here:** the bell's update wording and unread logic (3.2), phone notification switches and how a What is happening pick is used in a phone alert (3.3), official alert content and the banner data (5), profiles, stars, follows (9), the report page (8), the Low data view and the moderator screen layouts (kept as built), the report popup and detail sheet (kept as built), pull to refresh, tap the current tab to jump to the top, and text that grows with the phone's text size (deferred by the owner, ask before adding).

## Rules that bind every screen

The owner's rules D-01 to D-09, restated as testable lines. Every surface below was drawn against them.

| # | Rule | How it is checked |
|---|------|-------------------|
| R1 | Phone and laptop are the same product. Every feature, option, label and behaviour exists on both; only the arrangement differs. Never hide or add text, hints or options on one size only. | A feature list per surface is the same on both sizes; the only size specific elements are the ones named "phone only" or "laptop only" in this file, each with the reason (a laptop cannot dial, the bottom bar needs a phone, the person menu sits in the laptop top bar). |
| R2 | Nothing is repeated. Each control or link lives in one place. The first-time flow has no X because it is not dismissed, it is completed. |
| R3 | Everything visible. Nothing a user needs sits off the edge or under something floating. Labels never break onto two lines at 320, 360 and 390 px. | Browser check at 320, 360, 390 and 1280. Bottom bar labels are `white-space: nowrap`. The sheet resizes the map so no pin sits under it. |
| R4 | A chosen option looks chosen, never like an error. No black outline or black box around a chosen item. No selection ring on a pin or card. |
| R5 | No hint text under simple options (the owner rejected "Good to know", "Affects many", "Danger to life" under Low, Medium, Critical). | The severity boxes and the category tiles hold their label only. |
| R6 | Laptop pages use the whole screen, never a centred column. | Laptop pages are full width with a left heading column and a right list (My activity, Official alerts, Settings). | R7 | No faded (opacity) text. A disputed or ended item keeps full contrast and is marked by a grey icon and a status tag. | No `opacity` below 1 on any text, card or row. Muted text only on `--color-bg` (see Color). |
| R8 | No light or dark button on the phone. The phone follows the device setting. The laptop keeps its button. | Already built (`phone.css`, `chrome.css`, `theme.js`); this phase keeps it and adds the laptop button to the top bar. |
| R9 | When the owner reports an error, fix only that error. | Applies to the build's review rounds, not to this file. |

House rules (`site-design-rules`), all binding: no purple gradients, no pill shaped buttons (modest radius rectangles only; round icon discs and the pin circles are not buttons), no fake metrics, no hero text, no emoji (SVG icons only), no em or en dash in any user visible string or in this file's prose, no scroll triggered animation, transitions short and restrained. `TestUserVisibleCopyUsesPlainPunctuation` and `TestNoPillShapedControls` guard these and cover every new template automatically.

## Breakpoint, page frame and addresses

**Breakpoint:** the app's own, `max-width: 899.98px` is the phone layout and `min-width: 900px` is the laptop layout. Phone rules live only in `phone.css` inside its one `@media (max-width: 899.98px)` block (the file's own rule and `TestPhoneRulesLiveInTheNarrowBlock`). The mockup's 899 px is the same break. Layouts are drawn at 320, 360 and 390 px first, then 900 px and up (checked at 1280 and 1440).

**Page frame (the three places and Settings):** a full height column that does not scroll as a document. `body` is `display: flex; flex-direction: column; height: 100dvh` (legacy `100vh` first). Top bar, content, bottom bar. Only the content region scrolls (`overflow-y: auto; min-height: 0`), except Nearby, where nothing scrolls except the list inside the sheet or the list pane. `html { scroll-padding-top }` is no longer needed on these pages.

**Addresses (defaults the planner may rename, but each place must be a real, linkable server rendered page that works without script):**

| Place | Address | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Nearby | `/` | unchanged. `/?report=ID` links keep working (03.1 report links). |
| Official alerts | `/alerts` | new |
| My activity | `/profile` | kept so bookmarks, the Phase 4 Activity links and the account `data-account-id` keep working. Part screens and anchors: `/profile?part=updates\|posted\|confirmed\|disputed\|ended`. The old `?tab=` values are read only aliases: `posts`->`posted`, `votes`->`confirmed`, `disputes`->`disputed`, `resolved`, `not-resolved` and `all`->the list. |
| Settings | `/settings` | new, inside the verified account group |
| Disclaimer, Policy | `/disclaimer`, `/policy` | kept (public). They are the same reader content (one source), opened scrolled to the right part. |
| Post a report | `/?post=1` from any place other than Nearby; in place on Nearby | A link, so it works without script. It opens the window over Nearby (so the laptop strip is on screen, see Surface 8). |

**History:** moving between the main places and into a My activity part screen is real navigation (links, one history step each), because these are different screens, unlike 03.1's tab strip. The phone back arrow on a part screen is a link to `/profile`. The back arrow on Settings goes back to the place the person came from (`history.back()` when the previous entry is an app page, else `/`). The Show menu, the chips, the place search and the sheet position use `replaceState` or no history, as 03.1 decided.

**Nearby is one page on both sizes.** The sheet, the list pane and the map are the same elements; only CSS arranges them.

**Layer order (z-index, low to high):** Leaflet panes and the map tools layer 1000; bottom bar 1010; phone list sheet 1020; place search results 1030; top bars 1050; person menu, Show menu and bell list 1060; phone detail sheet 1080 (as built, covers the bottom bar); Post a report window, first-time flow and reader 1100; toast 1200. ## Interaction and Accessibility

- **Focus order (laptop Nearby):** strip link, wordmark, nav links, Post a report, Search a place field and its submit, bell, light and dark, person menu, then the list head (Show), place and filter lines, cards, the map chips, map pins. **Phone:** search field and submit, bell, gear, map chips, pins, sheet handle, Show, cards, bottom bar.
- **Escape order:** open menu or list closes first; then a results list; then the Post a report window; the reader; the first-time flow ignores it.
- **Names:** every icon button has a name (Settings, Updates, Back, Close, Account menu, Search a place); the Show button's name is its visible text; the bottom bar items are links with visible text; the sheet handle changes name and `aria-expanded` as specified; tiles and severity boxes are radios named by their words; the Call 112 box is a link named "Call 112. Someone in danger right now? Call 112 first, then post here."
- **State is never colour alone:** current nav item has weight and a bar or underline; a chosen tile has weight and fill; a chosen severity box has its tint and the word; a disputed card has the word "Disputed", a grey disc and the line; a pin that is grown is also in the list as a chosen card.
- **Live regions:** `#feed-status` for list changes; `#notice-live` for Phase 4 notices; the Disagree message is `role="status"`; `#form-error` keeps `role="alert"`.
- **Reduced motion:** the sheet, the pin scale, scroll into view and any popover reveal are instant under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. No scroll triggered animation exists.
- **Text size:** the top bars, the head row and the cards wrap or grow; nothing is cut to a fixed height except one line hub summaries, which use an ellipsis and keep the full text in the accessible name. No horizontal page scroll at 320 px and 200 percent text.
- **Forced colours:** see Color.
- **Keyboard:** every control reachable; the sheet by the handle and arrow keys; the Show menu by arrows; the person menu and bell list by `account-menu.js`'s pattern; no pointer only gesture is the only route (drag has the handle keys and the tap toggle).

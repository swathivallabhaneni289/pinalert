# Phase 3.4 Plan 15: Search a place in the Nearby top bar Summary

**Search a place is now a single always visible field in the Nearby top bar on both sizes, the map tools layer holds the chips alone with a soft chosen look, and the phone's notice rows live inside the sheet.**

## What was built

**Task 1, commit 6b69142: the search in the top bar.**
- `place_search.html.tmpl` holds `form#map-search.place-search[role=search]` with the 16px `input#map-search-input[type=search]` (placeholder and name "Search a place", `enterkeyhint="search"`, `maxlength=200`), the magnifier as `button#map-search-submit[type=submit]` named "Search a place" (44 by 44), `p#map-search-status` and `div#map-search-results`. `app_bar.html.tmpl` includes it only under `{{if eq .Nav.Current "nearby"}}`, after Post a report and before the bell; the light and dark button and the person button follow, as in the laptop order. On the phone the field takes the place of the title and the spacer, before the bell and the gear.
- `map-tools.js`: the open and close buttons, `setOpen`, the phone query and the `data-search-open` state are gone. `focusSearch()` is the box on both sizes, a pick still flies the map, refetches, shows the place line, closes the results and returns focus to the field, Escape closes the results only, and the one shot focus flag of plan 10 is kept. The reader lane budget and "a failed search never blocks" are untouched.
- CSS: the field geometry is in `app-bar.css` (laptop: one 44px bordered box, radius 8px, `--color-bg`, 260px that shrinks) and `phone.css` (surface fill, no border, placeholder and magnifier in `--color-text`). The results panel rules stay in `feed.css` (laptop 320px, `--shadow-menu`). `--map-tools-height` is one row on both sizes in `main.css` and `phone.css`; `trust.css` is not edited. The moved search's rules inside the tools layer are removed; the result, note and icon looks are kept.

**Task 2, commit d24ac0f: chips and notices.**
- A chosen chip: `--color-surface` fill, `--color-text`, weight 600, a 16px `check.svg` mask in a `::before`, a transparent border so the chip keeps its size; an unchosen chip keeps `--color-bg` and its 1px border; forced colours keep the 2px CanvasText border.
- `#notice-card` moved from the top of the body into the list pane right after the list head. Same rows, texts, ids, buttons and live region. A laptop keeps the fixed bottom left place (chrome.css untouched). On a phone `.pane--list > .notice-card` is static, `order: -1`, full width, so nothing floats over the list.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] A narrow laptop trims the bar gaps.**
- **Found during:** Task 1, while adding the 260px field.
- **Issue:** With the wordmark gap (24px), the link padding (12px each side), Post a report (160px) and the three right buttons, the 260px field would not fit between 900px and about 1085px; without a rule the person button would be pushed off the screen (D-03).
- **Fix:** The field is `flex: 0 1 260px; min-width: 0` so it shrinks first, and `@media (min-width: 900px) and (max-width: 1099.98px)` in app-bar.css trims the wordmark right margin to 8px and the link padding to 8px each side. At 900px the field still has about 155px. Pinned in `TestSearchAlwaysVisible`.
- **Files modified:** web/static/css/app-bar.css (a file already on the plan list).
- **Commit:** 6b69142.

**2. [Plan adjustment] The notice card position.** The plan says right after the list head; UI-SPEC 2f lists the notice rows last. `feed_stale_contract_test.go` (outside this plan's files) pins the stale block as the last element before the list, so the card follows the list head as the plan says, and the phone's `order: -1` places it under the head and the banner slot. This is the plan's literal position, recorded for the spec's order.

**3. [Plan adjustment] Tests listed but not edited.** `phone_layout_contract_test.go`, `chrome_css_contract_test.go`, `queue_contract_test.go` and the chip half of `browser_findings_contract_test.go` pinned nothing this plan changed (they stay green untouched), so they carry no amendment. The chrome.css notice card rules are unchanged on purpose.

Nothing was changed outside the plan's file list except that one new file, `place_search.html.tmpl` (on the list), and this SUMMARY. No file was retired.

## Z-index note for plan 13's interim 1030

Plan 13 raised the phone results to 1030 so they sat above the sheet (1020). That rule is gone with the move. The results and the status line are now inside `.top-chrome` (sticky, z-index 1050), so their local `z-index: 1030` is only an order inside the chrome. Effective level: above the sheet (1020), above the bottom bar (1010), under the detail sheet (1080). `TestMapToolsCSS` pins the 1030 and the chrome's 1050 together so one cannot change without the other. The `#map-search-results` entry stays in the phone detail sheet hide rule (pinned by `TestMapToolsHideUnderSheet`).

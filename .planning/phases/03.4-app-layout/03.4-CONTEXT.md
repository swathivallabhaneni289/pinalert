<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Rebuild where things live and how every screen is laid out, on the phone and the laptop, from the approved mockup:
the main navigation, the Nearby screen (with the phone list as a sheet over the map), My activity, Official alerts as
a main place, the Post a report window, the Settings page, the first-time flow, and how the disclaimer is shown.
Promotes backlog 999.10 (app-wide design pass, owner priority).

It runs after Phase 3.1 and Phase 4 are signed off, because it reworks parts of both, and before Phase 3.2, which
builds its bell, Updates list and Settings sections into this layout. The phase number is out of order on purpose.

It builds the places, the layout, the navigation and the empty states. It does not build features that belong to
later phases (see "What later phases fill in").
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Rules the owner set for every screen (apply them to every plan)
- **D-01 [OWNER DECIDED]: Phone and laptop are the same product.** Every feature, option, label and behaviour is the
  same on both; only the arrangement differs because the screen is smaller. Never hide or add text, hints or options on
  one size only. The phone matters most: in an emergency people only have their phone, and reporting from it must be the
  quickest path.
- **D-02 [OWNER DECIDED]: Nothing is repeated.** Each control or link lives in one place. One way to close each window:
  the X at the top (no Cancel, no Done beside it). Before adding a control, check it does not already exist elsewhere.
- **D-03 [OWNER DECIDED]: Everything visible.** Nothing a user needs sits off the edge of the screen or is covered by
  something floating over it. Labels never break onto two lines at 320, 360 or 390 px.
- **D-04 [OWNER DECIDED]: A chosen option looks chosen, never like an error.** No black outline or black box around a
  chosen item. Chosen severity uses a soft tint in its own colour; a chosen card uses a soft grey fill.
- **D-05 [OWNER DECIDED]: No hint text under simple options** (the owner rejected "Good to know", "Affects many",
  "Danger to life" under Low, Medium, Critical).
- **D-06 [OWNER DECIDED]: Laptop pages use the whole screen, never a centred column.**
- **D-07 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, accessibility]: No faded (opacity) text.** Disputed or expired items keep full contrast
  and are marked by a grey icon and a status tag instead.
- **D-08 [OWNER DECIDED, 2026-10-15]: No light or dark button on the phone.** The phone follows the device setting. The
  laptop keeps its button. (Amends UX-01 for phones.)
- **D-09 [OWNER DECIDED]: When the owner reports an error, fix only that error.** Do not redesign the part or anything
  near it unasked.

### Main navigation (LAYOUT-01)
- **D-10:** Three main places on both sizes: **Nearby** (opens first), **Official alerts**, **My activity**.
  - Phone: a bottom bar with those three plus a fourth item, **Report** (an outlined plus in a circle with the word
    Report, drawn like the other items, in the text colour; never a filled block). The current item has a short bar
    above it and bold text.
  - Laptop: a top bar with the Pinalert name, the three places, a **Post a report** button, Search a place, the bell,
    the light and dark button and a person menu.
- **D-11:** Phone top bar: on Nearby, Search a place, the bell and the Settings gear; on every other screen, the title,
  the bell and the gear (a back arrow on Settings and on My activity's inner screens). Laptop person menu: **My profile,
  Settings, Log out** only.
- **D-12:** The names are final: Nearby, Official alerts, My activity, Report (phone) and Post a report (laptop).

### Nearby (LAYOUT-02, LAYOUT-03)
- **D-13:** No tab strip on Nearby. Above the list: "N reports, 5 km" and one **Show** menu: All (default), Confirmed,
  Disputed, and an "Only Reliable reporters" tick (rescue and critical reports always stay visible). The Reliable tick is
  Phase 3.2's NOTIF-04; it lives in this menu, nowhere else.
- **D-14:** In All, disputed reports sit at the bottom with "Disputed: may not be true", a grey icon and a Disputed tag,
  never faded. This is presentation only: the single visibility resolver and its Hidden by dispute outcome stay as they
  are; the list groups by that outcome. (See Conflicts.)
- **D-15:** The official alert banner sits at the top of the list only when a Severe or Extreme alert covers the area
  (Phase 5 content); tapping it opens Official alerts.
- **D-16: The phone list is a sheet over the map** (the Apple Maps and Google Maps pattern) with three positions:
  short (map almost whole), half (the default) and full (the list right under the search row). Scrolling the list up
  first grows the sheet to full, then the list scrolls; scrolling down at the top shrinks it back. A grey handle can be
  dragged or tapped. The map above the sheet resizes so pins are never hidden under it.
- **D-17: Tapping a report shows its pin; tapping a pin shows its report**, on both sizes. On the phone the sheet drops
  to short when a report is tapped. The chosen card gets a soft grey fill and the pin grows a little (no black ring).

### My activity (LAYOUT-04)
- **D-18:** Five parts: **Updates** (Phase 3.2 fills it), **Posted by you**, **You confirmed**, **You disputed**,
  **Ended nearby** (everyone's reports near the reader that were resolved or faded out in the last 7 days, in two groups:
  Resolved and Faded out; this is Phase 3.1's Past content under a clearer name).
- **D-19: Phone:** My activity opens on one short list in a rounded box (the pattern of Instagram's "Your activity" and
  the phone's own Settings). Each row has a soft icon (bell, plus in a circle, tick in a circle, cross in a circle,
  clock with a back arrow), the name, one line saying what is inside ("1 still happening, 1 resolved", "Latest: ...",
  "Last 7 days, near your area", or the newest update and a red "N new"), the count and a chevron. A row opens that part
  on its own screen with a back arrow and the same name; tapping My activity in the bottom bar returns to the list.
  Rejected on the way: a sideways scrolling tab strip (parts hidden off the edge), a two row word block ("a bunch of
  words"), icon tiles ("a bunch of symbols").
- **D-20: Laptop:** all five parts stacked on one page; each part has its title, count and one line on the left, and a
  full width list on the right whose rows line up in columns (Report, Status, What happened, When, Distance). The
  owner rejected tabs and side by side card grids on the laptop ("I do want the stack").
- **D-21:** Every feature of today's Activity page stays: the trust counts and panel, the state word, Reopen and take
  back on resolved reports, the reporter record, empty states. "Resolved" and "Not resolved" stop being tabs; each
  report shows an Ongoing or Resolved tag instead. Phone cards: icon and status tag on top, title, description, when,
  a thin line, then what happened and the distance.

### Official alerts (LAYOUT-01, content in Phase 5)
- **D-22:** A main place of its own. One list, "In force now" with the count and the districts, and "Last checked N
  minutes ago" at the top. Laptop rows line up in columns (Level, Alert, Issued by, Issued, Until); phone cards end
  with Issued and Until on one line.

### Post a report (LAYOUT-05)
- **D-23:** Phone: a full screen sheet from the Report item. Laptop: a wide window from the Post a report button, map on
  the left (Search an address or a landmark, the pin to drag, "Near X" under it), form on the right. Close with the X
  only; the bottom has Post report.
- **D-24:** The form, in order: **What is it?** (eight tiles: Flood, Rescue needed, Shelter open, Power out, Road
  blocked, Fire, Storm, Other), **How serious?** (Low, Medium, Critical boxes with their colour edge; the chosen one
  filled with its own soft colour), then, only for Critical or Rescue, a **"Someone in danger right now? Call 112
  first, then post here."** box with a Call 112 button and the **What is happening?** picks (Water rising, People
  trapped, Fire, Building collapsed, Landslide, Medical emergency, Other; Phase 3.3 stores and uses them), then
  **Describe it** (280 letters).

### Settings (LAYOUT-06)
- **D-25:** A Settings page of its own, from the gear (phone top right) or the person menu (laptop). Sections stacked;
  on the laptop each section has its title and one line on the left and its rows on the right:
  - **About you:** Name, Home area (a town or area, never a street), View my profile (phone only; the laptop has My
    profile in the person menu).
  - **Phone notifications** (Phase 3.3 fills): on or off for this device, then one switch each for rescue and critical
    reports near the home area (on), severe official alerts for the area (on; Phase 5), changes to reports I posted or
    voted on (off), a Reliable reporter posts near me (off), someone I follow posts (off), and How far from my home
    area (1, 2, 5, 10 km).
  - **People and reports:** Let others follow me, Following, Followers, Muted reports, Blocked people (Phase 9 and 3.2
    fill them).
  - **About Pinalert:** Disclaimer, Policy, Low data view (text only, no map).
  - **Account:** Log out (phone only; the laptop has it in the person menu), Log out of all devices, Delete my account.
- **D-26:** Settings shows only rows whose feature exists when it ships; later phases add their rows here, never a second
  settings place.

### First-time flow (LAYOUT-07)
- **D-27:** After the first sign in, five steps (phone full screen with a progress bar; laptop a wide window with the
  step list on the left):
  1. **Welcome to Pinalert:** "Know what is happening around you." / "See how far to trust each report. Others nearby
     confirm or dispute it. The more different places confirm a report, the more likely it is true, but a report can
     still be wrong." / "Pinalert cannot send help. It tells you a situation is happening near you. If someone is in
     danger, call 112 first." Then Continue.
  2. **Disclaimer and policy:** the full text in a scroll box with **Disagree** and **Agree** (the Apple pattern the owner
     asked for). Disagree says "You need to agree to use Pinalert. You can still look around in the Demo without
     agreeing." Only Agree moves on.
  3. **Your location:** why it is asked (to show reports around you and place your pin; your exact spot is never shown
     or stored), Allow location or Not now, I will search a place.
  4. **About you:** name and home area, both optional, the home area filled in from the location step. The filled in
     home area is shown and can be changed, and it is saved only when the person taps Continue, never silently
     (matches 3.2 D-20).
  5. **Alerts:** what will buzz the phone, the iPhone add to home screen tip, Allow alerts or Not now (Phase 3.3 makes
     it real; until then the step is left out).
- **D-28:** The owner's earlier "one page after sign up" (name, home area, alert choices) is steps 4 and 5 of this flow.

### Disclaimer and policy (LAYOUT-08)
- **D-29: Laptop:** a dark strip at the top of the **Nearby and Post a report pages only**: "**Unofficial, not a
  government service.** Pinalert cannot send help, it shows what people near you are seeing. Reports can sometimes be
  wrong. In danger, call **112**." and a "Read the disclaimer" link. No call button on the laptop (a laptop cannot
  dial). **[OWNER DECIDED 2026-10-16, narrows the mockup's "every page":]** the strip is left off My activity (which
  holds Ended nearby, the old Past tab), Settings (it already holds the Disclaimer) and Official alerts ("not a
  government service" above real government alerts would confuse). The owner: "the site is unofficial doesnt make
  sense for the past tab", then agreed to Nearby and Post a report only.
- **D-30: Phone [OWNER DECIDED, departs from ROBUST-08]:** no strip and no call box on the main screens. The owner
  tried a strip, an amber strip and an "Emergency? Call 112" box and rejected each ("doesn't look good, doesn't make
  sense", "maybe let's just keep it at the disclaimer and the policy page"). On the phone the disclaimer is read and
  agreed in step 2 of the first-time flow, is one tap away in Settings, About Pinalert, and the call 112 line appears in
  the welcome step, the Disclaimer page, and the Post a report box for Critical or Rescue. See Conflicts.
- **D-31:** Disclaimer and Policy open in one reader window (title Disclaimer or Policy; a Policy link scrolls to the
  Policy part), close with the X. The login screen keeps its Disclaimer and Policy links.

### The bell and Updates (places only; Phase 3.2 builds them)
- **D-32:** The bell sits top right (phone, beside the gear) and beside the person menu (laptop), with a red count.
  Tapping it opens a short list of the latest 10 updates, unread marked, and "See all updates", which opens My
  activity, Updates.

### Still open (ask the owner once, in plain words, before planning)
- **O-1:** Same features rule versus D-29 and D-30: the laptop strip and the line "It shows as Not confirmed yet until
  people nearby confirm it" under Post report show on the laptop but not on the phone. Should both sizes show them, or
  neither? **Strip part answered 2026-10-16:** laptop only, and only on Nearby and Post a report (see D-29). **Line part
  answered 2026-10-16 [OWNER DECIDED]:** neither size shows "It shows as Not confirmed yet until people nearby confirm
  it" under Post report; the mockup's laptop line is not built. Reason: the new report shows "Not confirmed yet" on its
  own card right after posting, so the line repeats it (nothing repeated, D-05 no hint text). O-1 is closed.
- **O-2:** The chosen category tile in Post a report keeps a dark border; should it use the soft fill like the
  severity (D-04), or stay?

### Conflicts and risks the plans must handle
- **Phase 3.1 (built, awaiting sign-off):** its tab strip Active, Disputed, Past (D-01, D-02, HIST-01) and the phone
  bottom bar Feed and Activity are replaced; its Past content moves to My activity, Ended nearby; its chips, place
  search, compact rows, take back and rescue privacy rules stay. The 3.1 contract tests that pin the strip need a dated
  amendment note naming this owner decision.
- **Phase 4 (built, gaps found):** ROBUST-08 ("every page displays a persistent disclaimer") is met on the laptop only
  after D-30. Record the owner's decision in REQUIREMENTS, amend the disclaimer bar contract tests with a note, and keep
  the risk visible: CLAUDE.md marks India's IT Rules 2021 applicability as LOW confidence and asks for a legal review
  before wide promotion. The low data view link moves into Settings, About Pinalert.
- **Quick 261013-wm5 (six tab Activity page) and 261013-lrw (phone layout):** their layouts are replaced; keep every
  feature (D-21).
- **Trust model:** D-14 must not change the resolver, the counts or the gates. Disputed reports were hidden from the
  default list; now they show at the bottom of All, clearly labelled. Check that the label, order and Show menu make it
  impossible to mistake a disputed report for a confirmed one, and that critical or rescue reports keep their own rules.
- **UX-01:** the in-app light and dark button now exists on the laptop only (D-08).
- **Sheet on the phone (D-16):** must respect reduced motion, keep the list scrollable by keyboard and screen readers
  (the handle is a real button with an expanded state), and never trap the page scroll.

### What later phases fill in
- 3.2: the bell, the Updates part, muting, the Only Reliable reporters tick, the Reliable alerts rows in Settings.
- 3.3: install, phone notification rows and the first-time Alerts step, the What is happening picks' storage and use.
- 5: Official alerts content and banner, the severe alerts row, the Demo button on the login screen.
- 9: My profile, Let others follow me, Following, Followers, Blocked people, stars.
</decisions>

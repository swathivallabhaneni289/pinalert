# Phase 3.4 Plan 30: Free movement between the first time steps Summary

**The four first time steps can be visited in any order with a Back control, a clickable step list on the laptop and the browser's own back and forward, while Finish still needs Agree.**

## Accomplishments

- Steps 2 to 4 have a Back button at the left of the top line, beside the "Step N of 4" label, using the existing arrow-left glyph and the visible word Back, 44px tall, on the phone and the laptop. Step 1 has none.
- Each name in the laptop step list (Welcome, Disclaimer and policy, Your location, About you) is a button, the whole 44px row is the target, with a visible keyboard ring in the panel's own light colour. The current one carries aria-current=step.
- Every move adds a history entry (`history.pushState`, address `?step=N`); the first load only replaces the entry. `popstate` shows the step in the entry and adds nothing. Reload honours an address step only when the account may see it, so a reload never skips the agreement.
- Finish and Skip before Agree send nothing, show step 2 and a line "Please agree to the Disclaimer and policy to continue." The server's consent rule (409 from first-run-done) and its handling are unchanged and still tested.
- Typed and picked values survive moves: step 3 search text and pick, step 4 name and home area, and the Agree state.
- Focus still moves to the step title. The Tab loop skips the side panel buttons when the panel is not drawn (phone), and Shift+Tab from a title goes back to Back or the list instead of wrapping.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Re-entering step 4 would overwrite the person's home area**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (writing the "typed values kept" test)
- **Issue:** `enterStep4` re-ran its step 3 prefill every time step 4 was shown, which with free movement would replace a home area the person had typed or picked.
- **Fix:** the prefill runs only while the home area is untouched; a stale prefill is removed if step 3's pick is gone.
- **Files modified:** web/static/js/welcome.js

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Tab trap with the new buttons**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the new list buttons are focusable but not drawn on the phone, and Shift+Tab from the title wrapped past the new Back control.
- **Fix:** `focusables()` skips the side panel when it is not drawn; the title wrap only applies when nothing comes before it.
- **Files modified:** web/static/js/welcome.js; the existing Tab test was updated to the new order.

No other deviations. welcome.go was not changed: the plan does not list it, so the address step rule lives in the script and reads the server's `data-start-step`.

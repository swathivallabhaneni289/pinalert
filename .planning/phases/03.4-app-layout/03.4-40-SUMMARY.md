# Phase 3.4 Plan 40: The phone top controls float over the map Summary

**On the phone the 60px white row at the top is gone. On Nearby the map runs to the top of the page, and the search field, the bell and the gear float over it as solid page coloured controls with the soft menu shadow. In the report view the map strip starts at the top of the page, with a round Back at the top left and the bell and the gear at the top right. The report now starts 60px higher. Checked in the iPhone 17 Pro simulator's own Safari. The laptop is unchanged.**

## What changed (phone.css, inside the max-width 899.98px block only)

- **Nearby top chrome:** `body.has-app-bar:has(> .app-shell) > .top-chrome` is fixed at the top, left and right, and lets presses pass (`pointer-events: none`).
  - Its bar is see through, with 8px top and bottom padding, 12px at each side and 8px between the controls.
  - The search, bell, gear and bell list take their own presses.
  - The search field, bell and gear get `background: var(--color-bg)` and `box-shadow: var(--shadow-menu)`. Their 8px corners are unchanged.
- **Chips row and zoom:** `.pane--map > .map-tools` and `.pane--map .leaflet-top` get `top: var(--app-topbar-height)`, so they sit where they did before.
- **Sheet at full:** `.app-shell > .pane--list[data-snap]` gets `top: max(var(--sheet-top, 45%), var(--app-topbar-height))`. At full it stops under the floating controls, as before. sheet.js is unchanged.
- **Report view:**
  - The popup top is `calc(var(--space-3xl) * 2.5)` (160px). It was the top row plus 160px.
  - Back is `left: var(--space-12)`, `border-radius: 50%`, the page colour and the menu shadow. Before, it was a see through square at 4px.
  - The heading Report is clipped, not drawn.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **Focus order is as in the base build, not "Back, then bell, gear, then the report".** Measured in the base and after, at 390 and 320:
   - Tab from Back goes Back, Confirm, Dispute, Mark resolved, Flag, the trust details.
   - Shift Tab from Back goes Report (the heading), then My activity, Official alerts, Nearby in the bottom bar.
   - The bell and the gear come earlier in the page, in the top chrome before the shell. The view's pane is the last child of body (3c2e4ea).
   - The plan says "keeps", and the base never had the order it lists. Getting that order would mean moving nodes in map.js, which the plan allows only for strip or pan offsets. So the order is left as it was.
   - **The coordinator should decide whether to change it.**
2. **map.js and index.html.tmpl not changed.** Not needed: the strip centring and the pan paddings measure the boxes.
3. **Not available view left as it was.** The plan does not name it. Its Back stays a square at 4,8 on the page colour. Its top bar is still see through, and the floating bell and gear now carry the new look there too.
4. **Short landscape phone (max-height 359px) left at `calc(var(--disclaimer-bar-height) + var(--app-topbar-height))`.** With no strip, the 60px above the report now shows the map behind the floating controls instead of a white row.
5. **The half position moved up 33px at 390x844 (384 to 351; 259 to 226 at 320x568).**
   - sheet.js puts half at a share of the box, and the box now starts at the top of the page.
   - The visible map grows from 60..384 to 0..351.
   - The clear map under the chips row (which ends at 120) shrinks from 264px to 231px.
   - The short position (box less 208px) lands where it did before.
   - sheet.js is not in the plan's files, so this is left as is. The alternative was to keep the shell at 60 and pull only the map up under the chrome. That would bring the old half back if the owner prefers it.
6. **Reading of "the report's own title stays the page heading".** The view's heading for screen readers is still the h2 "Report", now clipped, which takes focus on open. The report's title in the card is a p element, as before, and was not promoted to a heading.
7. **Side observation, not caused by this plan (same in the base):** a script that clicks a list row while the view is already open moved the chosen pin below the strip in the simulator. Base: pin at 241 under a 220 strip. After: 211 under a 160 strip. A person cannot do this, because the list sheet is hidden while the view is open. Not fixed and out of scope.

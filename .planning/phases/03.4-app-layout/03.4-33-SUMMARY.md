# Phase 3.4 Plan 33: Step 3 is Allow location or Not now Summary

**Step 3 asks only Allow location or Not now, the person types their place once on step 4, and the map and the Low data list start at the home area cell centre (zoom 13) when the device location is not available.**

## What changed

### Task 1: step 3 (RED 78b1fc5, GREEN 9b869b0)
- welcome.html.tmpl: the whole typed location block (label, box, status, results, its Continue) is gone. The two buttons are `Allow location` and `Not now` (id `welcome-not-now`), in the same `welcome__actions--under` row. The `welcome-home-prefilled` line and its reference in the home field's `aria-describedby` are removed. The three step 3 cards are unchanged.
- welcome.js: removed `STEP3_SEARCH_URL`, `step3Search`, `step3Pick`, `enterStep4` (the whole prefill), `homeTouched`, `lastPrefill`, `drawnHome`, `homePrefilled`, `openSearch` and the Continue 3 wiring. Added `notNow()` (only `goTo(4)`). The home area search is now built when the home form exists (the old guard also required the step 3 form, which would have silently killed the step 4 search). The redundant home input listener that forgot a step 3 prefill was removed; `makeSearch` already forgets a pick when the text changes. `allowLocation` is untouched: it still asks the browser once, saves a fix only through PinalertPosition and goes to step 4 whatever the answer (denied, timeout, unsupported, bad fix).
- welcome.css: the `.welcome__search` rule is removed.
- Consent rule: unchanged (welcome.go, the 409 handling and Skip or Finish before Agree going back to step 2 are not touched; a new test pins that Not now is no way past Agree).

### Task 2: start at the home area (RED 2109514, GREEN c67c4f5)
- `service.AccountService.HomeAreaCentre` returns `geohash.DecodeCenter` of the stored precision 5 cell, ok false for no cell or a cell of the wrong shape, an error only when the row cannot be read. The exact picked point is not stored, so nothing finer than the cell centre can reach a page.
- `page.go` gets `PageConfig.HomeCentre` (interface `HomeCentreLookup`), `HomeAreaStartZoom = 13` and `startCentre`. `Page` and `LowPage` use it; their signatures are unchanged, so every existing caller and test is untouched. The router sets `page.HomeCentre = accounts`.
- Nearby: `data-fallback-lat`, `data-fallback-lon` and, for a home area centre only, `data-fallback-zoom="13"`. `app.js` reads it into `Pinalert.config.fallbackZoom` (default 11). `map.js` uses it in both fallback paths (first centring and Back to my location). A device fix still wins (it is tried first, zoom 13, own true); the fallback centre is never marked as the reader's own position, so no distances appear for it.
- Low data view: only the centre. Its script already prefers a stored or fresh device fix and falls back to the data-fallback centre.

## Tests removed, and why
All in `web/welcome_flow_test.mjs`, all pinning step 3 behaviour that no longer exists:
1. Type my location instead shows the search without asking for the position (the button and block are gone)
2. prefill from a step 3 search shows the line
3. no step 3 search shows no line (replaced by `step 3 never fills or empties step 4`)
4. stored home area shows no line (replaced by the same new test)
5. typing in step 3 after a pick forgets the pick, so step 4 is not prefilled
6. a failed or rate limited step 3 search shows a plain line and Continue still works
7. a search that never answers still ends, and Continue still works
8. typing after a step 3 prefill forgets the pick and hides the line
9. a prefilled home area from step 3 is saved with the picked place point on Continue
10. the `pickOnStep3` helper and five tests built on it: a street picked on step 3 prefills the area (never the street name), a street with no known area leaves step 4 empty, a street with no known area keeps the stored home area, a place that is already an area prefills as it did, editing the area filled in from a street forgets it
11. step 3 suggests any place while typing, with no areas filter
12. Enter in step 3 still searches at once
13. an untouched home area follows a changed step 3 pick; a touched one does not
Their facts that still hold (a typed home area stays, a stored home area is not re-sent, only a picked place is sent) are covered by the kept step 4 tests and the new ones. Two further tests were rewritten, not removed: `what was typed or picked is kept while moving between steps` now uses step 4 values only, and the Allow location test lost its `prefilled` line assertions.
Go contract and e2e pins changed in `web/welcome_contract_test.go` and `internal/api/handlers/welcome_e2e_test.go`: the pins for the Type my location button, the search block, Continue 3, the search label and placeholder, the Filled in line, `'/api/geocode?q='` and the `enterStep4` prefill were replaced by their opposites; the `.welcome__search` CSS pin was dropped. `web/trust_facts_contract_test.go` pins `boot(fallbackLat, fallbackLon, fallbackZoom, false)` instead of the literal 11.

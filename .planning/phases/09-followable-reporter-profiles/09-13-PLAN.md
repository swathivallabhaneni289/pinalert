Decisions this plan records (D-14, D-18, D-21, D-22, D-23):
- D-21, the cell rule: a rating's cell is the geohash_cell (precision 7) of the rater's most recent qualifying vote on any of the reporter's reports. Per reporter, ratings are grouped by cell and only the most recently written rating in each cell counts. Raters is the number of counted cells; the tap sum is the sum of those ratings. There is no own cell exclusion (orchestrator revision of 2026-10-27, reversing the first revision round): the report's own cell is the scene, where genuine witnesses stand, and Phase 3's confirmation count does not exclude it either. A reporter's second accounts are held back exactly as for confirmations: they need five different places about 150 m apart; there is no further protection against someone who controls accounts in five places, and plan 09-27 says so.
- D-22 replaces research A8: the tallies are returned whether the profile is on or off, with the reporter's profile_public flag; while off, points are min(outcome, combined).
- D-23: eligibility counts confirm, dispute, resolve and reopen votes; an unresolve alone does not qualify.
- G2 and W4: there is no account ban. A moderator removes one rating, or every rating by one rater; removing a rater's ratings also stamps accounts.rater_removed_at, and a removed rater can never rate again. Ratings from a deleted account stop counting through the join on accounts.deleted_at and are deleted with the account.
- G6: deleting an account deletes the ratings it gave and the ratings it received; plan 09-27 states it in docs/TRUST-MODEL.md.
- B4: five eligible raters in five different places can move the points by up to 40 either way ((20 times the average minus the outcome) divided by 2): they can drop any reporter below Reliable and lift any outcome score of 39 or more to it. The cell rule (five different places), moderator removal with follow restore and TRUSTX-03 are the answers; plan 09-27 documents this in these words, including that nothing more stops someone who controls accounts in five places.
- W5: the tapped part is shown rounded to half stars (TappedHalfStars), never as an exact average. This blurs the shown part but is not a guarantee: the exact total, the outcome part and the rater count still let a careful reader estimate the average closely; plan 09-27 documents the limit.
- The daily rule: a first rating is free; any later change, including Remove my rating, needs 24 hours since the rater's last change (UI-SPEC 3a).

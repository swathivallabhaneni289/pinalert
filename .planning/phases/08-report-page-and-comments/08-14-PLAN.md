## Artifacts this phase produces (this plan's part)

- comments.sql: `ClaimDueCommentChecks` (:many, params now, lease_until, batch; a MATERIALIZED CTE over comments with moderation_state = 'pending', deleted_at IS NULL (a comment its author deleted while held is never screened, review W7), next_attempt_at at or before now, a lease that is NULL or past, joined to reports with expires_at after now and moderation_state not removed, ORDER BY next_attempt_at, LIMIT batch, FOR UPDATE OF comments SKIP LOCKED; the update sets lease_until and check_attempts plus one; returns id, body, check_attempts), `FinishCommentCheck` (:execrows, sets moderation_state clear or removed, needs_review, verdict, scores, model, checked_at, clears the lease, only WHERE moderation_state = 'pending'), `RescheduleCommentCheck` (:execrows, next_attempt_at, last_error, clears the lease, only while pending).
- internal/service/commentworker.go: `type CommentWorkerQuerier interface` with `var _ CommentWorkerQuerier = (*sqlcgen.Queries)(nil)`; `type CommentWorker`; `NewCommentWorker(q CommentWorkerQuerier, s TextScreener, n CommentVisibleNotifier, opts ...WorkerOption) *CommentWorker` (reusing the WorkerOption clock, jitter and interval hooks if their type allows, else `CommentWorkerOption` twins); `Run(ctx)`, `SweepOnce(ctx) error`.
- Outcomes: pass gives clear; borderline gives clear with needs_review; reject gives removed (the comment never reached anyone but its author); a transient error reschedules by ModerationBackoff; quota and permanent errors move next_attempt_at past any claim, as the report worker does; a shutdown writes nothing.

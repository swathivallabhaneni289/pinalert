## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-08-10 | Tampering | absolute link built from Host | high | mitigate | URL from Deps.PublicBaseURL only; acceptance grep forbids r.Host and X-Forwarded-Host in share.go; TestShareLinkIgnoresHostHeader |
| T-08-11 | Denial of service | Share spam spending geocoder calls | medium | mitigate | per account Share bucket plus IP backstop, every attempt spends; lookup at most once per report (08-02) |

## Trust Boundaries

| one account's data to everyone's trust figures | Votes and reports feed other people's counts and ratings |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03.2-13 | Tampering | DeleteAccount | high | mitigate | Option A keeps votes, reports and sessions.account_id; the e2e compares another reader's view before and after |
| T-03.2-12 | Spoofing | a deleted account's cookie | high | mitigate | RevokeAccountSessions inside the same transaction, the gate's deleted_at filter (plan 06), TestDeleteAccountEndsEverySession with a copied cookie |
| T-03.2-11 | Tampering | a deleted account's browser signing in | high | mitigate | Plan 06's rotation: a deleted account's session is never rebound; TestDeletedBrowserSignsInGetsFreshSession |
| T-03.2-09 | Tampering (CSRF) | POST /api/me/delete | medium | mitigate | Gated, POST, strict JSON body that must say confirm true, SameSite Lax; the UI adds a confirm window (plan 12) |
| T-03.2-24 | Information disclosure | data kept after delete | medium | accept | The report text and the precision 7 voter cell remain by Option A, stated in the confirm window's third paragraph and the service comment; legal confidence is LOW (CLAUDE.md), so the owner decides Q2 before wide promotion |
| T-03.2-25 | Repudiation / Integrity | partial delete | medium | mitigate | One transaction through PgxAccountTx in production; the scrub is last, so a failure leaves a still usable account rather than a half deleted one |

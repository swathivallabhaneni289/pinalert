# Phase 03.2 Plan 13: Reliable reporter alert Summary

An opted in reader with a home area now gets one in app update when a Reliable reporter posts within 1, 2, 5 or 10 km of the middle of their home cell, written only through MayNotifyAboutReport and capped at one per reporter per hour, with Settings saving through PATCH /api/me/alerts.

## What was built

- **alertsettings.go**: the closed distance list, the default of 5 km, `HomeAreaAnchor(cell)` (Q5, owner confirmed 2026-10-18) and `AccountService.AlertSettings`, `UpdateAlertSettings`, `HomeAreaCell`. `UpdateAlertSettings` merges over the stored state, refuses a distance outside the list (`alert_radius_km`), a body that names nothing (`body`) and turning the alert on without a home area (`reliable_alerts`, "Add a home area first."), then saves through `UpsertAlertSettings`.
- **profile.go**: `ProfileQuerier` gains the four alert queries; `UpdateProfile` calls `DisableReliableAlerts` after a successful clear of the home area; `HomeAreaCentre` delegates to `HomeAreaAnchor`; the name screening comment now says it waits for Phase 9; `NewAccountService` takes variadic options (`WithAccountClock`).
- **updatesalerts.go**: `refreshReliableAlerts`, the last step of Refresh. It ends quietly with no nearby reader, no stored switch, a switch that is off or has no stamp, or no home cell. Otherwise it reads `Nearby` around `HomeAreaAnchor` with the chosen radius, keeps a report only when `MayNotifyAboutReport(UpdateReliableNearby, ...)` allows it, the reporter's tag is Reliable, it is not the reader's own, not followed or muted, and posted at or after `enabled_at`. It then does one `ReporterAccountsForReports` over the survivors and, oldest first, applies the per reporter hour cap (`AlertsFromReporterInWindow`) and `InsertReliableAlert`. No coordinate is written; the anchor is decoded, used for one read and dropped.
- **updatesservice.go**: Refresh calls the step with the same `now` and wraps its error as `service: Refresh: reliable alerts: %w`.
- **PATCH /api/me/alerts**: strict body, closed distance list enforced by the service, `Cache-Control: no-store`, answer `{reliable_alerts, alert_radius_km, has_home_area}`, swagger documented.
- **Settings**: `settingsViewModel` carries `AlertsOn`, `AlertRadiusKm`, `HasHomeArea`, `AlertRadii`. The home cell never enters the page.

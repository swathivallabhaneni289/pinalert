# Phase 03.2 Plan 07: Updates and mute routes Summary

The bell, the read mark, the mute control and the Settings muted list now have their routes in the gated group, every word of a row is written on the server, and My activity and Settings get their Updates rows and muted count from the same services.

## What was built

- **handlers/updates.go**: `GetUpdates` refreshes the caller's updates (the bell poll is the producer), logs a failed refresh and still lists, then answers `UpdatesResponse` with `Cache-Control: no-store`. `MarkUpdatesRead` takes `{up_to}` with `DisallowUnknownFields` and a 1 KB cap; `service.ValidationError` is a 400 on its field. `MuteReport` and `UnmuteReport` share `muteHandler` (account from the gate, `parseReportID`, `decodeEmptyBody`, 204, `ErrReportNotFound` as 404 field `id`). `GetMutedReports` lists the caller's mutes, no-store, with an empty array rather than null. The wire types have no account, session, cell, coordinate, email or description field. `updateRowLabel` builds the accessible name (UI-SPEC P32-C8) and `relativeTimeLong` the full words time.
- **activity.go**: `activityUpdateRow`, the four `activityPart` fields (`Updates`, `NewCount`, `UpdatesCut`, `UpdatesFailed`), `updateSeverityClass` (closed lookup), `updatesHubSummary` and `fillUpdatesPart`. `buildActivityPage` is unchanged in signature.
- **auth.go / settings.go / router.go**: `Profile` takes the updates service and reads `List(..., ActivityUpdatesListSize)` after `buildActivityPage`; a failed read flags the part and the page still renders. `SettingsPage` reads `MutedCount` into `settingsViewModel.MutedCount`. The router builds the two services and mounts the five routes with a comment each (no rate gate; the bell polls every 30 seconds).
- **docs**: `make swag` regenerated; additions only. `TestSwaggerSpecCoversRoutes` checks the five paths and their 401.

## Deviations from Plan

### Notes (no rule applied)

**1. The e2e harness has no fixed clock for the updates service.** The plan asked for a fixed clock harness. `UpdatesService` takes its clock only through `WithUpdatesClock`, and the plan forbids a new `Deps` field, so the router uses the wall clock. "An hour later" is made in the e2e by moving `update_cursors.last_update_at` two hours back for the report (`p32uAgeCursors`). `adopted_at` is left alone so the self caused rule keeps its meaning. The unit tests in plan 03 already pin the exact gap edges with an injected clock.

**2. The Live step uses cells 0, 1 and 2 (B, C, D).** The resolver threshold is 2 distinct places, so B and C would be enough; B, C and D were used as the plan says, which also makes the trust sentence "Confirmed by 3 people at different spots." for the leak test.

**3. The handler's relative time uses `time.Now()`.** Like `Profile`, the handlers do not take a clock, so the `when` and `label` words are not asserted to an exact minute in the e2e; the unit tests pin them with fixed times.

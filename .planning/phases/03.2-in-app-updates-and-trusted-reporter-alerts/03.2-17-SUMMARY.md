# Phase 03.2 Plan 17: Reliable reporter alert end to end and in the trust model Summary

The Reliable reporter alert is now proven over the bell's own route (GET /api/updates with PATCH /api/me/alerts, PATCH /api/me/profile, the mute route and the vote routes) with a seeded Reliable reporter, and docs/TRUST-MODEL.md states who gets one, the gate, the caps, the words, the price of the tag and the open TRUSTX-03 dependency.

## What was built

- **reliable_alert_e2e_test.go** (5 tests, prefix p17ra, real router, real Postgres on p32_17):
  - `TestReliableAlertOverTheBell`: a report posted before the switch never alerts; an unconfirmed critical report alerts once as "Reported nearby by a Reliable reporter." with "Not confirmed yet."; a second poll writes no second row; the answer names no email or session.
  - `TestReliableAlertAtPostingWhenNotCritical`: a low report alerts at posting while Provisional; two confirmations from two cells change the same row (same id) to "Confirmed by ..." with no second row; an hour later a second low report that two readers dispute from two cells answers `hidden` and never alerts.
  - `TestReliableAlertCapPerReporter`: two critical reports inside the hour give one alert (the older); after the hour the second alerts, still "Not confirmed yet.".
  - `TestReliableAlertSkipsOwnMutedAndOld`: a pre switch report, a report the reader muted, and the reporter's own report (the reporter has a home area and the switch on) give nothing; a fresh report then alerts the reader, so the quiet cases are the rule and not a broken setup.
  - `TestReliableAlertStopsWhenSwitchedOff`: after PATCH /api/me/alerts with reliable_alerts false a new report gives nothing and writes no row; removing the home area leaves the switch off with no `enabled_at` in the database and a new report still gives nothing.
- **docs/TRUST-MODEL.md**: new section "Reliable reporter alerts" after "Show menu: Only Reliable reporters", naming `DefaultAlertRadiusKm`, `MayNotifyAboutReport`, `ReliableAlertsPerReporterPerHour`, `UpdatesHourlyCeiling`, the three test names, and the open TRUSTX-03 dependency before push or email (ROADMAP 3.2 scope note 2; Phase 3.3 must record or meet it). It also says plainly that the tag is farmable at the "Honest limits" price and that the rule is not evidence of safety to widen.

## Deviations from Plan

None in behaviour; no production file changed and no case failed, so no production bug is reported. Notes:

- **Clock (test design, not a deviation of scope):** the plan says "the clock moves forward before R posts". Only the report and voting services can be given a clock through `api.Deps`; the updates and account services use the wall clock. The test therefore adds a settable offset to the wall clock for the report and voting services, and ages the stored alert rows for the hour cap. `newE2EServerWith` is used rather than `newTrustE2EServer` because the trust harness fixes its clock at a single instant one minute ahead, which would stamp a pre switch report later than the switch.
- **Session secret:** the seeded reporter's cookie is signed with the `newE2EServerWith` secret, in a local helper (`p17raSessionClient`), because the existing helper signs with the trust harness secret.
- **Sandbox:** `env -i` and `env -u` and output redirection were refused by the worktree guard, so tests ran with the plain `DATABASE_URL=...` prefix after confirming `DATABASE_URL` is empty in the shell.

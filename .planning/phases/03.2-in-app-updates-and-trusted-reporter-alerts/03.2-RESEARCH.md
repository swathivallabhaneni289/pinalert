#### Phase Boundary

A signed in person is told inside the app, while the app is open, when something changes on a report they posted or voted on, and can opt in to hear when a Reliable reporter posts nearby. #### Following and muting (NOTIF-02)
- **D-09 [LOCKED EARLIER]:** A person follows a report automatically when they posted it or voted on it, and can mute any report (NOTIF-02, ROADMAP criterion 2).

#### What an update says and when (NOTIF-03)
- **D-12 [LOCKED EARLIER]:** State changes only: the report reaches Live, is hidden by disputes, is resolved, or is within 1 hour of expiring. At most one update per report per hour. Anonymous words built from the same server trust object as the popup; never a voter, an account or a location. Every decision calls the single visibility resolver. Time events are evaluated lazily on the bell refresh, not by a scheduler (ROADMAP criterion 3, scope note 3).
- **D-13 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q5]:** People do not hear about their own actions (own Mark resolved, own confirm that makes a report Live).
- **D-14 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q7]:** The "about to fade" update goes to everyone following the report, only while it is still active, once per expiry, and says it fades in about an hour and nothing about keeping it alive (backlog 999.12).
- **D-15 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q8]:** A row shows a plain event line ("Now confirmed", "Hidden by disputes", "Marked resolved", "Fades in about an hour"), the category and severity words, how long ago, and the trust sentence for now. No description, no place name, no frozen number. Note: Phase 3.3's lock screen text adds an area or landmark name for push only (03.3-CONTEXT); bell rows keep this rule.
- **D-16 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q12 and Q14]:** At most 10 stored updates per person per hour, anything beyond rolls into one "and N more" row. Updates are kept 7 days, the same as the Past tab.

## Summary

The phase is still mostly a composition problem. `Resolve` is the single visibility authority, `BuildTrust` builds the one trust object, `ViewOf` maps a visibility plus expiry to a view word, `CurrentVotesForReports` and `ReporterAccountsForReports` are the batched reads, and `ReportService.Nearby` already filters by distance and resolves each report. What changed since 2026-10-15 is the surface around them. Phase 3.4 built the bell button and its empty list, the five-part My activity, the Settings page with About you, the home-area storage and the Show menu, and Phase 4 added the moderation state that every `Resolve` caller must now pass. So the work is: fill the bell and the Updates part, add the storage and the decision service, add the tick, and add the Settings rows.

The recommended shape stays lazy and read driven. A new gated `GET /api/updates`, called by a new self contained `bell.js` on the four pages that carry the bell (Nearby, Official alerts, My activity, Settings), every 30 seconds, loads the account's open followed reports, resolves each with the same pipeline the feed uses, compares the result with a per (account, report) cursor row, and writes at most one update per report per hour with one atomic statement. The "about to fade" event is one more comparison against `expires_at`. The Reliable alert no longer needs coordinates from the browser: it measures from the decoded centre of the stored home-area cell, so the whole alert path is server side and the browser sends nothing.

Three designs need an owner answer before planning is final, and the research below presents the options with a recommendation: (1) Delete my account (anonymise or remove, with their effect on other people's counts), (2) how "1 km" and "2 km from my home area" can mean anything when the stored home area is a 5 km cell (the cell's half-diagonal is about 3.4 km, measured below), and (3) the words of the Phone notifications section while the only rows in it are in-app. Log out of all devices needs a new revocation marker on `sessions`; clearing `sessions.account_id` would erase authorship and change other people's screens.

**Primary recommendation:** Build in three waves: (A) NOTIF-01 to NOTIF-03 as one vertical slice (migration 00009 tables, follow query, `DecideUpdate`, `UpdatesService.Refresh`, `GET /api/updates`, `bell.js`, the Updates part, the Mute control and the Muted reports row); (B) NOTIF-04 and NOTIF-05 (the Show menu tick, the alert settings rows and the alert rule); (C) the account lifecycle rows (Log out of all devices, Delete my account) once the owner has chosen anonymise or remove. Re-check `ls internal/store/migrations` for the free number at plan time; 00009 is free on 2026-10-17.

### Pattern 1: the pure decision function
**What:** `DecideUpdate(in DecideInput) Decision` takes the cursor row (or none), the current `Visibility`, `ViewOf`, `expiresAt`, the account's own latest vote time on that report, whether the report is muted and `now`. It returns nothing, a silent baseline write, or one event with the new cursor. No I/O, no clock, like `Resolve` and `ClassifyOutcome`, so it is table testable with injected times.

**Rules, in order** `[ASSUMED: design]`:
1. Muted: do nothing and write nothing.
2. `Withheld` (held or removed after review): no event, do not move the cursor. The row, if shown later, reads "no longer available" (Pitfall 9).
3. No cursor row: adopt the current visibility as the baseline and emit nothing. This stops a first load flooding the bell for a person who already has 40 votes on open reports.
4. Self caused: if the account's own latest vote on this report is newer than `adopted_at`, adopt the current visibility silently (Pitfall 3).
5. State change: if the current visibility is Live, Hidden or Retracted and differs from `last_state`, and the hour cap allows, emit `reached_live`, `hidden` or `resolved`. If it is Provisional, emit nothing and do not move `last_state`. If the cap blocks, change nothing, so the next poll re-checks (a change that reverts inside the hour is never reported, which is intended).
6. Fading: if `ViewOf` is active, `now < expires_at <= now + 1h`, `fading_for != expires_at`, and the cap allows, emit `fading` and set `fading_for = expires_at`. Never after expiry.

Critical (and old Rescue) reports are Live from the first poll, so for them the only events are `resolved` and `fading`; this falls out of the rules with no special case.

### Pattern 2: exactly once without a transaction
Two tabs or devices will poll in the same second. One statement does the compare and swap and the insert together:

```sql
-- name: AdvanceCursorAndInsertUpdate :one
WITH moved AS (
    UPDATE update_cursors
       SET last_state = sqlc.arg(new_state), last_update_at = sqlc.arg(now)::timestamptz
     WHERE account_id = sqlc.arg(account_id) AND report_id = sqlc.arg(report_id)
       AND last_state = sqlc.arg(old_state)
       AND (last_update_at IS NULL OR last_update_at <= sqlc.arg(cap_edge)::timestamptz)
    RETURNING account_id, report_id
)
INSERT INTO updates (account_id, report_id, kind, created_at)
SELECT account_id, report_id, sqlc.arg(kind), sqlc.arg(now)::timestamptz FROM moved
RETURNING id;
```
Zero rows returned means another request won or the cap blocked; both are fine. The repo has no transaction on the vote path by design (T-02-02). That a single data modifying CTE of this shape is atomic under concurrent callers is `[ASSUMED: PostgreSQL documentation knowledge, not fetched this session]`; prove it with the concurrent store test. The Reliable alert insert uses the partial unique index with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`.

### Pattern 3: the followed set is derived and bounded
`FollowedOpenReports(account_id, now, row_cap)`: unexpired reports the account posted (join `sessions` on `account_id`, uses `idx_reports_session_expires`) UNION unexpired reports the account has any vote row on (`votes.account_id`, any kind, uses `idx_votes_account_kind_report_created`). It also returns `severity`, `category`, `created_at`, `expires_at` and `moderation_state` so the refresh builds the `ReportMeta` without a second read. Only unexpired reports can change state, so the set is bounded by report lifetime. Cap rows at about 200, newest first, and document that a heavy account loses the oldest. Select no session id and no voter cell (T-01-02). Any vote kind counts (D-10).

### Pattern 4: alerts anchored on the home area
`Refresh` runs the alert step only when the account's settings row has `reliable_alerts_on` and the account has a `home_area_cell`. It decodes the cell with `geohash.DecodeCenter(cell)` and calls `ReportService.Nearby(NearbyQuery{Latitude, Longitude: that centre, RadiusKm: alert_radius_km, ViewerAccountID: account})`, then keeps a `ReportView` only when `MayNotifyAboutReport(Visibility, ExpiresAt, now)` and `Trust.Reporter.Tag == ReporterReliable` and not `IsOwnReport` and not already followed by this account and not muted and `created_at >= enabled_at` and the per reporter cap allows. The per reporter cap needs the reporter account id: call `ReporterAccountsForReports` once over the surviving ids (usually zero to a handful). The coordinates in play are a decoded cell centre, not a person's position, and nothing is written. Owner question Q-B explains why 1 and 2 km are finer than the stored cell. With no home area the row shows a plain line (Pattern 9) and the step is skipped.

### Pattern 5: words at read time, link at click time

### Pattern 10: Log out of all devices **[NEW 2026-10-17]**
**How sessions work:** the cookie is `pinalert_session = <id>.<HMAC(id)>` (`cookie.go:57-82`), one year, no account inside. A session belongs to an account only through the row `sessions(session_id, account_id)`; the gate reads that row (`sessions.sql:17-22`, `gate.go:84`). Plain Log out clears the browser's cookie and leaves the row bound on purpose (DEC-K, `cookie.go:149-175`): that binding is how posts reach their author (`ReportsByAccount`, `ReporterAccountsForReports`, `ReportVoteContext` and so the self-vote block and the reporter's instant resolve). A copied cookie therefore stays valid after plain Log out.

**Do not** clear `sessions.account_id`: it would erase the person's history and make the reporter nil in `BuildVoteTally`, so their own resolve stops being the privileged vote and other people's screens change.

**Recommended:** add `sessions.revoked_at TIMESTAMPTZ NULL`. `POST /auth/logout-all` runs `UPDATE sessions SET revoked_at = now() WHERE account_id = $1 AND revoked_at IS NULL`, then clears this browser's cookie and redirects to `/login` like `Logout` (`auth.go:806-812`). `GetAccountBySessionID` gains `AND sessions.revoked_at IS NULL` (covers `requireVerifiedAccount` and `requireModerator`, `moderator.go:43`). Authorship joins are untouched.

**The sign-in path must be changed with it, or the marker moves authorship.** `VerifyToken` decides "already signed in as someone else" with `GetAccountBySessionID` (`service/auth.go:251`) and then binds with `BindSessionAccount`, whose upsert is `ON CONFLICT (session_id) DO UPDATE SET account_id = EXCLUDED.account_id` (`sessions.sql:6-12`). A revoked device keeps its HMAC-valid cookie, so its next magic link reuses the same `session_id`. With the new filter the lookup returns no row, the conflict check never fires, and the bind rewrites the row to whoever signs in, which moves every report filed from that session to the new account (today the conflict check prevents exactly this). The same hole opens for a deleted account under Option A. Rule: **a revoked or deleted session id is never rebound to a different account; sign-in rotates to a fresh id.**
- Add `GetSessionBinding(session_id)` returning `account_id` and `revoked_at` without the revoked filter; `VerifyToken` reads it first.
- Unbound row: bind as today. Bound and live: today's logic (same email passes, a different email is `OutcomeConflict`). Bound, revoked and the token's email belongs to the same account: clear `revoked_at` and carry on, so a device that logs in again with its old cookie works. Bound, revoked and a different account: do not touch the old row; draw a fresh id (`Manager.NewID`, `cookie.go:56`), bind the new id to the signing-in account, and have `Verify` (`handlers/auth.go:226`) set the new cookie (a small exported `Manager` method that writes the cookie `Middleware` writes at `cookie.go:112`).
- Make the bind itself safe too: `ON CONFLICT ... DO UPDATE SET account_id = EXCLUDED.account_id, revoked_at = NULL WHERE sessions.account_id IS NULL OR sessions.account_id = EXCLUDED.account_id`, run as `:execrows`, so a future caller that forgets the rule changes nothing.
- The token is consumed before the bind (`service/auth.go:272`, bind at `:293`), so the binding read and the rotation decision must come before `ConsumeToken`.

Also delete the account's push subscriptions in Phase 3.3 (03.3-CONTEXT lists this). Tests: a second client with the same cookie gets a 401 after logout-all; authorship, `ReportsByAccount` and the tally are unchanged; a re-login on the revoked browser as the same email works; **a revoked browser that signs in as a different email gets a new session, and the first account's reports stay with the first account.**

Optional hardening, a separate decision: make plain Log out also set `revoked_at` on the current session row. It closes the copied cookie hole but changes DEC-K's documented behaviour; leave it out unless the owner asks (T-5).

### Pattern 11: Delete my account **[NEW 2026-10-17]**
**What touches what.** `accounts(id, email UNIQUE NOT NULL, ...)` is referenced by `sessions.account_id`, `votes.account_id` (NOT NULL), `report_flags.account_id` (NOT NULL), `moderation_actions.moderator_account_id` (NOT NULL), `reports.request_account_id`, and the new tables of this phase. `magic_link_tokens.email` and `email_cooldowns` hold the email by value. Votes are an append only log (00004 header) read by `CurrentVotesForReports`; the independence counts are distinct `geohash_cell`s over each account's latest vote per kind, and the reporter is identified through `sessions.account_id`. `ClassifyOutcome` and `ScoreReliability` read other people's reports' tallies, so removing a vote changes other reporters' ratings.

Either option first deletes what is purely the person's: `updates`, `update_cursors`, `report_mutes`, `account_alert_settings`, push subscriptions (3.3), the name and home area, and `magic_link_tokens` and `email_cooldowns` rows for the email.

**Option A: anonymise (keep the reports and votes, cut the link to a person).** Keep the `accounts` row's id, so every FK stays valid, but scrub it in place: email replaced by a unique placeholder that cannot receive mail (the column is `NOT NULL UNIQUE`), `display_name`, `home_area_*`, `terms_*`, `first_run_done_at` cleared, `deleted_at = now()`; revoke every session (`revoked_at`) and make the gate refuse a deleted account; the sign-in rotation rule of Pattern 10 stops the next account that signs in on that device from inheriting the history. The same address can then sign up as a brand new account with no history. Effect on the trust mechanic: none. Votes, sessions and report authorship are byte for byte unchanged, so every count, `Resolve` outcome, `ClassifyOutcome` result and reporter rating stays as it was; the deleted account's reports and votes remain, attributed to no one anybody can identify. What stays: the report text people wrote and the voter cell at precision 7 (about 150 m) on each vote row. The report text may still say who the person is; a variant blanks the description of the account's own reports (changes what others read, nothing in the tally). Whether keeping these satisfies the owner's and a legal reader's idea of "delete" is a policy call (`[ASSUMED]` that India's DPDP Act 2023 right to erasure is relevant; CLAUDE.md marks legal confidence LOW and asks for a review before wide promotion).

**Option B: remove (delete the account's reports and votes and let counts recompute).** Delete the account's votes (and flags, moderation rows if any), then its reports with the votes others cast on them, then the account. Effects on others: any report this person confirmed or disputed has its cells recomputed on the next read, so a report can fall from Live to Provisional or rise from Hidden, and other reporters' `ClassifyOutcome` and reliability scores can move; a report this person posted vanishes from other people's "You confirmed" and "You disputed" lists and from Ended nearby; a resolve vote they cast on someone else's report only counts as an ordinary resolve cell (only the reporter's own resolve is privileged), so deleting it can only move `ResolveCells`, while deleting a reporter's own resolve on their own report removes the report with it. The append only log principle (Phase 2 D-08) is broken for this account. Needs one service transaction and explicit delete order because no FK cascades; `moderation_actions.moderator_account_id` blocks deleting a moderator's account unless handled.

**Recommendation:** Option A, because nobody else's report changes state and the rating stays honest, with the plain words "your reports stay, no longer linked to you" on the confirm window. Put the choice to the owner in plain words (Open owner question Q-A). Either way: confirm window (X only), a typed or tapped confirmation, `Cache-Control: no-store`, ends every session, deletes in one transaction, and an e2e test that another account's feed and rating are unchanged (A) or recomputed (B).

Labels: "Mute updates for this report" and "Turn updates back on". Routes `POST /api/reports/{id}/mute` and `/unmute`, idempotent, scoped to the gate's account, report id digits only. Muting deletes the account's unread updates for that report and stops new ones (D-11). Whether the Low data view gets it too (flag.js mounts there, `low.js:366`) is technical question T-4; D-01 (same features) argues yes if it costs little.

### Seam for Phase 3.3 (installable app and phone alerts)
`03.3-CONTEXT.md` D-06 and D-22 assume this phase delivers `UpdateKind`, `DecideUpdate`, the cursor rows, the hour cap, `MayNotifyAboutReport`, the Reliable rule and the shared "How far from my home area" setting, and says the Reliable alert measures from the stored home area. What that needs from 3.2:
- **In 3.2 the bell poll is the only producer.** Nothing is created while the app is closed, so 3.3 adds a write time trigger on top of the same decision code (after a successful `CastVote`, evaluate the report's followers except the actor; the fading check on any incoming request). That trigger is 3.3's.
- **Keep every shared piece keyed by (account, report), never by the requesting reader:** `DecideUpdate`, the cursor, `MayNotifyAboutReport`, `UpdateKind`, the word builders and the per reporter cap query take an account id as a parameter and never read request context.
- **The home-area distance setting is shared.** Store it once (`account_alert_settings.alert_radius_km`); 3.3's other four switches join the same table.
- **Home-area read:** because 3.3 measures kind (1) and (3) from the cell too, add the cell read as a service method now, not a handler detail.
- **No push, service worker, VAPID or subscription code in 3.2.** Keep `updates` rows free of anything channel specific.
- **The bell must keep working for people who never install or who refuse push** (03.3 criterion 5).

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- **Notify from `Submit`.** `Submit` never calls `Resolve`; a hook there bypasses the provisional gate by construction.
- **A visibility check in SQL for alerts.** TRUST-02 and T-02-04: SQL stays blind, Go decides.
- **Copying the critical rule.** Use `Resolve`'s answer, or `ReportMeta.Serious()` for the tick.
- **Calling `Resolve` without setting `Moderation` in the same function.** `TestResolveCallersMentionModeration` fails the package (`resolve_callers_test.go:96-`).
- **Storing counts or words in `updates`.** They go stale and could contradict the popup.
- **Writing a coordinate or `distance_km` anywhere.** The home cell is the only stored place.
- **Giving update rows `report-row` markup.** `activity.js` would upgrade them (Pattern 8).
- **Putting a request in `app-bar.js`, or the bell's data on a view model field.** `renderTopBar` only passes `Nav` (Pattern 7).
- **A GET that touches anyone else's rows.** `Refresh` writes only rows keyed to the gate's account id.

## Don't Hand-Roll

| Problem | Don't Build | Use Instead | Why |
|---------|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Visibility of a report | A second state machine in the updates code | `BuildVoteTally` + `Resolve` (with `Moderation`) + `ViewOf` | One authority (TRUST-02); `TestWithheldInNoView` and `TestResolveCallersMentionModeration` guard it |
| Distance and radius | A new Haversine or bounding box | `Nearby` / `BoundingBox`, with `geohash.DecodeCenter` for the anchor | Already proven and index tested |
| Reporter reliability | A new tag computation | `Trust.Reporter.Tag` from `ReporterSignals` | Already composed and tested |
| The keep-critical rule for the tick | A second critical predicate | `ReportMeta.Serious()` | The one rule (R-04) |
| Exactly once insert | Application locks | One CTE statement, partial unique index | Matches the repo's no transaction stance |
| Scheduling the fading event | A ticker or cron | Compare `expires_at` on the poll | The host sleeps; scope note 3 |
| Bell list behaviour | A second dropdown | `app-bar.js` (Escape, X, capture phase outside click, Tab trap) plus `bell.js` for data only | Same accessibility contract and tests |
| Per report control with viewer state | A new pattern | `flag.js` (create once, update in place, state on the wire) | Same lifecycle bugs already fixed there |
| Edit and confirm windows | A new modal | The `app-window` component (`window.css`, `settings.html.tmpl:100-138`), X only | D-02 one way to close |
| Wording | Strings in JavaScript | Server written sentences from `BuildTrust` facts | "No threshold may live in JavaScript" (Phase 3) |
| Ending sessions | Deleting `sessions` rows or clearing `account_id` | A revocation marker | Pattern 10 |
| Account deletion | `DELETE FROM accounts` | A service transaction in the order of Pattern 11 | No FK has `ON DELETE` |

**Key insight:** the cheapest way to meet "never bypass the provisional gate" is to have no code path that decides eligibility except by reading `Resolve`'s answer.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Riding the feed request leaves most bells frozen **[CORRECTED 2026-10-17]**
**What goes wrong:** Putting `unread` on `GET /api/reports` looks like "rides on the existing 30 second refresh". But the feed request and its timer exist only on Nearby (`map.js:366` starts it; `data-poll-interval-ms` is only on `index` and `low` templates). Official alerts, My activity and Settings carry a bell and never poll. Phase 4 also pins `app.js` at two `fetch(` calls in five tests.
**How to avoid:** A separate gated endpoint and `bell.js` with its own timer on the four bell pages. "Rides on the 30 second refresh" is met by the same interval, not the same request. Flag the wording to the owner (D-04).
**Warning signs:** The count updates on Nearby and stops on My activity.

### Pitfall 2: Duplicate and missed rows from concurrent polls
**What goes wrong:** Two tabs poll together and both insert. Or the cap blocks and the cursor moves anyway, so the change is never announced.
**How to avoid:** Pattern 2. When the cap blocks, do not advance the cursor. Test two concurrent refreshes against a disposable database and assert one row.

### Pitfall 3: Updates about the reader's own action
**What goes wrong:** A reporter taps Mark resolved and the bell says the report was resolved. A voter's second confirm flips the report to Live and the bell announces it.
**Fix:** rule 4 of Pattern 1: compare the account's own latest vote time with `adopted_at`. It needs no write path hook; it can swallow another person's change landing in the same 30 seconds, which is documented as acceptable. `[ASSUMED]`

### Pitfall 4: "Within 1 hour of expiring" promising too much
**What goes wrong:** Wording like "confirm it to keep it alive" is false (backlog 999.12). It must not fire after expiry or on a resolved or withheld report.
**How to avoid:** Emit only while `ViewOf` says active. Words say "fades in about an hour". Store `fading_for` as the announced expiry.

### Pitfall 5: Stored text goes stale or leaks
**What goes wrong:** A frozen count is wrong an hour later; a description excerpt leaks an address; a place name locates the report.
**How to avoid:** Pattern 5. A Go test over `updatescopy.go`: no em or en dash (the dash guards scan templates and listed scripts; add `bell.js`, `mute.js`, `settings-alerts.js` to `phaseScripts`, `layout_rules_contract_test.go:466`; generated swagger docs carry long dashes already, backlog 999.8), and no description, coordinate or account value.

### Pitfall 6: Dispute spam as a notification weapon
**What goes wrong:** Two colluding accounts at different spots can Hide a report and flip it back; each flip could tell the poster and every follower.
**How to avoid:** The cursor collapses flip flops (announce only when the net state differs from the last announced one), one per report per hour per recipient, a ceiling of 10 stored updates per recipient per hour with an "and N more" row (D-16). The 24 per day worst case per report per recipient is the stated cost. `[ASSUMED]`

### Pitfall 7: A farmed Reliable account is a fan out lever
**What goes wrong:** Phase 3 D-03 prices the tag at extra verified accounts plus a claimed location. A farmed Reliable account can post a Critical report, which bypasses the gate, and reach every opted in reader nearby with zero confirmations.
**How to avoid:** The per reporter per recipient hourly cap (NOTIF-05) is mandatory. Also the words say "Not confirmed yet"; a per reporter ceiling across all recipients per hour (default 3); TRUSTX-03 burst detection is not blocking for an in-app only bell but is a hard dependency before push or email reuse this rule (ROADMAP scope note 2, `docs/TRUST-MODEL.md` "Hand offs").

### Pitfall 8: The Reliable tick silently drops real Reliable reporters
**What goes wrong:** `Nearby` loads history for at most 200 reporter accounts, nearest first (`report.go:838`); past that the tag reads none, so the tick would hide their reports.
**How to avoid:** Document it. With the 5 to 10 km Nearby read it is rare; the line under the tick may say the list can be incomplete when the read was capped, or the cap can be raised for the read. On a small deployment most reporters read New reporter (D-13), so the tick may keep only critical reports until Phase 5's demo accounts exist, and the empty state must say what the tick keeps. `[ASSUMED]`

### Pitfall 9: Moderation state and the resolver guard **[REWRITTEN 2026-10-17]**
**What goes wrong:** Phase 4 landed. A report can be `Withheld` (held pending a text check, or removed after review). `TestResolveCallersMentionModeration` fails any service function that calls `Resolve` without a `Moderation` key in a `ReportMeta` literal or an assignment to `.Moderation` in the same function body (`resolve_callers_test.go:96-`). `TestWithheldInNoView` fails if a new `bool` method on `Visibility`, or any function whose declaration line names `FeedView`, is not called in it (`moderation_view_test.go:39-94, 200-218`).
**How to avoid:** The follow query returns `moderation_state`, and `Refresh` builds `ReportMeta{Severity, Category, Moderation}` itself. Write `MayNotifyAboutReport(vis Visibility, expiresAt, now time.Time) bool` as a free function, not a method on `Visibility`, with no `FeedView` in its declaration line (the regexes would not catch it, but add a `Withheld` row to `TestWithheldInNoView` anyway so the guard is explicit). `DecideUpdate` ignores `Withheld`. A row for a report that became withheld or unlisted reads "no longer available" (04-CONTEXT: "Phase 3.2 may show Removed after review as an update"; this research keeps it as a silent "no longer available" row, not a new event kind).

### Pitfall 10: Polling and the host that sleeps
**What goes wrong:** Alerts only exist while a page is open. A report posted and expired between two polls is never alerted. A user returning after hours finds updates stamped "now" for earlier events.
**How to avoid:** Say it in the product copy. Stamp rows with detection time and word them as the current state. Re-check on `pageshow` and `visibilitychange`.

### Pitfall 11: Unread badge and screen readers
**What goes wrong:** An `aria-live` region on a count that changes on poll re-announces every 30 seconds (Phase 3 forbids it on the trust block for the same reason).
**How to avoid:** No `aria-live` on the count. Put the number in the button's accessible name ("Updates, 3 new") and update it quietly. The bell list is a dialog opened by the user; a short text status only when it opens.

### Pitfall 12: The Account section is phone only today **[NEW 2026-10-17]**
**What goes wrong:** Log out of all devices and Delete my account must be reachable on both sizes (03.4 D-01), but the Account section is wrapped in `place-section--phone` (`settings.html.tmpl:82`) and hidden at 900px (`places.css:163`), and `sizeOnlyNamed` names only that.
**How to avoid:** Draw the section on both sizes; move the phone only class onto the Log out row (the laptop has Log out in the person menu); replace the `sizeOnlyNamed` entry with one naming the row and its reason.

### Pitfall 13: Distance finer than the stored place **[NEW 2026-10-17]**
**What goes wrong:** The home area is a geohash precision 5 cell (about 4.3 to 4.9 km across), not the picked point. A row that says "1 km from my home area" implies a precision the data lacks. See Open owner question Q-B.

### Pitfall 14: Deleting or logging out must not change other people's screens **[NEW 2026-10-17]**
**What goes wrong:** Clearing `sessions.account_id`, or removing votes and reports, changes the reporter identity or the cells other people's counts are built from (Pattern 10, Pattern 11).
**How to avoid:** Revocation marker; anonymise in place; test that another account's feed and score are unchanged.

### The one gate predicate for anything that may notify
```go
// MayNotifyAboutReport is the only place that says a report may be announced.
// It asks the resolver's output, never the report's own severity, so the
// provisional gate cannot be bypassed by a second copy of the critical rule.
// A free function on purpose: a bool method on Visibility, or a signature that
// names FeedView, would have to be exercised in TestWithheldInNoView.
func MayNotifyAboutReport(vis Visibility, expiresAt, now time.Time) bool {
	return vis == VisibilityLive && ViewOf(vis, expiresAt, now) == FeedViewActive
}
```

### Revocation in the gate query
```sql
-- name: GetAccountBySessionID :one
SELECT accounts.id, accounts.email, accounts.terms_version, accounts.first_run_done_at
FROM sessions
JOIN accounts ON sessions.account_id = accounts.id
WHERE sessions.session_id = sqlc.arg(session_id)
  AND sessions.revoked_at IS NULL;

-- name: GetSessionBinding :one
-- Raw read for the sign-in path only: it does NOT filter on revoked_at, so a
-- revoked session still shows the account it is bound to.
SELECT account_id, revoked_at FROM sessions WHERE session_id = sqlc.arg(session_id);

-- name: BindSessionAccount :execrows
-- Never moves a bound session to a different account. Zero rows affected means
-- the caller must rotate to a fresh session id (Pattern 10).
INSERT INTO sessions (session_id, account_id)
VALUES (sqlc.arg(session_id), sqlc.arg(account_id))
ON CONFLICT (session_id) DO UPDATE
   SET account_id = EXCLUDED.account_id, revoked_at = NULL
 WHERE sessions.account_id IS NULL OR sessions.account_id = EXCLUDED.account_id;
```

### Known Threat Patterns
| Pattern | STRIDE | Standard Mitigation |
|---------|--------|---------------------|
| Dispute spam to flood a poster's bell | Denial of service / Tampering | Net state cursor, one per report per hour, ceiling with roll up row |
| Farmed Reliable account sends a false critical alert to many readers | Spoofing / Tampering | Per reporter per recipient hourly cap, per reporter ceiling, "Not confirmed yet" wording, TRUSTX-03 before wider channels |
| Reading another person's updates or muting for them (IDOR) | Information disclosure | Account id only from the gate; list and mutate queries filtered by it; leak test |
| Update text or link reveals a held, removed or private detail | Information disclosure | Words built at read time from the current listable view; "no longer available" when not shown; no description; moderation state never revealed |
| Stolen cookie survives Log out | Spoofing | Log out of all devices revokes server side; plain Log out limit stays documented (T-5) |
| Deleted account's cookie keeps working | Spoofing | Deleting revokes every session and the gate refuses a deleted account |
| Stored XSS through report text or a muted-report row in a panel | Tampering | Words are server written; every string reaches the DOM through `textContent` / `setText` (T-01-03); no markup parsing sink; `js_sinks_contract_test.go` scans every script |
| Cross site POST to mute, settings, logout-all or delete | Tampering | SameSite=Lax cookie, POST/PATCH only, JSON body; delete needs a confirm step |
| Polling endpoint abuse | Denial of service | Bounded follow set and `Nearby` radius (at most 10 km); no new limiter in this phase. Per account limiting has landed (`internal/ratelimit/perkey.go`, `handlers/ratelimit_gate.go`; the geocode routes already use a per account budget) and can be reused for `GET /api/updates` if load shows; per IP limiters suffer CGNAT |

## Open owner questions

Plain words, one decision each, with a recommendation. Q-A and Q-B block the waves named; the rest can stand on the default.

**Q-A. BLOCKING for wave C. When someone deletes their account, what happens to the reports they posted and the confirm or dispute taps they made?**
Option 1 (recommended): they stay, but nobody can tell who they came from. Nobody else's report changes (no confirmed report suddenly drops to unconfirmed, no reporter score moves). Option 2: they are removed. A report that someone's tap helped confirm can go back to unconfirmed, other reporters' scores can change, and other people's "You confirmed" lists lose entries. Under option 1 the words of a report stay readable; we can also blank the description of the person's own reports if the owner wants.

**Q-B. BLOCKING for wave B. "How far from my home area" offers 1, 2, 5 and 10 km, but the home area is stored as a cell about 4.5 to 5 km across, and the person's exact pick is discarded.** I measured the cells (geohash precision 5, 2026-10-17): near Bengaluru 4.76 by 4.89 km with a half diagonal of 3.4 km; near Mumbai 4.62 by 4.89 km, 3.4 km; near Delhi 4.29 by 4.89 km, 3.3 km. The picked point sat 1.9 to 3.0 km from the cell middle. So "1 km" or "2 km" from the cell middle can be a different place from 1 or 2 km from where the person lives. Options: (a, recommended) measure from the middle of the home area and say so in the row's second line ("measured from the middle of your home area, which is about 5 km across"); (b) measure from the nearest edge of the cell so a person never misses a report next to home, at the cost of more alerts; (c) store a finer cell or a rounded point for the home area (a change to migration 00008's rule and to the privacy promise, and 03.4 D-24b and 03.3 D-20 call the stored value coarse). The owner decides how much precision is promised.

**Q-C. What does the Phone notifications section say while its only rows are for the app being open?** UI-SPEC's line is "What makes your phone buzz when Pinalert is closed." (`03.4-UI-SPEC.md:573`), which is untrue until Phase 3.3. Recommendation: keep the owner's section title, use "Alerts while Pinalert is open." as the line, and let 3.3 replace it when the phone switches arrive.

2026-10-09 after the owner gave two pictures of the look they want for the Activity page. The project keeps no images, so the
pictures are described in words below. A working preview exists: `.planning/sketches/004-activity-page/` (commit 42d4a54), with

## The reference, in words

**Picture 1, the look.** A page called Activity with one line of help. Text tabs with a count chip on each: All, My posts 3,
My votes 5, My disputes 2, Resolved 8, Not resolved 4, the selected one underlined. A list of white cards with a thin coloured bar
on the left. Each card has a round coloured icon, a small grey tag (Your post, Your vote, Your dispute, System update), a bold
title, a grey line such as "You posted this report, 4 min ago, 0.6 km", a chip on the right (Critical, Medium, Low, or Resolved
and Not resolved on system rows) and an arrow. A left side menu, a top bar with a bell and an account icon, and a phone example
complete it.

**Picture 2, the tab meanings.** All: a chronological feed of your reports, votes, disputes and important updates to reports you
follow. My posts: every report you created, whether active, resolved, expired or hidden. My votes: reports you confirmed or
disputed, each showing what you did and the report's current status. Resolved and Active: quick filters for the state of reports
you posted, kept inside My posts and not as top level tabs.

## Decisions taken (the owner can veto each in one word)

- **Three tabs:** All, My posts, My votes. My posts has an All, Active, Resolved filter. Disputes sit inside My votes. Veto word:
  "six" brings back the picture's six tabs.
- **Each row keeps** the trust block, the state word, the reporter words and, on the poster's resolved row, Reopen. The picture
  leaves them out, but dropping them leaves nowhere to reopen a report, and TRUST-07 and D-20 require both trust signals on every
  report without opening anything. Veto word: "like the picture" drops them and needs a recorded amendment first.
- **Left out:** the distance on rows (the server stores no reader location), the left side menu and top bar (the app wide design
  pass, backlog 999.10, and Phase 5's Alerts work), and round icons coloured by event kind (colour stays for severity).
- **Update rows** are drawn dashed and labelled as arriving with Phase 3.2. Phase 8 adds the "Comment added" kind.

## Row anatomy as sketched

1. Round badge (the existing one: severity colour, outlined when the report is Disputed, Resolved or Expired).
2. First line: small label on the left (Your post, Your vote, Your dispute, Update), severity label on the right.
3. Title with an arrow. The first two lines are one link to `/?report=ID`. A pointer or finger can press anywhere on the card.
4. Meta line: category, then when, and for a vote or an update what happened ("you confirmed it 1 hour ago").
5. State line: the state label (none when the report is Live) with the reporter words beside it (sketch 003 variant H).
6. The shared trust block.
7. Reopen, on the poster's own Resolved row only.

Rows are neutral cards (page colour, hairline, 12px corners) with the 4px severity bar. Tabs and count chips have 8px corners
at most. The selected tab is semibold with a 1px underline, not the picture's 2px, because the design rules allow hairlines and
the 4px severity bar only. The email line leaves the page (the account menu shows it).

## What waits

## Data needed

- My posts: already served by `ActivityForAccount`.
- My votes: a new account scoped read over `votes` (latest content vote per report). The poster's own resolve and reopen belong
  under My posts. The only votes index is report first, so this read wants a new index, which is a migration the owner runs.
- Tab counts are real: reports filed, and distinct reports voted on.
- All merges posts, votes and updates, newest first. One report can appear more than once on All.

3. Should All carry a count? The picture's has none.
4. Should the tab and the filter live in the address so Back and a bookmark land on the same view?
5. Should the whole card open the report, or only the title line?
6. If the owner chooses "like the picture", amend TRUST-07, D-20 and the Activity contract tests first.

## Rules to carry into the UI contract

No pill shaped buttons, no purple gradients, no emoji icons, no long dashes, no hero text, no fake metrics, restrained motion
(site-design-rules). Colour is for severity only. Text sizes 14, 16, 18, 24, weights 400 and 600, spacing from the scale, 44px
targets. Guard the new tab and chip corners with a test: `TestNoPillShapedControls` only covers `.view-toggle`, `#toast` and the
severity slider.

# Phase 3.2 Plan 15: Notifications section of Settings Summary

Settings now has a Notifications section with the Reliable reporter switch and a 1, 2, 5 or 10 km distance that save at once through PATCH /api/me/alerts, one honest Add a home area first row without a home area, and a section that follows the home area both ways without touching settings.js.

## What was built

- **settings.html.tmpl**: `section#settings-alerts-section` (title Notifications, the line, the Q4 template comment) after About you. `div.place-box#settings-alerts-on` (hidden by the server without a home area) holds the native switch row (label only, no second line) and the distance row with a `select` whose options are ranged from `.AlertRadii` and selected from `.AlertRadiusKm`. `div.place-box#settings-alerts-off` (hidden with a home area) holds the button row with "Add a home area first" and a chevron. `p#settings-alerts-error` sits under the boxes. The Home area window gets `p#settings-home-alerts-note` before its actions.
- **settings-alerts.js**: `saveAlerts(body)` (PATCH, same origin, JSON, the sender disabled while in flight, old value back plus "Couldn't save that. Try again." on a failure, a 400, a network error or a timeout, the offline sentence and no request when `navigator.onLine === false`), `redrawAlerts(hasHome)` (swaps the two boxes, unchecks the switch and says "Home area removed. Reliable reporter alerts are off." only if it was on), a MutationObserver on `#settings-home-row` `data-value`, a second one on the Home area window `hidden` for the focus rule, and the note kept equal to the switch state.
- **places.css**: not edited. `.place-box` declares no display, so `hidden` works on the boxes.
- **settings.js, welcome.js, the welcome template**: not edited.

## Phase Boundary

A signed in person is told inside the app, while the app is open, when something changes on a report they posted or voted on, and can opt in to hear when a Reliable reporter posts nearby. A bell shows the unread count and opens a short list of the latest 10 updates with See all to the Activity page. A reader can filter the feed to Reliable reporters only. The phase also builds the account's own settings: one skippable page right after sign up (name, home area, alert choices) and a Settings page of its own (name, home area, alert choices, muted reports with Unmute, Log out of all devices, Delete my account). The phone bottom bar becomes Feed | Activity | Settings, and the phone loses its light and dark toggle and follows the device setting.

Not in this phase: phone notifications while the app is closed, the installable app, the four notification kinds and their switches, the lock screen text and the "what's happening" question on the report form (all Phase 3.3); star ratings, follows, Followers and Following lists, blocked people and the opt in public profile (Phase 9); comment events (Phase 8); email digests (backlog 999.4).

Requirements: NOTIF-01 to NOTIF-05, with the amendments listed under "Amendments to make".

### The bell and the updates list (NOTIF-01)
- **D-01 [OWNER DECIDED, item 5, informational]:** The bell sits top right on the phone, in the corner where the theme toggle was, and beside the account icon on the laptop. Why: the phone header is hidden under 900px, and the phone theme toggle is removed (D-17), which frees that corner.
- **D-02 [OWNER DECIDED, item 5]:** Tapping the bell opens a short list of the latest 10 updates, with a See all link that goes to the Activity page. This holds on phone and laptop alike. Why: one look at what changed, and the full history lives where the person's own activity already is.
- **D-03 [OWNER DECIDED, item 5]:** Updates arrive only while the app is open in this phase. Phone notifications with the app closed are Phase 3.3.
- **D-04 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, not yet put to the owner]:** The bell uses its own request (`GET /api/updates`) from its own script on the same 30 second timer as the feed, not a field on `GET /api/reports`. Research Pitfall 1: the feed poll stops on the Past tab, never runs on the Activity page, and Phase 4 pins `app.js` at two `fetch(` calls. The roadmap wording "rides on the existing 30 second refresh" is met by the same interval, not the same request. The owner has not been asked about this wording; raise it at UAT in plain words.
- **D-05 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** On the Activity page, See all lands on the All tab, where updates show as "System update" rows as drawn in the owner's Activity picture (`03.2-ACTIVITY-PAGE-REFERENCE.md`). The bell list and the Activity rows read one source.
- **D-06 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q4]:** Opening the list clears the number; each update keeps a small "new" mark until the list is closed. No "mark all read" button.
- **D-07 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q16, informational]:** The unread number is a plain dark badge, not red, because house rules keep colour for severity. The owner's Activity picture has a red dot, and the memory rule says pasted pictures are reproduced as drawn, so this is a known conflict: raise it at UAT and follow the owner's answer.
- **D-08 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, informational]:** On the phone the bell is shown on the Feed page and on the Activity and Settings pages too, so the count is visible on every tab. The bell shares the top right corner with Phase 3.1's collapsed place search; when the search opens it takes that corner, so the bell hides while the search is open (the same rule 3.1 used for the theme toggle).

### Following and muting (NOTIF-02)
- **D-09 [LOCKED EARLIER]:** A person follows a report automatically when they posted it or voted on it, and can mute any report (NOTIF-02, ROADMAP criterion 2).
- **D-10 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q13]:** Every vote kind makes you a follower, including Mark resolved and the take back (`unresolve`).
- **D-11 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q6]:** Muting a report clears its unread updates and stops new ones. The opened report shows "Turn updates back on". Muted reports are listed on the Settings page with an Unmute control (D-23).

### What an update says and when (NOTIF-03)
- **D-12 [LOCKED EARLIER]:** State changes only: the report reaches Live, is hidden by disputes, is resolved, or is within 1 hour of expiring. At most one update per report per hour. Anonymous words built from the same server trust object as the popup; never a voter, an account or a location. Every decision calls the single visibility resolver. Time events are evaluated lazily on the bell refresh, not by a scheduler (ROADMAP criterion 3, scope note 3).
- **D-13 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q5]:** People do not hear about their own actions (own Mark resolved, own confirm that makes a report Live).
- **D-14 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q7]:** The "about to fade" update goes to everyone following the report, only while it is still active, once per expiry, and says it fades in about an hour and nothing about keeping it alive (backlog 999.12).
- **D-15 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q8]:** A row shows a plain event line ("Now confirmed", "Hidden by disputes", "Marked resolved", "Fades in about an hour"), the category and severity words, how long ago, and the trust sentence for now. No description, no place name, no frozen number. Note: Phase 3.3's lock screen text adds an area or landmark name for push only (03.3-CONTEXT); bell rows keep this rule.
- **D-16 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, research Q12 and Q14]:** At most 10 stored updates per person per hour, anything beyond rolls into one "and N more" row. Updates are kept 7 days, the same as the Past tab.

### Sign up details page
- **D-19 [OWNER DECIDED, item 7, informational]:** Right after sign up the person gets one skippable page asking for name, home area (a town or district, coarse) and alert choices. There is no nickname (name replaces it), no one line bio, no phone number, no photo and no job title. Email is never shown. Names get the reserved word screening. Name and area are shown only on an opted in profile, which is Phase 9; in this phase they are stored and editable but shown to nobody else.
- **D-20 [OWNER DECIDED, item 7, informational]:** The home area is the one deliberate place the site stores a location. It is coarse, chosen by the person (never taken from GPS silently), and it is the same area Phase 3.3 uses for phone alerts. This amends ROADMAP 3.2 scope note 3 ("the server stores no user location") and 3.3 criterion 2 ("only for people who opt in"): the area is stored for anyone who fills it in on this page or in Settings, and removing it in Settings deletes it.
- **D-21 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, informational]:** In this phase the alert choices on the page are the in app ones: Reliable reporter alerts on or off and the distance (D-26). Phase 3.3 adds its phone notification switches to the same Settings page; whether the sign up page also shows them is 3.3's call.
- **D-22 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, open, informational]:** How a typed town or district becomes a stored area is not settled. "Town or district" and "about a 5 km cell" are not the same size (a district is far larger). Research default to propose: the person picks a place from the search (the geocoder, its own budget, a failed search never blocks), and the server stores the place's display name plus one coarse cell (geohash precision 5, about 5 km) at the place's centre, never the person's GPS position. Put the size question to the owner in plain words before Phase 3.3 relies on it.

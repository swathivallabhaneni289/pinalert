# Phase 3.2 Plan 12: Settings rows for muting and the account Summary

Settings now lists muted reports with a working Unmute this report, and the Account section draws on phone and laptop with Log out of all devices and Delete my account, each behind a confirm window that closes only with the X or Escape.

## What was built

- **settings.html.tmpl**: section People and reports (line "Reports you have turned updates off for.", one row Muted reports with `{{.MutedCount}}`); Account line "Log out everywhere, or delete your account.", the Log out form carries `place-row-form--phone`, plus rows Log out of all devices and Delete my account (red label class only); three windows `#settings-muted-window`, `#settings-logout-all-window`, `#settings-delete-window`. The Delete window's third paragraph is the owner's Option A sentence, one string in one `p`, with the Q2 template comment just before it. Section order: About you, People and reports, About Pinalert, Account.
- **places.css**: the 900px rule now hides `.place-row-form--phone` (the Log out row) instead of the whole section; one extra rule removes the hairline above the first drawn row when that form is hidden.
- **settings-alerts.js**: self contained IIFE with copies of settings.js's page lock, Tab trap and request helper. Muted list loads with skeleton rows, empty and error states; Unmute posts `/api/reports/{id}/unmute` (id checked against `^[1-9][0-9]{0,17}$`), removes the row, lowers the count, moves focus and says so politely; Log out of all devices and Delete my account post once, show "Logging out…" and "Deleting…", then go to the two fixed sign in addresses. Text only through textContent and createElement. settings.js is untouched.

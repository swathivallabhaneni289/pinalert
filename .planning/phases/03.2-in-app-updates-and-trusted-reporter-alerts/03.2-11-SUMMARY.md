# Phase 3.2 Plan 11: Mute control in the opened report Summary

A quiet Mute this report / Turn updates back on text button now sits before Flag in the opened report (laptop popup and phone detail pane share one card), for followers and muted reports only, and a saved vote or a mute asks the bell to refresh.

## What was built

- mute.js in flag.js's shape: button born hidden, `p.inline-error` with role alert, two listeners that stop propagation and never preventDefault. Press posts `/api/reports/{id}/mute` or `/unmute` with `{}`, same origin, 10 second limit raced with the post and its body read. Success flips every control for the report (loop over the class), says the P32-C16 line through #notice-live, keeps focus, and dispatches `pinalert:updates-refresh` once. 404, 500, 429, timeout and a dropped request show "Couldn't change this. Try again." and leave the label; offline sends nothing and shows the offline line.
- Per page maps `mutedHere` and `unmutedHere` beat a stale poll and are dropped once the server agrees.
- map.js builds the control once after the vote error and before Flag, stores it as `mute` on the card, and refreshes it after the vote block and before Flag. map.js still has 0 `fetch(`, app.js still 2.
- votes.js dispatches the refresh event on the success path of castVote's postVote only; the resolve confirm and toast are untouched.
- index.html.tmpl loads mute.js once between flag.js and map.js. Low data is untouched.

## Deviations from Plan

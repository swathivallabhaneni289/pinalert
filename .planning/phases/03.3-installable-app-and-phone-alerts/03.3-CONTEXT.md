<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A person near an emergency is told on their phone even with the site closed. The site becomes an installable web app (home screen icon, opens like an app) on Android, laptop and iPhone, and a device that allows it receives Web Push notifications for four kinds of event, each with its own switch on the Settings page. The person is asked once on first open or first sign in with the app's own Allow or Not now box, and can turn phone notifications on or off any time in Settings. A notification shows a picked "what's happening", an area or landmark name, the distance and "Not confirmed yet" where it applies, and never the reporter's own words. The report form gains a tap to pick "what's happening" question for critical and rescue needed reports.

Not in this phase: the in app bell, the home area, the Settings page itself and the Reliable alert rule (all Phase 3.2, which this phase extends); follows and profiles (Phase 9); pushing Severe or Extreme official alerts (stays in backlog 999.3, waits for Phase 5); email digests (999.4).

Requirements: PWA-03, PWA-06 (the Phase 3.2 updates part only).
</domain>

### The four notification kinds (owner Q1)
- **D-04 [OWNER DECIDED, item 12]:** Phone notifications cover four kinds:
  1. A critical report near your home area. ON by default once phone notifications are on.
  2. Changes on reports you posted or voted on (the Phase 3.2 updates). OFF by default.
  3. A Reliable reporter posting near you. OFF by default.
  4. Someone you follow posting. OFF by default.
  Each kind has its own switch. All switches live on the Settings page on both laptop and phone.
- **D-05 [OWNER DECIDED, item 12, overrides the roadmap]:** This widens push beyond ROADMAP 3.3 criterion 3 and scope note 4, which said push goes out for critical and rescue needed reports only. The owner's answer is the amendment; the ROADMAP needs the hand edit listed below.
- **D-06 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** How kinds (2), (3) and (4) plug in.
  - **Kind (2)** reuses Phase 3.2's update decision (`DecideUpdate`, the cursor rows, the hourly cap), keyed by account and report. Because the bell poll is 3.2's only producer and nothing runs while the app is closed, this phase adds a write time trigger: after a successful `CastVote`, evaluate the report's followers (skipping the voter) through the same decision code; the fading check runs on any incoming request because the host sleeps (03.2-RESEARCH "Seam for Phase 3.3"). One update row, two channels: the bell and the push record the same event.
  - **Kind (3)** reuses Phase 3.2's Reliable alert rule (`MayNotifyAboutReport(kind UpdateKind, vis, expiresAt, now)` called with `UpdateReliableNearby`, which allows a Live or Provisional report while every other kind needs Live (03.2 D-29, owner amendment 2026-10-18), plus the Reliable tag, not own, not muted, the per reporter caps), measured from the stored home area instead of the client's feed centre, because a closed app sends no coordinates.
  - **Kind (4)** waits for Phase 9's follows table. Build the switch and the kind in the closed list only if Phase 9 has landed; otherwise leave a named seam (the kind value reserved, the switch hidden) and let Phase 9 turn it on through the same pipeline. Amended 2026-10-18: there is no Settings switch for this kind (Phase 9's per person Notify me bell drives it), and its timing is the Reliable reporter rule, not "critical immediately, others once Live" (09-CONTEXT D-09): Phase 9 adds its kind to `MayNotifyAboutReport` with the same Live or Provisional allowance as `UpdateReliableNearby`.
- **D-07 [LOCKED EARLIER]:** Every push goes through the same visibility resolver the feed uses and never bypasses the provisional gate; non critical reports only once Live, except the Reliable reporter kind, which may go out while Provisional through `MayNotifyAboutReport` with `UpdateReliableNearby` (owner amendment 2026-10-18); capped per person per hour and per report; no voter, no account, no exact location (ROADMAP 3.3 criterion 3). Never notify from `Submit` directly: a new critical or rescue needed report is evaluated only through `Resolve` and the shared predicate.
- **D-08 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** Kinds (1) and (3) need a home area. With no home area set, their switches show a plain line ("Set your home area to get these") instead of a broken switch.

### When and how to ask (owner Q2)
- **D-09 [OWNER DECIDED, item 13, overrides the recommendation]:** Ask on first open or first sign in (option C), like the location prompt. Claude recommended option A; the owner chose C. This also overrides backlog 999.3's note "ask for permission right after a person posts a report, not on first visit".
- **D-10 [OWNER DECIDED, item 13]:** The ask is the app's own small box first, with Allow and Not now. Allow then fires the browser's own prompt, because browsers need a tap and Chrome quietens prompts fired on page load. Not now does not use up the browser permission.
- **D-11 [OWNER DECIDED, item 13]:** On an iPhone that has not added the site to the home screen, the box shows the "Add to home screen first" guide instead (Claude's recommendation, the owner agreed).
- **D-12 [OWNER DECIDED, item 13]:** Settings has an on or off switch for this device, usable any time, labelled **"On this device"** (renamed from "Phone notifications" by the owner on 2026-10-18, "rename it", so it does not repeat the section name Notifications; one label for phone and laptop). With it off, the in app bell from 3.2 still shows everything while the app is open. A refused or unsupported device sees a plain message instead of a broken switch (ROADMAP 3.3 criterion 5).
- **D-13 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** The box is shown once per device; after Not now it is not shown again on its own, and the Settings switch is the way back. The "On this device" switch belongs to this device (a push subscription is per device), while the four kind switches belong to the account. Whether the 3.2 sign up page also shows the phone switches is the planner's call; Settings must.

### Lock screen text (owner Q3)
- **D-14 [OWNER DECIDED, item 15]:** The owner rejected a bare "Rescue needed" as unclear. A notification shows: a picked "what's happening" (water rising, fire, building collapsed, landslide, medical emergency, people trapped, other), the area or landmark name (never a street or house number), the distance, and "Not confirmed yet" when the report is not Live by confirmation. Never the reporter's own words, names, phone numbers or address.
- **D-15 [OWNER DECIDED, item 15]:** The report form gets a new tap to pick "what's happening" question, shown only for critical reports. Phase 3.3 builds it. The list is closed: the seven values above.
- **D-16 [OWNER DECIDED, item 15]:** The spot gets an area or landmark name by a reverse lookup at post time, stored with the report.
- **D-17 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** "Not confirmed yet" is decided from the resolver's reason, not from a second copy of the critical rule: a critical or rescue needed report comes back Live with the reason `critical_bypasses_gates`, and that reason, not the severity, triggers the words. `criticalBypass` stays unexported.
- **D-18 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** The question is required for critical reports (one tap, Other is allowed), because the owner rejected the bare category word. If it is somehow absent (older reports), the notification uses the category and severity words plus "Not confirmed yet".
- **D-19 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** The reverse lookup shares the geocoder's 1 request per second process budget (Phase 7 D-02) and must never block or slow posting (Phase 7 D-04): it runs after the report is saved, and a failed or slow lookup leaves the name empty, in which case the notification shows the distance only. Only the coarse area or landmark name is kept (no road, house number or postcode), and it is not shown in the feed by this phase.
- **D-20 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** Distance is measured from the home area's stored cell, rounded (about 1 km steps), never to the metre. Kinds (2) and (4) use the same text rule. Bell rows in 3.2 keep their no place rule; the area name is for push only.

### The rough area
- **D-21 [OWNER DECIDED, item 7]:** The rough area is the home area from Phase 3.2's sign up page and Settings (town or district, coarse, chosen by the person). There is no separate area picker in this phase. Removing the home area in Settings stops kinds (1) and (3) and deletes the stored area. The open size question (town or district against about 5 km) is recorded in 03.2-CONTEXT D-22 and must be settled before this phase builds "near your home area".
- **D-22 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** "Near your home area" means the home cell and its eight neighbours (geohash precision 5), checked from the report's own geohash, so the server never needs the person's position.

### Conflicts and risks the plans must handle
- **Kind (3) widens the farmable Reliable rule to push.** ROADMAP 3.2 scope note 2 and 03.2-RESEARCH Pitfall 7 make TRUSTX-03 burst detection a hard dependency before the Reliable rule goes beyond the in app bell. Kind (3) is off by default, which softens the risk but does not remove it. The planner must either build the per reporter ceilings and "Not confirmed yet" wording and record the open TRUSTX-03 gap in `docs/TRUST-MODEL.md`, or hold kind (3) until TRUSTX-03 exists. Put the choice to the owner in plain words.
- **The area name conflicts with "no exact location".** ROADMAP 3.3 criterion 3 and the 3.2 research rule "never a place name" are amended by item 15 for push only. A known home area plus a distance plus a landmark narrows the spot, so keep the name coarse (D-19) and the distance rounded (D-20), and never include it in bell rows or the feed.
- **Finished Rescue reports are coarsened** to about 1 km with no description (03.1 D-23). A notification is only for live reports, but the stored area name must follow the same rule wherever a finished report is shown.
- **The home area size question** (03.2-CONTEXT D-22) blocks "near your home area".
- **Volume.** Cap per person per hour and per report; a dispute flip flop must not reach a lock screen more than the 3.2 cursor allows.
- **Secrets and HTTPS.** VAPID keys stay out of the repo; push needs HTTPS in production, so local checks run on localhost.
- **Delete my account and Log out of all devices** (3.2) must also delete every push subscription of the account.
- **Testing:** never run against `pinalert` or `pinalert_test`; use a disposable database and tell every subagent. Never ports 8080 or 8090. No Python. Phone first: 320, 360 and 390 px first. No long dashes in any notification text (add a Go test like 3.2's copy test).

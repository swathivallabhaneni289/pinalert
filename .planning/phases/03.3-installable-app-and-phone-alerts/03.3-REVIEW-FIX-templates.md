## T-03 (low): fixed

**Commit:** 92930c3
**Files:** `web/static/css/places.css`, `web/review33_templates_fix_contract_test.go`
**Change:** removed `cursor: pointer` from the bare `.place-row` rule and put it on `button.place-row, a.place-row`. **Test:** `TestPlainSettingsRowsHaveNoPointerCursor`. It failed before (verified by restoring the old CSS).

## T-04 (low): fixed

**Commit:** 68efcea
**Files:** `web/templates/welcome.html.tmpl`, `web/static/css/welcome.css`, `web/static/js/welcome.js`, `web/welcome_alerts_test.mjs`, `web/welcome_flow_test.mjs`, `web/welcome_contract_test.go`, `web/review33_templates_fix_contract_test.go`

**Tests:**

- New `TestWelcomeStatusIsAlwaysPresentLiveRegion` pins the template tag, the `:empty` rule and that welcome.js never calls hideLine or showLine on it.
- Amended with dated notes: the stub status node in welcome_alerts_test.mjs and welcome_flow_test.mjs is no longer hidden; two assertions in welcome_alerts_test.mjs check the text instead of `hidden`; welcome_contract_test.go pins `id="welcome-alerts-status" role="status"></p>` instead of the `hidden` form.

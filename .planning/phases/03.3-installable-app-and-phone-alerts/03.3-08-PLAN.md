## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser to /api/me/push routes | Endpoint and keys come from the client and may be forged or hostile |
| account to account | A shared device carries one endpoint across sign ins |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03.3-01 | Tampering (SSRF) | Register | high | mitigate | push.EndpointPolicy and CheckKeys before storing; unknown hosts refused with a plain message; the send path checks again (plan 02, plan 11) |
| T-03.3-04 | Tampering (CSRF) / Denial of service | the three routes | medium | mitigate | Inside the verified account gate; SameSite Lax cookie (DEC-L); application/json only; strict decoding; 4 KB cap; per account limiters; TestPushRoutesRateLimited, TestPushRoutesStrictJSON |
| T-03.3-10 | Spoofing / Information disclosure | shared device | medium | mitigate | Upsert moves ownership; status and unsubscribe act on the caller's rows only; TestPushSubscriptionMovesBetweenAccounts |
| T-03.3-02 | Information disclosure | logs and responses | high | mitigate | Endpoint only in request bodies, never returned or logged; TestPushSubsNeverLogsEndpoint and the e2e body check |
| T-03.3-20 | Denial of service | device rows | low | mitigate | Trim to 8 devices per account on every register |

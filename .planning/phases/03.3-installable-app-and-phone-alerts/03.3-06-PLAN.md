  <behavior>
    - At load, with a secure context and navigator.serviceWorker, register is called once with '/sw.js' and {scope: '/', updateViaCache: 'none'}; without serviceWorker nothing throws and nothing is registered.
    - baseState: no secure context, or no serviceWorker, PushManager or Notification, on a non Apple device gives 'unsupported'; a user agent with iPhone or iPad, or Macintosh with navigator.maxTouchPoints above 1, while navigator.standalone is not true and matchMedia('(display-mode: standalone)') does not match, gives 'needs-install' (even though PushManager is missing in that tab); Notification.permission 'denied' gives 'blocked'; otherwise 'maybe'.
    - deviceState(key) with an empty key gives 'unsupported'; with 'maybe', permission 'granted' and a subscription whose status call answers registered true gives 'on'; registered false, no subscription or permission 'default' gives 'ready'; a status call that fails gives 'ready'.
    - enable(key) with permission 'default': Notification.requestPermission is called synchronously, before enable returns its promise and before any other awaited call (the fake records the call order and the test checks it happened in the same tick); 'denied' gives {ok: false, state: 'blocked', reason: 'refused'} and nothing is posted; 'granted' then serviceWorker.ready, pushManager.subscribe({userVisibleOnly: true, applicationServerKey: the 65 key bytes}) and a POST of subscription.toJSON() to /api/me/push/subscription with credentials same-origin and Content-Type application/json; a 204 gives {ok: true, state: 'on'} and stores the intent flag; navigator.onLine false gives reason 'offline' with no request; a 401 gives reason 'session'; any other failure gives reason 'failed'.
    - enable(key) with permission already 'granted' does not call requestPermission.
    - disable(): POST {"endpoint": ...} to /api/me/push/unsubscribe, then subscription.unsubscribe(); success gives {ok: true, state: 'ready'} and clears the intent flag; a failed server call gives reason 'failed' and keeps the browser subscription.
    - reconcile(key): permission granted, a local subscription, the status answers registered false and the intent flag is set: the subscription is posted again quietly and the result state is 'on'; without the intent flag nothing is posted.
    - The endpoint never appears in any request address, only in JSON bodies.
    - notify.js adds no listener on window or document (it reacts only to calls from welcome.js and settings-alerts.js).
  </behavior>

### Owner design rules (binding, quoted)

- Not "vibe coded": no purple gradients or gradient heavy backgrounds, no pill shaped buttons (modest radius rounded rectangles are fine), no fake metrics or testimonials, no hero text, no emoji used as icons (real SVG icons or plain text), no em dashes or en dashes in any user visible text, no scroll triggered animations (short restrained hover, press, focus and state transitions only).
- Same features on phone and laptop; only placement differs. Phone first: judge at 320, 360 and 390 px wide first, then laptop.
- No hint text under simple options. Plain everyday words; the owner dislikes long text ("neat, not too much text, not AI coded, not beginner level, clean").
- One way to close each window (the X at the top; no Cancel plus X). Each thing lives in one place; before adding a control, check it does not already exist elsewhere.
- A chosen option must look chosen (soft fill in its own colour), never a black outline or a focus ring look.
- Nothing a user needs may sit off the edge of the screen. Touch targets at least 44px.
- Light and dark themes both; phones follow the device setting.
- Do not save images of any kind in the repo.
- **No buzzes anywhere (owner, 2026-10-18).** Every notification is built as one that makes no noise and no vibration. **No user visible string, template, script comment shown to users, or notification text may promise or mention** the words in `BANNED_NOISE_WORDS`: buzz, buzzes, vibrate, vibration, sound, ring, silent, silently, quiet, quietly, hear. (A promise about silence is also a promise iOS can break, because on iPhone the phone's own settings decide the sound and the site can only ask for none.) The only place these ideas may appear is `docs/` and code comments, as a note that the site asks for no sound and no vibration and cannot force it.

### 1c. What the buttons do

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Allow alerts (permission not asked yet) | Disable both buttons and set `aria-busy="true"` on the step, run the browser's own prompt (this tap is the gesture it needs), then subscribe this device and register the subscription with the server for this account. ## Surface 3: The notification (lock screen text)

A notification is system UI. This section is its text, its fields and its tap behaviour. It is built by **one Go function** that takes only the closed inputs below and has no parameter for the report's description, name, email, phone number or address, so the reporter's words can never reach a lock screen.

### 3a. Inputs the text may use (owner, D-14 to D-20)

| Input | Rule |
|-------|------|
| What is happening (the pick) | one of the seven: Water rising, People trapped, Fire, Building collapsed, Landslide, Medical emergency, Other. **Critical reports only.** |
| Category and severity words | the server's closed words (Flood, Traffic, Shelter open, Power outage, ...; Low, Medium, Critical). Used when there is no pick (older reports, non Critical reports). |
| Area or landmark name | the coarse name stored at post time. Never a street or house number. Cut at **28 characters** at a word end, ending in "…" if cut. May be empty. |
| Distance | whole kilometres from the person's **home area** (D-20), never to the metre: "1 km", "2 km", ... ; below 1 km reads "Under 1 km". Left out when the person has no home area. |
| "Not confirmed yet" | only when the resolver's reason is `critical_bypasses_gates` for a Critical report, or the report is Provisional for the Reliable kind (D-17). Never decided by a second copy of the critical rule. |

Never any of: the reporter's text, a name, a phone number, an address, coordinates, a vote count, an account.

### 3b. The title and body of each kind

Titles are at most about 50 characters; bodies at most about 120. No dash of any kind. Samples use a landmark.

| Kind | Title | Body | Sample |
|------|-------|------|--------|
| **Critical report near my home area** (1) | `{Pick} near {Area}` | `{Distance} from your home area. Not confirmed yet.` | **Water rising near Central Market** / 2 km from your home area. Not confirmed yet. |
| Critical, no area name | `{Pick}` | `{Distance} from your home area. Not confirmed yet.` | **Fire** / Under 1 km from your home area. Not confirmed yet. |
| Critical, no pick (older report) | `{Category}, Critical near {Area}` | as above | **Flood, Critical near Central Market** / 3 km from your home area. Not confirmed yet. |
| **Reliable reporter posts near me** (4) | `{Category} near {Area}` | `From a Reliable reporter. {Distance} from your home area. Not confirmed yet.` | **Power outage near Old Bridge** / From a Reliable reporter. 4 km from your home area. Not confirmed yet. |
| Reliable, **updated in place** once people nearby confirm it | unchanged | `Confirmed by people nearby. {Distance} from your home area.` | **Power outage near Old Bridge** / Confirmed by people nearby. 4 km from your home area. |
| Critical, **updated in place** once people nearby confirm it (O-1 a, decided 2026-10-18) | unchanged | `Confirmed by people nearby. {Distance} from your home area.` | **Water rising near Central Market** / Confirmed by people nearby. 2 km from your home area. |
| **Changes to reports I posted or voted on** (3) | `{Whose} {Event}` where Whose is "Your report" or "A report you voted on" and Event is "is now confirmed", "is now disputed", "was marked resolved" or "fades in about an hour" | `{Pick, or Category and Severity}. {Area}, {Distance} from your home area.` (parts left out when unknown) | **Your report is now confirmed** / Flood, Critical. The four event words are 3.2's event lines (P32-C4) carried to the lock screen: "Now confirmed", "Now disputed", "Marked resolved", "Fades in about an hour". "Not confirmed yet" is never added to a Changes notification.

### 3c. Rules every notification follows

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| One per report | `tag = report-{id}`. A second push for the same report replaces the first. Chrome requires every push to show something (`userVisibleOnly`), so the in place update is a **replacement under the same tag**, not a data only message. `renotify` is false, so the update does not alert again. |
| One per report per device | when several kinds match one report for one person, the order is fixed: **Critical, then Changes, then Reliable**; only the first matching kind is sent. |

## Surface 6: The app icon

**Rule:** no image file is saved in the repo. The mark is defined once, on a **512 by 512 grid**, as numbers shared by two Go functions: one that writes the SVG markup, one that rasterises PNGs with the standard library only (`image`, `image/color`, `image/png`; per pixel geometry tests with 4 by 4 supersampling for smooth edges, no third party renderer). The PNGs are built once at first request, held in memory and served with a long cache lifetime and the asset version.

### 6a. The mark: a map pin with an exclamation cut out

Monochrome, flat, no gradient, no shadow, no text, no emoji. It reads as a pin (the product's name) carrying an alert.

| Shape | Geometry on the 512 grid | Fill |
|-------|--------------------------|------|
| Tile (background) | square 0,0 to 512,512; for the **any** icons, corner radius **96**; for **maskable** and **apple-touch** the full bleed square (radius 0, the system masks it) | `#1A1D21` (`--color-notice-bg`) |
| Pin head | circle centre (256, 216), radius 120 | `#FFFFFF` (`--color-notice-text`) |
| Pin point | triangle (256, 412), (161, 290), (351, 290). These are the tangent points of the circle from the tip, so head and point join as one drop with no kink | `#FFFFFF` |
| Exclamation bar | rounded rectangle x 236, y 152, width 40, height 78, corner radius 20 | `#1A1D21` (cut out of the pin) |
| Exclamation dot | circle centre (256, 274), radius 22 | `#1A1D21` (cut out of the pin) |

The pin spans x 136 to 376 and y 96 to 412. Its farthest point from the tile centre (256, 256) is 160 units.

### 6b. Sizes, purposes and safe zone

| File (served by Go) | Size | Purpose | Tile | Notes |
|---------------------|------|---------|------|-------|
| `/icons/app.svg` | any | favicon, desktop, `sizes any` | rounded, transparent corners | markup from the same numbers |
| `/icons/app-192.png` | 192 | manifest `any`, notification `icon` | rounded, **transparent corners** | |
| `/icons/app-512.png` | 512 | manifest `any`, splash | rounded, transparent corners | |
| `/icons/app-maskable-512.png` | 512 | manifest `maskable` | **full bleed, opaque** | the mark must stay inside a circle of **radius 0.4 times the size (204.8 px)** about the centre. At 160 units the mark uses 31 percent of the size, so any platform mask (circle, squircle, rounded square) keeps it whole. |
| `/icons/apple-touch-icon.png` | 180 | iPhone and iPad Home Screen | **full bleed, opaque, no transparent pixel** (iOS fills transparency with black and rounds the corners itself) | the same artwork as the maskable icon, scaled to 180 |
| `/icons/badge-96.png` | 96 | notification `badge` (Android status bar) | **none: alpha only**, the pin drawn opaque white with the exclamation as transparent holes, everything else transparent | cropped to the pin: the 364 by 364 unit square with corner at (74, 72) in the 512 grid, so the pin fills the badge with a small margin |

The `/icons/apple-touch-icon.png` address (also answered at `/apple-touch-icon.png`) is the one Safari looks for without a link tag.

**Stored PNG: not unavoidable.** Chrome's install check and iOS both need real PNG responses, but a response is not a stored file: the Go stdlib is enough to draw this geometry, so nothing is saved in the repo.

## Interaction and Accessibility

- **Names:** the switch is named by its row label ("On this device", "Critical reports near my home area", "Changes to reports I posted or voted on"); the guide window by its title; the example by "Example notification". The step carries `aria-busy` while working. The guide is an ordered list, so a screen reader reads "1 of 3".
- **State is never colour alone:** a switch has its native on and off look, a blocked row has words, a message row has words.
- **Focus:** step 5 title on arrival; the Back control and both buttons keep the global ring; the guide window title on open; after a browser prompt or a failed save, focus stays on the control the person used.
- **Live regions:** `#notice-live` (polite) for "Alerts are on/off for this device"; errors are `role="alert"`; the refusal line in step 5 is `role="status"`.
- **Touch targets:** every control is at least 44px; the buttons in the flow are 48px (built).
- **Motion:** none added. The step change uses the flow's built behaviour. No scroll triggered animation.
- **Text size:** every block wraps; no horizontal scroll at 320 px and 200 percent text.
- **Forced colours:** cards and the guide box keep a visible border; nothing relies on fill alone.
- **Order of keys:** Escape closes the guide window and returns focus to the row; the first-time flow itself ignores Escape (03.4).
- **Security:** every string from the server (area name, pick, category, event) enters a notification through the Go builder's closed lists, never as markup; in the page, text is written with `textContent`, and the settings state comes from the server, never from the local subscription alone.

# Phase 3.3 Plan 16: Critical alert and the hourly limit, Apple first push Summary

A Critical alert near the home area is never held by the hourly limit but still counts toward it, and an Apple endpoint gets only the first push about a report, both through two named pure functions in `pushworker.go`.

## What changed

- `heldByHourlyCap` (pushworker.go): false for Critical and confirmed updates, otherwise `sentInHour >= PushPerAccountPerHour`. `sendRow` step 6 still reads `CountAccountPushesInWindow` for every kind except a confirmed update (so Critical is counted), then skips with `cap_account_hour` only when `heldByHourlyCap(run.kind, sent)` is true.
- `deviceSkipsRepeatPush` (pushworker.go): `alreadyPushed && push.IsAppleHost(host)`. `sendRow` step 7 reads `AccountPushSentAboutReport` once, only when at least one policy checked device is an Apple one and the kind is not a confirmed update, then drops each device the helper names. When none is left the existing `no_device` skip runs. One log line per left out device with numeric ids and the closed word `apple_repeat`.
- New query `AccountPushSentAboutReport` (sent rows of any kind for the account and report), regenerated with sqlc 1.31.1; only `push.sql.go` changed under `internal/store/sqlc/`.
- Comments fixed: the pushworker.go header and the `pushkinds.go` caps comments no longer say the bell holds everything or that Critical is dropped by the cap. No constant changed. No migration.
- `TestPhoneAlertCapShowsNothingExtra` now shows five Critical notes (every one kind critical, no summary), then a Reliable reporter's report in the same hour held back as `cap_account_hour` with nothing extra shown.
- `docs/TRUST-MODEL.md` "Phone alerts": the confirmed update paragraph names `deviceSkipsRepeatPush` and `TestWorkerAppleFirstPushOnly`; the caps paragraph names `heldByHourlyCap` and `TestWorkerCriticalNeverHeldButCounted` and states the new cap order.

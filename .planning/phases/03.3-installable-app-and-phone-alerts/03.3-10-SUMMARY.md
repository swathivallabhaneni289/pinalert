# Phase 3.3 Plan 10: Sign out stops phone alerts Summary

**Every way out of an account now ends phone notifications for the devices it ends: plain Log out removes this session's subscriptions, Log out of all devices removes the account's, and Delete my account removes them and the queued push rows in its one transaction.**

## What was built

- `internal/api/handlers/auth.go`: `Logout(mgr *session.Manager, subs *service.PushSubscriptionService)`. Before `mgr.ClearCookie` it reads the session id with `session.FromContext` and, when present and `subs` is not nil, calls `subs.DeleteForSession`. An error is logged as `handlers: Logout: deleting this device's push subscriptions: <scrubbed error>` (SQLSTATE only, never an endpoint) and the log out proceeds. Comment extended with decision 11, UI-SPEC O-3 and DEC-K. The 303 to `/login` and `Cache-Control: no-store` are unchanged.
- `internal/api/router.go`: only the Logout route line changed (plus a two line comment): `handlers.Logout(deps.Session, pushSubs)`.
- `internal/service/sessions.go`: `SessionQuerier` gains `DeleteAccountPushSubscriptions`; `LogOutAllDevices` calls it right after `RevokeAccountSessions` with the same account id.
- `internal/service/accountdelete.go`: `AccountDeleteQuerier` gains `DeleteAccountPushOutbox` and `DeleteAccountPushSubscriptions`; `DeleteAccount` calls them (outbox first) after the four personal row deletes and before `RevokeAccountSessions`, inside the transaction function, so a failure at either commits nothing.
- Tests: `TestLogOutAllDevicesDeletesPushSubscriptions` (sessions_test.go); `TestDeleteAccountOrder`, `TestDeleteAccountRunsInOneTx` (which fails each step in turn, so the two new steps are covered) and `TestDeleteAccountTouchesNoOtherPersonsData` amended to the eleven step order with the dated note "Amended 2026-10-18 by Phase 3.3 plan 10 (decision 11, UI-SPEC O-3)"; `push_signout_e2e_test.go` with `TestPushSubscriptionsRemovedOn` (sub tests `plain log out`, `log out of all devices`, `delete my account`), `TestLogoutStillWorksWhenDeleteFails` and `TestLogoutWithoutSubscriptionServiceStillWorks`.

The e2e plain log out case registers a device from each of two sessions of one account, logs the first out, and checks that its row is gone, the other session's row stays, the answer is the usual 303 to `/login`, and the logged out session row is still bound to its account (DEC-K). The delete case checks that another account's subscription and outbox rows stay.

## Note on pinalert.pushIntent

This plan does not touch notify.js or any JavaScript, so it does not clear the `pinalert.pushIntent` flag on log out. The server now deletes the rows, so after a plain log out the same account signing in again on that browser will still hold its intent flag and reconcile re-registers it (intended: the person opted in on their own device). A different account on a shared phone does not match the page's `data-account-id`, so reconcile does not re-register for them. Whether to also clear the flag client side on log out is left to a later plan; the server side privacy guarantee does not depend on it.

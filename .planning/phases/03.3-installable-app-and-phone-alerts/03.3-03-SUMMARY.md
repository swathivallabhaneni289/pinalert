## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Spec inconsistency] PushTitleMaxRunes is 64, not 50**
- **Found during:** writing TestPushTextBudgets.
- **Issue:** the plan fixes the constant at 50 and says every title must fit with the longest pick, the longest category and a 28 rune area. That cannot hold: "Medical emergency near " plus 28 runes is 51, and the no pick Critical fallback "Storm/Cyclone damage, Critical near " plus 28 runes is 64. UI-SPEC itself says "about 50".
- **Fix:** the constant is 64 with a comment explaining the arithmetic; the test enforces it over the whole grid. Body budget stays 120 (longest body is far under it). If the owner wants a hard 50, the area in the title would have to be cut shorter than 28; that is a copy decision for the owner, not made here.
- **Files:** internal/service/pushcopy.go. **Commit:** a3cd929.

**2. [Interpretation] AreaWords limit includes the ellipsis**
- The plan says "cut at the last word end at or before 28 runes" and also "a 40 rune name with no space is cut at 27 runes plus the ellipsis". I read both as: the result is at most 28 runes in total. A name of 28 runes or fewer is untouched.

**3. [Additions beyond the listed tests]** TestBuildPushTextRefuses (the ok false rows), TestCriticalKindTakesNoCategoryInput (the plan check warning 1 row) and TestPushWordConstants were added as separate tests.

Otherwise the plan was executed as written. No auth gates.

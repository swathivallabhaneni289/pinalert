## Sentence map

T is `IndependentAgreementThreshold` (2), K the raised bar, R the recent count, W the window phrase, MIN `ReliabilityMinJudged` (5), C corroborated, J judged. Labels are unchanged.

| Id | Line, case | Old | New |
|----|------------|-----|-----|
| W1 | Lead, Provisional, nobody confirmed | Needs {T} confirmations to count as confirmed. | Needs {T} people at different spots to confirm it. |
| W2 | Lead, Provisional, need 1 | (same as W1) | Needs 1 more person at a different spot to confirm it. |
| W2b | Lead, Provisional, need 2 to T minus 1 | (same as W1) | Needs {need} more people at different spots to confirm it. (not reached while T is 2) |
| W3 | Lead, expired | Expired, so it can no longer be confirmed. | unchanged |
| W4 | Confirmed, N 0 | No one has confirmed this yet. Counts places, not votes. | No one has confirmed this yet. |
| W5 | Confirmed, N 1 | 1 place confirmed this. Counts places, not votes. | 1 person confirmed this. |
| W6 | Confirmed, N 2 or more | {N} different places confirmed this. Counts places, not votes. | Confirmed by {N} people at different spots. |
| W7 | Disputed, M 0 | No one nearby has disputed this. | No one has disputed this. |
| W8 | Disputed, M 1 | 1 place disputed this. | 1 person nearby disputed this. |
| W9 | Disputed, M 2 or more | {M} different places disputed this. | {M} people at different spots disputed this. |
| W10 | Report up to date | {R} places confirmed this in the last {W}. | {R} confirmations in the last {W}. (R 1: 1 confirmation) |
| W11 | Report getting old | Fewer than {T} places confirmed it in the last {W}. | Fewer than {T} confirmations in the last {W}. |
| W12 | Report needs re-confirming | Gone quiet. People nearby can confirm it again. | unchanged |
| W13 | Dispute raised the bar | Disputed nearby, so it needs {K} places in the last {W}. | Disputed nearby, so it needs {K} confirmations in the last {W}. |
| W14 | Too early to tell | Fewer than {T} places have confirmed this. | Fewer than {T} people have confirmed this yet. |
| W15 | Still current score (popup) | Score {S} out of 100, based on recent confirmations. | Score {S} out of 100. |
| W16 | Rated reporter | {C} of {J} earlier reports were confirmed by people nearby. | {C} of their last {J} reports were confirmed. (C 1: was confirmed) |
| W17 | New reporter | A rating needs {MIN} reports with a clear result. They have {J} so far. | Not rated yet. It needs {MIN} reports with a clear result. |
| W18 | Reporter score (popup) | Score {S} out of 100, based on how often their reports were confirmed. | Score {S} out of 100. |

Known choices that differ from the literal templates of the request, for the owner: W16 reads "was" when C is 1 and "report" when J is 1 (grammar; J is 5 or more for a rated reporter, so only the C of 1 form can be seen); W2b exists so a larger threshold never produces a wrong number.

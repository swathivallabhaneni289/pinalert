## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading the feed during an emergency, **I want to** see how many different nearby
people confirmed or disputed each report, and whether it is still current and its reporter reliable,
**so that** a few accounts cannot make a weak report look well corroborated.

<objective>
Make geohash precision 7 a tested, documented choice instead of an interim value (D-03, closing
02-RESEARCH assumption A3), and write the first version of the trust model document that records
the 1 km voting rule and its grandfathered votes (D-01 docs).

plan a test proves how many distinct places a crowd inside a 300 m incident circle can ever produce

`docs/TRUST-MODEL.md` can see the derivation, the 1 km rule and exactly what the model cannot
prevent. Covers TRUST-05 ("a deliberately chosen, documented geohash precision") and V-10.

`## Who can vote: the 1 km rule` (D-01): one named constant, `MaxVoteDistanceKm = 1.0` in `internal/service/geo.go`.

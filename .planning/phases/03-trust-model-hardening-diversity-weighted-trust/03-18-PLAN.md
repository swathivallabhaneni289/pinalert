## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading the feed during an emergency, **I want to** see how many different nearby
people confirmed or disputed each report, and whether it is still current and its reporter reliable,
**so that** a few accounts cannot make a weak report look well corroborated.

<objective>
Once the viewer's location is known, Confirm, Dispute and Mark resolved on a report farther than the
server's radius are marked too far with `aria-disabled` and an always visible sentence built from the
page's radius attribute, cleared when the fix is stale or near, while the reporter's own Mark resolved
stays enabled and the server stays the gatekeeper. This delivers D-22 in full as amended by A8 and
RR-3, with D-01 (the server refusal stays the real check) and D-02 (the fix freshness that the hint
bound relies on), and the validation rows V-34 and V-36.

Purpose: this is the hint half of the outline's S5 "reasons and hint" slice. After 03-04 a far voter
learns why only after tapping, from the 403 sentence. After this plan a far viewer sees before tapping
that the buttons are off and reads why as plain text under them.

Increments, task by task:
- After Task 1: the real page shell rendered by `Page` carries `data-vote-radius-km="1"` sourced from
  `service.MaxVoteDistanceKm`, which a handler test reads from the rendered HTML and from the wiring in
  `cmd/server/main.go`.
- After Task 2: a real viewer more than 1 km from a report, with a fix younger than 120 seconds, sees
  Confirm, Dispute and Mark resolved marked too far with the sentence under them, and the marks clear
  when they move near or the fix ages out.

Output: the radius field and attribute, the hint functions in `votes.js`, and three new tests
(`TestPageShellCarriesVoteRadius`, `TestVoteHint`, `TestVoteRadiusReadWithoutConfig`) plus
`TestVoteHintSentenceMatchesServer`.

No database: every gate in this plan is a static or rendering test with no Postgres. The plan database
name p3_0318 is reserved but never created.
</objective>

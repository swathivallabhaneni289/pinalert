## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading the feed during an emergency, **I want to** see how many different nearby people confirmed or disputed each report, and whether it is still current and its reporter reliable, **so that** a few accounts cannot make a weak report look well corroborated.

<objective>
Make a reporter's history readable in one bounded, index backed query. After this plan, `sqlcgen.Queries.ReporterHistoryReports` returns, for a batch of reporter account ids, each account's latest expired reports across all of its sessions: at most `RowCap` per account (30 in production, D-13), only those that expired at or before `Now` and after `WindowStart` (90 days earlier, D-13 and R-05), keyed by the verified account through `sessions.account_id` (D-10). Migration 00005 adds `idx_reports_session_expires`, and an EXPLAIN over 48,000 seeded reports proves the planner uses it by name.

Purpose: this is the second step of slice S4 "source reliable" in the outline's MVP framing. Plan 03-03 decides outcomes and ratings from pure inputs, this plan makes the input readable, plan 03-10 composes the rows into the reporter signal, and plan 03-12 calls this query once per read path. TRUST-07's "is this source reliable" signal cannot exist until a reporter's capped, windowed history can be read across all of their devices without a per report query.

Increments, task by task (vertical, each ends at something a real test observes):
- After Task 1: a real Postgres test reads a reporter's capped, windowed history across all of their sessions, sees the window edges, the per account cap and the tie break hold, and sees that a session with no account and an account that was not asked for contribute nothing.
- After Task 2: the same read is index backed. The migration is embedded and applied, the index is in `pg_indexes`, and EXPLAIN ANALYZE names `idx_reports_session_expires` at 10 and at 200 requested accounts.

Output: new `internal/store/queries/reliability.sql`, generated `internal/store/sqlc/reliability.sql.go`, new `internal/store/reliability_test.go`, new `internal/testutil/history.go`, new migration `00005_add_reports_session_expires_index.sql`, and an extended `TestMigrationsEmbedded`.
</objective>

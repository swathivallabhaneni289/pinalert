## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading the feed during an emergency, **I want to** see how many different nearby people confirmed or disputed each report, and whether it is still current and its reporter reliable, **so that** a few accounts cannot make a weak report look well corroborated.

<objective>
A reader sees the trust block on every feed row and map popup. Two counters are always shown. The still current word and the reporter tag appear whenever the server sends them. Scores show only in the popup. One toggle opens a panel of server sentences, and the row fade takes the worse of the time stage and the server stage. This covers D-07, D-09, D-14, D-16, D-17, D-18, D-19 as amended by A5, D-20, R-02, R-03 and R-07, with A3, A4, A5, A6, A22, A23 (listener and row branch), A33, A39 (clock), A47, A48, A50 (popup render), A53, A61 and A69, and V-37, V-38 (feed and popup), V-39 and V-40.

Purpose: this is the third step of the outline's S2 "counters" slice and the panel half of S5. After 03-07, `trust.confirmed_places` is on the wire. After this plan a reader sees `N confirmed` and `M disputed` from distinct places on the row and the popup, and can open the panel to read why. The block renders each signal the server sends and nothing for an absent one, so when 03-14 adds currency and reporter to the wire, both signals appear on real data with no further UI change.

Output: the new `web/static/js/trust.js`, `web/static/icons/clock.svg` and `web/trust_contract_test.go`; edits to `feed.js`, `map.js`, `index.html.tmpl`, `trust.css` and `css_contract_test.go`.
</objective>

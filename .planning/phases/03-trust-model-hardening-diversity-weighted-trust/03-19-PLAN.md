## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading the feed during an emergency, **I want to** see how many different nearby people confirmed or disputed each report, and whether it is still current and its reporter reliable, **so that** a few accounts cannot make a weak report look well corroborated.

<objective>
A keyboard or screen reader user can open a pin with Enter or Space, land in a named popup dialog, close the trust panel and then the popup with one Escape per level by surface ownership, return to the row or marker that opened it, and keep focus through polls that remove or reorder reports. Autopan clears the fixed controls and never animates under reduced motion. This covers A23 (popup branch), A45 (pin name), A49, A51, A59, A60, A65 (JS and token test), A67, A68, A70, A71 and A74, and extends V-39 (`TestTrustDisclosure`).

Purpose: this is the second and last step of the outline's "Popup card" slice. After 03-17 a pin opens the neutral card that keeps its state across polls. After this plan the same card, with its trust block counters, signals and panel (TRUST-05, TRUST-07), is fully usable from the keyboard and a screen reader, and focus is never stranded on the page body by a close, a poll or a re-measure.

Output: edits to `web/static/js/map.js`, `web/static/js/feed.js` and `web/static/js/trust.js`; new tests in `web/popup_contract_test.go` (created by 03-17) and an extended `TestTrustDisclosure` in `web/trust_contract_test.go` (created by 03-13).
</objective>

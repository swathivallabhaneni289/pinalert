## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading the feed during an emergency, **I want to** see how many different nearby people confirmed or disputed each report, and whether it is still current and its reporter reliable, **so that** a few accounts cannot make a weak report look well corroborated.

<objective>
Give every trust object the still current signal (TRUST-07, first half). After this plan, one pure function `buildCurrency` turns a report's confirm and dispute vote times, its lifetime, its visibility, its metadata and an injected `now` into a `CurrencySignal`:
- a band: `up_to_date`, `getting_old`, `needs_reconfirming`, `not_yet_corroborated` or `not_applicable` (D-05, D-06, R-01, R-03, R-08);
- an integer score from 0 to 100 that stays null until 2 distinct places have confirmed (D-08, R-01);
- the facts behind it: the distinct confirming places inside the window and the window length in minutes;
- the server authored word, panel sentence and popup score sentence (D-18, D-19, A50);
- a fade stage for non critical rows only (D-07, R-02).

`BuildTrust` sets `Trust.Currency` from the same `filterVoteRows` buckets that give N. `Nearby`, `CastVote` and `ActivityForAccount` already build their trust objects through `BuildTrust` with injectable clocks (03-05), so all three carry the signal with no change to those paths.

How the roadmap word "score" is met (D-08, still current half): the roadmap asks for a fast decaying "is this still current" confidence score. The plan delivers an integer from 0 to 100 per report, recomputed on every read from real vote times and never stored. It loses one point per started 1/25 of a window and reaches 0 after the whole lifetime. The words are cut from that integer, and it is null, never 0, below 2 confirming places. Plan 03-10 records the reporter half, and 03-14 puts both on the wire.

Purpose: this is slice S3 "still current" in the outline's MVP framing. Plan 03-14 exposes the signal on the wire, and 03-13 renders the word, the popup number and the combined fade.

Increments, task by task (each ends at something a real test observes):
- After Task 1: a test can feed any vote times and an injected `now` into `buildCurrency` and get the band, score, word, sentence, facts and fade stage, pinned at every boundary for 8 hour and 24 hour reports.
- After Task 2: a service caller of `CastVote` gets `Up to date`, score 93 and fade `fresh` on the returned trust object, and `Getting old`, 66 and `aging` when the injected clock moves on. `Nearby` and `ActivityForAccount` carry the same signal, proven by the unchanged three path test.
- After Task 3: a reader of `docs/TRUST-MODEL.md` can see the formula, the boundary table, the worked example, what disputes do, when no score is shown and how the roadmap word score is met. The full suite is green on a fresh disposable database.

Output: new `currency.go` and `currency_test.go` in `internal/service`; edited `trustcopy.go`, `trustview.go`, `trustview_test.go` and `trustcopy_test.go`; a new section in `docs/TRUST-MODEL.md`.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading the feed during an emergency, **I want to** see how many different nearby people confirmed or disputed each report, and whether it is still current and its reporter reliable, **so that** a few accounts cannot make a weak report look well corroborated.

<objective>
Every feed row and Activity row names its severity in a visible rounded label read before the title. Read only labels share one rounded shape that no control uses. Row meta and titles stay legible and wrap. Expired Activity rows get their own treatment. This covers A1, A24 (the shared formatter), A26 (feed row), A37, A38, A40 (the label colours), A42, A44 (CSS), A45 (rows), A53 (label geometry as tokens) and A55, RR-5 as amended by A55, RR-7 for the labels, UI-SPEC open items 5 and 10, and V-40 narrowed to controls. It serves TRUST-05 and TRUST-07 by making the severity that the trust block qualifies readable as a word on every row, for sighted colour blind readers and screen reader users alike.

Purpose: the outline "Rows" slice. Before this plan a row carried severity by colour only (bar, tint, badge fill), so a screen reader user heard no severity at all and a colour blind reader had to guess. After it, every feed row says Critical, Medium or Low in words before the title, the state labels look like labels rather than buttons, the meta line passes 4.5:1 on every tint, and a pasted URL no longer overflows a 320px phone.

Output: the new `web/labels_contract_test.go`; edits to `app.js`, `trust.js`, `feed.js`, `trust.css` and `main.css`. No database is used by this plan.
</objective>

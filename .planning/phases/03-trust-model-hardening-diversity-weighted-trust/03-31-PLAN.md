## The facts

`Trust.Facts` is an ordered array of `{ kind, tone, title, detail, score_detail }`. Kinds: `status` (first, only for an expired report that is not resolved), `confirmations`, `disputes`, `currency`, `reporter`. A kind is left out when it does not apply: currency for Hidden, Retracted, expired, Provisional and not yet corroborated non critical reports (exactly when the existing still current label is empty), reporter for the none signal. Tones: `good`, `neutral`, `caution`, `bad`. An empty `detail` or `score_detail` is allowed.

T is `IndependentAgreementThreshold` (2), MIN is `ReliabilityMinJudged` (5), W the existing window phrase.

| Kind | Case | Tone | Title | Detail | Score detail |
|------|------|------|-------|--------|--------------|
| status | expired, not resolved | neutral | Expired | It can no longer be confirmed. | |
| confirmations | N 0 | neutral | Not confirmed yet | Needs T people at different spots. | |
| confirmations | N 1 | neutral | Confirmed by 1 person | Needs 1 more person at a different spot. | |
| confirmations | N 2 or more | good | Confirmed by N people | At different spots nearby. | |
| disputes | M 0 | good | No disputes | No one nearby has disputed this. | |
| disputes | M 1 | bad | Disputed by 1 person | Someone nearby says this is wrong. | |
| disputes | M 2 or more | bad | Disputed by M people | At different spots nearby. | |
| currency | Report up to date | good | Still happening | R confirmations in the last W. | Score S out of 100. |
| currency | Report getting old | caution | Getting old | Fewer than T confirmations in the last W. | Score S out of 100. |
| currency | Report needs re-confirming | bad | Gone quiet | People nearby can confirm it again. | Score S out of 100. |
| currency | a standing dispute raised the bar | caution | Needs more confirmations | Disputed nearby, so it needs K confirmations in the last W. | Score S out of 100. |
| currency | Too early to tell | neutral | Too early to tell | Fewer than T people have confirmed this yet. | |
| reporter | Reliable | good | Reliable reporter | C of their last J reports were confirmed. | Score S out of 100. |
| reporter | Mixed | caution | Mixed record | the same | Score S out of 100. |
| reporter | Unreliable | bad | Unreliable reporter | the same | Score S out of 100. |
| reporter | New | neutral | New reporter | Not rated yet. It needs MIN reports with a clear result. | |

Currency detail and score detail are the existing signal's own sentences (nothing is recomputed), and the reporter detail and score detail are the existing reporter sentences. A raised bar is read from the signal itself: the band's sentence is then not the plain sentence of the band. The "Needs ..." detail of a confirmations fact (N of 0 or 1) is left empty for Hidden, Retracted and expired reports, because nobody can act on it.

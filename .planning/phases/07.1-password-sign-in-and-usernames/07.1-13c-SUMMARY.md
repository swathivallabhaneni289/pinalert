# Phase 7.1 Plan 13c: Alerts ask inside the installed app Summary

**Inside the installed iPhone or iPad app, a signed in person is asked once per device and account "Get alerts for critical reports near {home area}?" on Nearby, with the sign up pop-up's words, the unticked Reliable reporter box, Not now and Allow.**

## Accomplishments

- page.go: `ProfileReader`, `PageConfig.Profiles`, `pageViewModel.HomeAreaName` and `homeAreaNameFor` (never fails the page, logs the account id and never the name). router.go: `page.Profiles = accounts` beside `page.HomeCentre`. `PushPublicKey` already existed on the view model and was reused.
- `installed_alerts_ask.html.tmpl`: `dialog.logout-confirm` `#installed-alerts-ask` with `data-push-key` and `data-account-id`, title, Reliable tick box (unticked), Not now, Allow. Included once in index.html.tmpl inside `{{if and .PushPublicKey .HomeAreaName}}`, with the deferred script. Low data, the Demo templates and the sign up page carry neither.
- `installed-ask.js` (`window.PinalertInstalledAsk = { ASKED_KEY, run }`): checks the dialog, notify.js functions, push key and account id, then `isAppleMobile()`, `isInstalled()`, `baseState() === 'maybe'` and the asked list; awaits `deviceState(dialog key)` for `ready`; then checks `dialog[open]` and `data-modal-open` at the moment it would open (same as changes-ask.js); writes the id to `pinalert.alertsAsked` just before `showModal`; title takes focus. Allow calls `enable(key)` first, then the Reliable PATCH only if ticked; Not now and Escape send nothing. Unreadable or junk storage means no ask. Builds no markup. A Mac Dock app is kept out by the isAppleMobile condition.
- welcome.js: `rememberAlertsAsk()` (copy of the 13b list rules for `ALERTS_ASKED_KEY`) runs in `openAsk` just before `showModal`, in its own try and catch, so a signup inside the installed app is not asked again on Nearby.

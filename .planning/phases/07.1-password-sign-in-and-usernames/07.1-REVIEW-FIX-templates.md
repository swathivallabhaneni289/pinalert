## Fixed

### CR-T01: no focus indicator on any sign in card field

- **Changed:** `web/static/css/auth.css` gets `.login-field:focus-within { outline: 2px solid var(--color-focus-ring); outline-offset: 2px; }`, as sketch 010 draws it.
  - The input keeps `outline: none`, so each field shows one ring.
  - While Show has focus, the field's ring draws around Show's own ring, as in the sketch.
- **Test:** `TestLoginFieldFocusRing`.
  - It pins the rule, and checks that no stylesheet takes the field's outline away.
  - It computes the ring's contrast in both themes against four backgrounds: the 65 percent glass over black, the same glass over white, the solid surface and the solid page colour. The floor is 3 to 1.
  - Lowest result: 4.99 to 1 (dark, glass over white). Light, glass over black: 6.30.
- **Measured:** after Tab, the field's outline is `solid 2px` in the text colour, in both engines, both themes and every size.
- **Commit:** 767871e4

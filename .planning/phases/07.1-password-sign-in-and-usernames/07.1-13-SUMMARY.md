# Phase 7.1 Plan 13: Step 4 required, with the shared username live check Summary

Step 4 of the first-time flow now asks for a Username (with a 300 ms debounced live check), a Name and a Home area, all three required, with no Skip; Continue stays switched off until all three are good, and the live check script is written once for step 4 and for Settings (plan 15).

## Open question for the owner (does not block this plan)

The plan and UI-SPEC Surface 7 give the home area help line as **"Filled in from your location. A town or area, never your street. Used for alerts."** The built flow does not do that: since plan 03.4-33 nothing fills the home area from the device position (step 3 says the exact location "is never shown to anyone and never stored"), and `TestWelcomeStep3And4Privacy` bans "Filled in from your location." on purpose. Writing it would state something untrue about privacy, so the line was built as **"A town or area, never your street. Used for alerts."**

To restore the plan's exact words, change one string in `web/templates/welcome.html.tmpl` (the `welcome-home-help` paragraph) to "Filled in from your location. A town or area, never your street. Used for alerts." and lift that ban (`"Filled in from your location."` in the `p13MustNotContain` call) in `TestWelcomeStep3And4Privacy` and the `p13Ordered` words next to it in `web/welcome_contract_test.go`. The decision is left to the orchestrator or owner.

## What was built

- **username-check.js** (new): an IIFE exposing `window.PinalertUsernameCheck.attach(input, line, options)`. It folds capitals as typed, drops a typed leading `@` and surrounding spaces, shows the local rule at once (`/^[a-z0-9_.]{3,20}$/`, at least one letter, no leading or trailing full stop, no `..`), shows the checking line (Checking, then an ellipsis) and asks `GET /api/username/check?u=` (encodeURIComponent) 300 ms after the last keystroke, ignores a stale answer, and treats a failed request, a non 200 or an unknown state as `failed`. Controller: `isGood()`, `state()`, `name()`, `showSaveError(message)`. States: rule, checking, available, taken, invalid, reserved, yours, wait, failed, refused. `isGood()` is true for available, and for yours in `signup` mode only (an account filling in keeps its own name). Every write is textContent; role and aria-live stay the template's.
- **Template**: step 4 has the Username (the `@` as an aria-hidden prefix inside the bordered `.username-field` box so the input itself is the 44px target), the live line `welcome-username-status`, `welcome-username-help`, the Name (label "Name", `welcome-name-help`), the Home area (`welcome-home-help` words changed), `welcome-error-4`, and only Continue (`btn btn--off`, `aria-disabled="true"`). The lead follows `.FillingIn`. `data-after-terms-step="{{.AfterTermsStep}}"` is on `#welcome`; `username-check.js` loads deferred between `position.js` and `welcome.js`. Skip, `welcome-skip` and the old optional lead are gone. The laptop step panel, the alerts pop-up and the "of 4" count are untouched.
- **welcome.js**: attaches the check in signup mode; `usernameGood`, `nameGood` (a letter or digit), `homeGood` (picked place, or the stored one left as drawn) feed `updateContinue` (aria-disabled plus the `btn--off` / `btn--primary` swap); a press while off calls `focusFirstMissing` and writes "Add your username, name and home area." into `welcome-error-4`; `profileBody` sends `username` (when it differs from the stored one), `display_name` and `home_area`; a 400 or 422 with field `username` goes under the Username through `showSaveError` and keeps step 4; other refusals (a name 400, a 429 with the server sentence) go to `welcome-error-4`; Agree goes to `data-after-terms-step` (4 or, by default, 3); `postDone` sends a 409 with field `profile` back to step 4 with the server's sentence (the pop-up is already closed by `doneFailed`), any other 409 still goes to step 2. `endFlow`, `canAsk`, `openAsk`, `saveReliable`, `ASK_TITLE_START`, `isMacDockApp` and the Phase 3.3 and Phase 5 behaviours are unchanged.
- **chrome.css**: `.username-field`, `.username-field__at`, `.username-field__input`, `.username-status:not([hidden])`, `.username-status--good|--bad|--warn` (icons by mask from check.svg, circle-x.svg, circle-alert.svg), a forced-colors block; no `!important`, no hex.

## Deviations from Plan

### Judgement calls and additions

1. **[Open question] Home area help line** built without "Filled in from your location." (above).
2. **`name="handle"` on the Username input** (the sketch used `name="username"`). It is pinned by `TestWelcomeUsernameNotLoginField`; plan 15's Settings input needs the same.
3. **API beyond the plan's list:** the controller also has `refresh()` (for a value set by the page), the extra state `refused` (a refusal shown by `showSaveError`), and the class `username-status--warn` (circle-alert icon for the wait state, which UI-SPEC asks to have an icon). A click on the `@` or the box edge focuses the input (done inside `attach` when the input sits in a `.username-field`), so Settings gets it too.
4. **Failed check and save refusal use the bad tone class**, not the `.inline-error` look UI-SPEC names; the words, icon and tone are the same family and the line stays one place with a fixed height.
5. **`finishStep4`:** the plan keeps `finish`, but `makeSearch` already has an inner `function finish()`, which broke `jsFunctionBody` lookups. Step 4's function is `finishStep4()`; the public hook `PinalertWelcome.finish` is the same function and now ignores any argument (it used to take save).
6. **Task 2 also touched `web/static/js/username-check.js` and `web/username_check_test.mjs`** (the click focus addition), which are Task 1's files, and went out as one commit as repo rule 4 says. Task 1 was RED then GREEN as two commits.
7. **A 429 on save** (profile PATCH) shows the server sentence in the general line and leaves Continue on, so the person can try again; only 400 and 422 on field `username` go under the Username and switch Continue off until the name is typed again.
8. **Stored values count as filled in:** the username, name and home area the page is drawn with are read from the field values at load (the same pattern as the stored name). The alerts tests keep reaching the pop-up's own home area search, the defensive path, by passing `askHome: ''` with step 4's field prefilled (the seam).
9. **Test helper:** `fillStep4(page)` exists in both node suites. It keeps the live checks in a separate `page.checks` list and takes the home area search request out of `page.requests`, so the old index assertions survived. The default `atStep4` of the alerts test now holds a stored username, name and home area (Continue on, nothing to save), as step 4 behaved when it was optional.
10. **Contract test edits:** `TestWelcomeStep3And4Privacy` (new help words, lead and Skip pins removed), `TestWelcomeStepsAreFreelyNavigable` and `TestWelcomeAlertsScript` (`finish(save)` is `finishStep4()`), `TestWelcomeScriptsAndStyles` (username-check.js in the load order, the sink list and the dash list), and `TestPhase4ContrastPairs` (tone good and tone bad on the page background in both themes; `--color-tone-bad` is an alias, so the test follows a single `var(--name)` once). Each carries a dated note.

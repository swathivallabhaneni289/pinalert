<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Sign up and sign in move from the emailed link (Phase 1.1) to an email and a password, with the email proved once at sign up by a typed 6-digit code. A forgotten password is reset with the same kind of code. Step 4 of the first-time flow (About you) becomes required for all three fields: Username (new, unique, public), Name and Home area. Accounts made with the old link set a password and fill in anything missing, once, at their next sign in. The Username can be changed in Settings.

Not in this phase: the report page, comments and Share (Phase 8), profiles, follows and stars (Phase 9), the Demo and the globe (Phase 5, which builds first and whose sign in screen this phase keeps), phone notifications (Phase 3.3).

Requirements: IDENT-01 and IDENT-04 as amended (password and typed code, limits on codes and tries), and the required step 4 (08-CONTEXT D-08, D-09). REQUIREMENTS.md and PROJECT.md's Access model are updated at planning (scope note 6).
</domain>

### Sign in and sign up
- **D-01 [OWNER DECIDED, 2026-10-17, was 08 D-10 and scope note 6]:** The sign in page offers two clear choices, Create account and Sign in, replacing today's single "Verify your email to continue" form. Create account takes an email and a password, emails a 6-digit code once, the person types it in (no link to click anywhere), then the first-time flow runs. Sign in takes the email and the password only. A forgotten password is reset with the same kind of typed code. The owner's words: "i want a clean sign up without complicating anything it should be an easy way to sign up and sign in", "lets not have any link. lets just have a password", "lets do what you recommended - check email once at sign uo".
- **D-02 [LOCKED EARLIER, scope note 6 engineering notes]:** Passwords are stored only as a slow hash (argon2id or bcrypt, research picks one). Wrong passwords are limited per account and per device, mindful of shared carrier addresses (CGNAT), so one abuser cannot lock out a whole carrier. The sign in error never says whether the email has an account ("Email or password is wrong"). Create account with a known email shows the same "we sent a code" screen, while the email itself says the account exists. Codes are short lived, single use, limited in tries and stored hashed.
- **D-03 [OWNER DECIDED, 2026-10-17, was 08 D-07]:** An account is needed to use Pinalert. Only these open without one: the Demo (Phase 5), the Disclaimer and Policy pages, and Phase 8's shared report status page. The Phase 1.1 gate stands; the new sign up, code and reset screens join the exempt list pinned by `internal/api/gate_test.go`.
- **D-04 [LOCKED EARLIER, 05-CONTEXT D-03 and D-14, 05-UI-SPEC section 5d]:** The new card keeps everything Phase 5 builds on the sign in screen: the ids `login-gate` and the card panel (`login-form-panel` or its successor) in the order heading, fields, button, Low data panel, links row; the globe partial `login_globe.html.tmpl` included after `.login-column`, never inside the card; the `login_ways_links.html.tmpl` row ("Try the demo", "Disclaimer", "Policy", with the reserved explainer video slot); the Low data words and the `LowDataDefault` gate; the credit line outside the faded wrapper; and the tests that pin the globe tags, the WebGL2 probe, the tile cap and the Try the demo link. Phase 5 builds first, so this phase builds on Phase 5's `login_gate.html.tmpl`.
- **D-05 [LOCKED EARLIER, 05 UI-SPEC section 5d item 6]:** No email address ever appears in a page address on any new screen (the code screen, the reset screens), because the map tile requests carry the page address as their referrer. Carry the email in the server session or a form field, never in the query string.

### Step 4 and the username
- **D-06 [OWNER DECIDED, 2026-10-17, was 08 D-08]:** Step 4 of the first-time flow (About you) requires all three: **Username** (live check), **Name** and **Home area** (a town or area, never a street). No Skip. Today's step 4 says "Both are optional. They show only if you later let others follow you." and has Skip (03.4 as built); this phase changes it once.
- **D-07 [LOCKED EARLIER, was 08 D-09]:** The Username is unique and public to everyone, Instagram style; the Name stays a profile only detail (Phase 9). The Username is editable in Settings (About you) with the same live check: available, taken, wrong characters, or kept for official use (police, ndma, imd, govt and similar), and it is screened like other free text with Phase 4's text check.
- **D-08 [LOCKED EARLIER, scope notes 5 and 6]:** Accounts made with the old link fill in whatever is missing (password, Username, Name, Home area) once, at their next sign in, in a single pass, so nobody is stopped twice.
- **D-09 [LOCKED EARLIER, 08 D-11]:** The new sign in and the required step 4 ship together, so step 4 changes only once.

### Rough edges from sketch 009 that outlive the link flow
- **D-10 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** Each sign in screen is a real form, so Enter submits and the browser checks the fields. A signed in person who opens `/login` is sent to the app instead of seeing the form. On the code screen, a refused Resend shows its message where it can be seen, and there is a way back to change a mistyped email. The link flow's other rough edges go away with it.

### Rules nobody was asked about
- **D-11 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** The Username rules, the code lifetime and try count, the password minimum and the limiter numbers are set at research and planning, written in plain words in the UI-SPEC, and shown at the viewing.
- **D-12 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION]:** The old link routes and the `check_inbox` and `verify_outcome` pages are removed once the new flow works; an old emailed link that is opened afterwards lands on the sign in page with a short plain message rather than an error page.

### Decisions at planning (2026-10-25, CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, shown at the viewing)
- **D-13:** Password minimum 8 characters, refused if it is on a common password list, no composition rules. Research proposed 15 (NIST SP 800-63B-4 for a password that is the only factor); the owner asked for an easy sign up, so 8 is the default and 15 is listed as the stricter option in the UI-SPEC defaults and in the docs.
- **D-14:** Option X from research: the migration ends the sessions of accounts that have no password, so the one time fill in (D-08) always rides a real sign in through a typed code.
- **D-15:** Once step 4 is required, Name and Home area can be changed in Settings but not cleared.
- **D-16:** Research for 7.1 and 8 disagree on an unfinished username screen (7.1: save as pending; 8: refuse to save until the check finishes). The 7.1 planner picks one, writes why, and Phase 8 follows it.

### Added after planning (2026-10-28)
- **D-17 [OWNER, 2026-10-28]:** An iPhone Safari tab gets a small pop-up at the end of sign up that shows how to add Pinalert to the Home Screen, built together with the 7.1 sign in. Asked by pinalert-6d during the 3.3 checks and answered "yes" (03.3-UAT.md, gap for test 4, open_question). Why: a Safari tab on an iPhone cannot get phone notifications, so neither alerts pop-up of the 3.3 gap fix opens there, and the install steps lived only in Settings, which the owner's rule of 2026-10-27 says nobody will find. Why with 7.1: until the password sign in, the Home Screen app could not sign in, because the emailed link opened in Safari. Once the person opens Pinalert from the Home Screen and signs in, the installed app asks the normal alerts pop-up once, so they never have to go to Settings, and step 3 of the install guide says so. Plans 13b and 13c.
- **D-18 [CLAUDE'S DISCRETION, shown at the viewing]:** The details of D-17.
  - The install pop-up has the Log out box look, the title "Get alerts for critical reports near {home area}", the guide's lead and three steps, and one button, Not now. It never asks the browser for anything, and sign up finishes whatever is tapped.
  - It shows once per account on a browser (a browser key holding the account id), because step 4 is passed again after every later terms update. An account made with the old link sees it at the end of its one time fill in.
  - It also shows on an iPad Safari tab, since the same rule applies there.
  - The installed app asks on Nearby only, never on the Low data view or the Demo. It asks once per device and account (a browser key holding the account id), only when this device is not already on or blocked, and only when no other pop-up is open.
  - The words are the same as the sign up alerts pop-up, pinned by a test.

D-10 to D-16, and D-18.

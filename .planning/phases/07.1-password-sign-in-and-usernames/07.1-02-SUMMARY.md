## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] Stricter stored hash parsing**
- **Issue:** the plan caps m and t and refuses p of 0; a stored row could still carry t of 0 (which makes argon2.IDKey panic), an oversized salt or key, or trailing text that Sscanf ignores.
- **Fix:** parsePHC also refuses m, t of 0, salt or key over 64 bytes, and any string that does not re-encode to itself. Tests for each are in TestHasherRejectsMalformed.
- **Files:** internal/auth/password.go, internal/auth/password_test.go

**2. [Note] go get moved other official golang.org/x modules**
- `go get golang.org/x/crypto@v0.58.0` and `go mod tidy` also raised golang.org/x/text v0.41.0 to v0.43.0, golang.org/x/sync v0.22.0 to v0.24.0 and golang.org/x/tools v0.48.0 to v0.51.0 (minimum versions x/crypto v0.58.0 requires) and added `golang.org/x/sys v0.49.0 // indirect`, as research A3 predicted. All are Go team modules; `go mod verify` passes and the whole tree builds. No third party module was added.

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (04-28, slice S2 "abuse screened, held state"):** a five language fixture of 150 to 300 realistic genuine reports (en, hi, te, ta, hinglish; urgent wording, all capitals, addresses, phone numbers) plus abusive examples runs offline through `Decide` and the gibberish rule, and once scores are recorded proves no genuine report rejected, no serious report withheld and the abusive set rejected (D-07). After this plan the gibberish half of that claim and the gate itself are proven in every quick run, and the full claim becomes provable the moment the owner records the scores in 04-27, with no code change.

# Plan 04-22: Saved reports on the device, Send after the wait, and the held toast

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP slice S6 "offline queue", first plan):** a report posted with no signal, a timeout or a 502, 503 or 504 is saved on the device bound to its account, shown in a "Waiting to send" strip on the main page and the Low data view, retried on `online`, load, a timer and Send now, never expires, and a 429 offers "Send after the wait"; a created report with `held` true (201 or 200 replay, form or queue flush) shows the 6 second held toast instead of "Report posted." or "Report sent."; and `?resend=<request_id>` on the main page or Low data view opens that page's form prefilled ("Not sent. {REASON}") only for a saved row of the same account (D-02, D-18, D-19, D-20, D-12 serious line, P4-A12, P4-A13, P4-A17, T-04-14). Before this plan a failed post showed "Something went wrong. Try again." and the typed report was lost the moment the form closed; a held report said "Report posted."; after it the report survives a dead connection and sends by itself.

<objective>
Build the device queue and wire it into the shared report form and both pages that have a form.

1. Queue core (D-18, D-19, D-20, T-04-14): new `web/static/js/queue.js` exporting `window.PinalertQueue` (`mount`, `save`, `flush`, `discard`, `get`) over plain IndexedDB with an in memory fallback, the Waiting to send strip, its exact status table, the 60 second wait refresh, the cross tab guard and the `resend` landing; `Pinalert.submitReport` classifies a failed request, a timeout and 502, 503, 504 as queueable and passes the 429 fields and `held` through (P4-A13); the strip's look in `chrome.css`; and the main page wiring (`data-account-id` on `#app-shell`, the strip, the `queue.js` tag, the `feed.js` mount), so the strip mounts on the main page at the end of this step.

3. The Low data view and the rest of the pages (D-20): the strip, the note slot and the `queue.js` tag on the Low data view, the note slot on the main page, the mount in `low.js`, the resend landing tests, and the notice card Queue row that 04-13 handed to this plan.

## Decisions recorded for this plan (Claude's discretion and open item defaults)

- Open item 6 default kept: no HTTP answer, a 10 second timeout and 502, 503, 504 queue a report; every other answer keeps the form open; a 429 offers Send after the wait (open item 19, owner decision).
- Open item 4 defaults kept: post request timeout 10 seconds, queue flush timer 30 seconds. Open item 9: the held toast and the two save toasts use 6000, the Send after the wait toast the default 3000. Open item 16: the strip heading is `h2` on the main page and the Low data view (04-25 passes `h3` on Activity).
- **`modal.js` is added to this plan** (the outline's File ownership row now lists 04-22 (w7) as its one later editor and the Sizing row says 12 files, a warning, accepted; no other wave 7 plan lists it, so no same wave conflict exists). Reason: 04-10's `startLocation` re-runs `initLocation` on every open, and its geolocation success callback places the marker at the live fix unconditionally, so an Edit and resend prefill (which must run after `start`, because the map is created there) would have its saved location silently moved to wherever the person is now. One guard at the top of both callbacks keeps any chosen place; it also stops a late fix from moving a typed search result the person already picked.
- **Saved location on the Low data view:** a saved payload has no geocoder display name, so `report-form.js` passes `displayName: ''` and `low.js` shows the planner authored sentence "Using the saved location.

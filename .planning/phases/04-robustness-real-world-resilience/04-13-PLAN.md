## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP slice S4 "weak network main page", first plan):** after this plan, on a weak connection the main page keeps its list and says "Last updated N minutes ago" instead of hiding it, a stalled poll times out after 10 seconds, and when the list has not loaded in about 6 seconds a notice says "Taking long? Open the low data view." even when unpkg hangs (D-17, D-20, P4-A12, P4-A19, P4-A24). Before this plan one failed poll replaced the whole list with an error panel, a stalled request hung forever, and a hung unpkg left a blank page with no way out.

**Accepted interim states (MVP framing, no human check runs before 04-27):**
- The link "Open the low data view." targets `/low`, which 04-18 (wave 5) serves; until then it answers 404, as 04-06 accepted for `/disclaimer`.
- The card's hide rule already reads `html[data-modal-open]`, which only `Pinalert.lockPage` (04-19, wave 6) sets. Until then the open report form's backdrop (z-index 1100) paints over the card (z-index 1000).
- The card's Queue row ("{N} waiting to send." with "Show waiting reports") needs `queue.js`, which 04-22 (wave 7) creates; 04-22 inserts it between `#notice-slow` and `#notice-stale` with the class `notice-card__row` and follows the re-sync rule below.

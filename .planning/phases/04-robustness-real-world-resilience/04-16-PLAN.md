## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S2 "abuse screened, held state", its last step):** after this plan an author sees their own held report as "Waiting for a text check" and a removed one as "Removed after review" on the Activity page, with no trust block, counters or vote controls, and a held row past expiry shows Expired. Before it, 04-07 made those reports invisible everywhere else, but the Activity row showed a zero trust object and an unstyled `vis-` class built from the withheld slug, so the author could not tell why a report vanished.

<objective>
Tell the author, on their own Activity page, why a report of theirs is not in any feed (ROBUST-03, ROBUST-09; D-02, D-08, D-24, R-01, R-02, R-03, P4-A8, P4-A9, P4-A10).

After this plan:
- The server decides moderation once from `Resolve`'s answer (`withheld` plus its reason) and writes `data-moderation="held"` or `data-moderation="removed"` on the row, omits `data-trust`, writes the first paint row class with the P4-A9 precedence, and renders the exact sentence from the 04-12 Go constants, with the Policy link only when `CONTACT_EMAIL` is set.
- Every Activity row whose report was sent more than 30 minutes after it was written shows "Written 4 hours ago" under its meta line (P4-A8, the Activity line).
- `PinalertVisibility.displayState` gains the removed and held rungs, the label table gains the two labels, and `activity.js` reads `moderation` from the row, places the label before the sentence and mounts no trust block on a moderated row.
- `trust.css` gives `vis-held` and `vis-removed` the `vis-expired` treatment in their own rules.

Increments, task by task:
- After Task 1: an end to end test on a real database proves the held, removed, held and expired, resolved then removed, and pending serious rows carry the right attribute, class, sentence and (absent) trust, the Policy link appears only with a contact address, and the Written line renders.
- After Task 2: the author sees the labels after load in both themes, the label and class survive page load because `displayState` decides them, and the row reads as inactive like an expired one.

Output: edited `internal/api/handlers/auth.go`, `web/templates/profile.html.tmpl`, `web/static/js/visibility.js`, `web/static/js/activity.js`, `web/static/css/trust.css`, `web/votes_contract_test.go`; new `internal/api/handlers/activity_moderation_e2e_test.go` and `web/activity_moderation_contract_test.go`.

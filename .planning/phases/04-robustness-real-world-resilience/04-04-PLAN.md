## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to**
keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out
and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not
misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S2 "abuse screened, held state", outline row 04-04):** a provider client sends
only the trimmed text to the pinned snapshot with a 3 second deadline, classifies 429, 5xx and timeouts as
transient and quota and other 4xx as permanent, honours `Retry-After`, a loader picks the real client, a
development pass screener or a startup error, and an opt in recorder can capture fixture scores (D-01, D-07,
Claude's Discretion provider client). After this plan, the screening provider can be called safely and
proven safe by tests with no live call, and the owner has a command that records the D-07 fixture scores.

<objective>
Build the standalone package `internal/moderation` (client, loader, development pass screener) and the
command `cmd/modrecord`, each proven by `httptest` or fake screener tests that never touch the network.

Purpose: D-01 needs a short, text only provider call whose failure modes the submit path (04-11) and the
background worker (04-14) can act on: transient means pending and retry later, quota and permanent faults
are logged and never retried in a storm. D-07 needs provider scores recorded once against the pinned snapshot
so the fixture test (04-28) runs offline; the recorder also answers the "does a zero balance key work" risk
(CONTEXT Claude's Discretion, account setup risk).

Output: six new files. Nothing is wired into `cmd/server/main.go` here (04-11 does that), and no existing
file changes.
</objective>

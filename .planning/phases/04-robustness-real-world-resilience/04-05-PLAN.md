## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP framing: a store plan, like 03-06, that ends at store level guarantees a test proves):** after this plan the database can hold moderation state, a display only written time and a request id. A real Postgres test proves that a retried insert with the same account and request id makes one row while another account gets its own, that the account scoped replay finds it, that flags are unique per account, that the worker claim hands a check to one claimant, that the extras read is one batched call, and that seeded rows are `clear` with no check row (R-01, R-05, R-10, D-04, D-08, D-09, D-19). Nothing user visible changes yet; 04-07 makes held reports invisible, 04-11 writes the state at submit, 04-17 uses the request id.

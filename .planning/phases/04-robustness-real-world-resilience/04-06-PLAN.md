## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S1 "says it is unofficial", first plan):** after this plan, every existing full page (login, verify outcome, map, Activity) shows the thin unofficial bar first in `<body>` inside `.top-chrome`, with the exact D-22 sentence composed from one numbers setting and a link to the Disclaimer, and every full page renders with the shared `Chrome` data (D-21, D-22, D-23 bar link, D-24 setting, R-08, P4-A1 markup). Before this plan no page says Pinalert is unofficial.

**Accepted interim look (outline MVP framing):** 04-06 does not edit `main.css` or `auth.css`. Until 04-08 (wave 2) the fixed account trigger overlaps the bar on the map and Activity pages, and the map, Activity, login and verify pages overflow by the bar height. No UAT, deploy or human check runs between wave 1 and wave 2. The bar link targets `/disclaimer`, which 04-12 (wave 3) serves; until then it answers 404, which is also accepted interim.

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S2 "abuse screened, held state", after 04-01, 04-02, 04-04, 04-05 and 04-07):** after this plan, posting runs the text check inline. Abusive text is refused with a named reason and nothing is stored, at every severity; borderline text posts and is queued for review; a check that cannot finish holds a non serious report off every feed (the 201 says `held`) and publishes a serious one with its check still queued; and the description length counts characters, not bytes. Before this plan nothing screened a post and nothing could produce a held report, so 04-07's "held is invisible" read path had no real input.

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S2 "abuse screened, held state"):** after this plan, a held or removed report is provably in no feed, no map, no history view and no vote. `Nearby`, `CastVote` and `ActivityForAccount` pass moderation into `Resolve` through one batched extras read (the vote path through its context row), a vote on a withheld report answers 404, the Activity read carries each row's state and display only written time, and a source guard keeps every future `Resolve` caller honest. Nothing produces a pending report until 04-11, so this plan proves it with fakes and with rows whose state a test sets directly.

<objective>
Make moderation state reach every resolver call on the read and vote paths (ROBUST-03; R-01, R-02, R-05, D-02, D-19; corrections 5 and 6; U11).

After this plan:
- `CastVote` refuses a withheld report with `ErrReportNotFound` right after the context read (the handler already maps it to 404 field `report`), before the own report block and expiry, and passes `Moderation` into `Resolve`.
- `Nearby` makes one `ReportExtrasForReports` read per non empty page, builds every `ReportMeta` with `Moderation`, never lists a withheld report, and fills `ReportView.WrittenAt` and `ReportView.ViewerFlagged` for later plans (04-17, 04-20).
- `ActivityForAccount` makes the same read, carries `Moderation` and `WrittenAt` on every row, and skips `BuildTrust` for a withheld row.
- `TestResolveCallersMentionModeration` fails the build for any future `Resolve` caller that forgets moderation.
- `TestWithheldReportsInNoFeed` proves it end to end on `p4_0407`.

Increments, task by task:
- After Task 1: a vote on a held or removed report is refused before anything is written, proven by service tests.
- After Task 2: the feed and Activity reads carry moderation from one batched read, a withheld report never lists, and the read paths carry the display only written time, proven by service tests with fakes.
- After Task 3: a source guard pins every `Resolve` caller, and a real router over a real database proves the whole rule.

Purpose: 04-01 gave `Resolve` its moderation rungs, and 04-05 gave the store its columns and the extras query. Without this plan neither reaches a real request, so 04-11 could not hold a report off the feed.

Output: edits to `trust.go`, `feed.go`, `report.go` and `auth.go` in `internal/service`; two fakes extended; four new and two amended pinned tests plus one added test; two new test files.
</objective>

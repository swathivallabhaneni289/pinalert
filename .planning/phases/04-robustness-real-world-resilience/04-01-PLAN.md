## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to**
keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out
and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not
misled about what Pinalert is.

This plan's step (slice S2 "abuse screened, held state", first plan): after it, a test can hand the single
visibility resolver any moderation state and prove that a removed report is withheld above every other rung
(even critical), that a pending non serious report is withheld, that a pending serious report falls through
to the critical bypass and is published, that the zero value behaves exactly as clear, and that `withheld`
is listable in no view. 04-07 then wires the state into every read path, 04-29 makes the reporter rating
neutral for it, and 04-11 produces the pending state at submit.

<objective>
Add the moderation rung to `Resolve` in `internal/service/visibility.go` and prove it test first.

1. A closed `ModerationState` type (`clear`, `pending`, `removed`) carried on `ReportMeta.Moderation`,
   whose zero value `Resolve` treats as `clear` (R-01, R-02).
2. A fifth `Visibility`, `withheld`, and two `ResolveReason` slugs, `removed_after_review` and
   `awaiting_text_check`, appended to their closed lists so `Valid()` and the totality loop accept them
   (R-01).
3. Two exported predicates: `Serious()` (the one serious rule, R-04) and `Withheld()` (removed, or
   pending and not serious; D-02, R-01, R-03), and two new top rungs in `Resolve` that read `Withheld()`.
4. Five pinned tests (V-06): `TestResolveModeration`, `TestResolveModerationZeroValueNeutral`,
   `TestReportMetaSeriousMatchesBypass`, `TestReportMetaWithheld`, `TestWithheldInNoView`.

Purpose: R-01 makes moderation a separate authority from the vote tally. Putting it inside the one
resolver, above Retracted and the bypass, means no read path can show a held or removed report by
re-deriving visibility on its own (T-04-16), and one predicate decides "serious" for the bypass and the
report allowance alike (R-04).

Output: the edited `internal/service/visibility.go` and `internal/service/visibility_test.go`, the new
`internal/service/moderation_view_test.go`, one RED commit and one GREEN commit.
</objective>

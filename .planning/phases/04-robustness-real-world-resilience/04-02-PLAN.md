## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (04-02, slice S2 "abuse screened, held state"):** one reusable text policy decides pass, borderline or reject from provider scores (only hate, harassment and sexual reject; violence, self harm and illicit never do), rejects gibberish counted in runes, and names the problem in four plain messages (D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06, R-06). After this plan a test can hand any provider scores to `Decide` and any text to `IsGibberish` and get the answer the decisions define, and the four exact refusal messages exist behind `RejectMessage`. Nothing is wired into posting yet: 04-11 calls these from `CheckText`, 04-14 from the worker, and 04-28 runs the D-07 fixture through them.

<objective>
Ship `internal/service/screening.go`, a pure policy file with no I/O, and prove it test first.

1. `Decide(scores, serious)` maps provider category scores to a `ScreenDecision`: reject (hateful, harassing or sexual) at or above `RejectScoreThreshold`, borderline at or above `BorderlineScoreThreshold`, otherwise pass. `serious` never changes the answer, so choosing Critical cannot skip screening (D-03, T-04-02). Violence, self harm and illicit scores only ever reach borderline, because genuine disaster reports use those words (D-03, D-04).
2. `IsGibberish(text)` is the only spam rule (D-05): a run of one letter repeated 10 times, or fewer than two words of two or more letters, counted in runes with Indic vowel signs kept inside words (R-06).
3. `RejectMessage(reason)` returns the three exact UI-SPEC abuse sentences and, for gibberish, the owner's sentence of 2026-10-08 that asks the author to say what is happening (04-CONTEXT D-05 owner note; it replaces the UI-SPEC gibberish row). A shared test helper `assertServerCopy` holds every Phase 4 server message to the closed character set, and a Go AST guard proves the file carries no report vocabulary (D-06).

Purpose: the screening rules must be provable in isolation before any handler, worker or fixture relies on them. Every later screening plan reads these names verbatim.

Output: three new files, `internal/service/screening.go`, `internal/service/screening_test.go`, `internal/service/servercopy_test.go`; a RED and a GREEN commit per task.
</objective>

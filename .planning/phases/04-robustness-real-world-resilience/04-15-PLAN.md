## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S3 "limits", after 04-03 and 04-09):** after this plan, posting a report has two separate allowances keyed on the verified account (serious 5 then 1 every 5 minutes, other 8 then 1 every 15 minutes) chosen by `Serious()`; a rejected, invalid or failed post costs nothing; the submit attempt bucket and the configured IP backstop guard the report route; a blocked post gets a 429 that says exactly how long to wait, carries `serious` and, for a serious post, the emergency number from the one setting; every block is logged; and every 429 (report and vote routes) is in OpenAPI. Before this plan the report route had no limit at all (U1), so one account could flood the moderator screen and the provider budget.

<objective>
Wire steps 4 and 5 of the pinned `POST /api/reports` order into the handler 04-11 built, using the `RateGate` 04-09 built, and document every 429 (ROBUST-04; D-10, D-11, D-12, D-22, R-04, P4-A13, U1; orchestrator decision 1; verified correction 1).

After this plan:
- `RateGate.AdmitReport` runs the IP backstop, then the submit attempt bucket, then the serious or other allowance, writes the `rate_report` 429 through `writeRateLimited` with `service.ReportLimitMessage(serious, wait)` and the configured number, logs the block, and returns the allowance refund.
- `SubmitReport(svc, gate)` calls it once after `ValidateSubmitInput` and before `SubmitChecked`, and defers the refund unless a new report was stored.
- `router.go` passes the gate to `SubmitReport`.
- `@Failure 429` with a `Retry-After` header sits on `POST /reports` and the four vote routes, `make swag` regenerates the docs once, and `TestSwaggerSpecCoversRoutes` requires them.

Increments, task by task:
- After Task 1: a person who posts too many reports in a short time gets a 429 with the exact wait, `serious` and the number, keyed on their account, with separate serious and other allowances (proven end to end on `p4_0415`).
- After Task 2: a rejected, invalid or failed post provably costs nothing, the submit attempt bucket is never refunded, and every block is logged without personal data.
- After Task 3: every 429 is in the published API reference and a drift guard fails if it goes missing; the whole `internal/api` database suite is green with the new limits.

Purpose: D-11's allowances are the only automatic brake on fake serious posts (critical reports cannot be hidden by disputes), and orchestrator decision 1 keeps a person who rewords rejected text from being locked out. 04-17 then inserts the replay lookup (step 3) in front of this gate, and 04-22 builds the form message, Send after the wait and the 112 link from this 429 body.

Output: edits to `ratelimit_gate.go`, `reports.go`, `votes.go`, `router.go` and `swagger_test.go`; the new `report_ratelimit_e2e_test.go`; the regenerated `docs/docs.go`, `docs/swagger.json` and `docs/swagger.yaml`.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S5 "Low data view", first plan; a pure refactor plan by design per the outline's MVP framing):** after this plan, the report form logic is proven by contract tests to live in one shared script, `report-form.js`, behind a small location interface that `modal.js` implements with Leaflet; the main page posts exactly as before with every pinned test re-pointed and green; the form's secondary button reads "Cancel report"; and the description box carries the owner's hint line (2026-10-08, D-05), so both forms tell a person to say what is happening. Before this plan the form could not run without a Leaflet marker (`validate()` required `modalMarker`, `buildPayload()` read `modalMarker.getLatLng()`), so no map free page could post (D-15 prerequisite, P4-A23, RESEARCH "The form extraction is the riskiest refactor"). The refactor merges green two waves before the Low data view mounts the form (04-19).

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S6 "offline queue", its last step):** after this plan the Activity page shows the author's own waiting reports with Send now and Discard report, and a rejected one carries an Edit and resend link to the page that has a form (`/low?resend=ID` when this device's default is the Low data view, else `/?resend=ID`). Before it, 04-22 drew the strip only on the main page and the Low data view, so a person who opened Activity to check on a report saved offline saw nothing and could not resend it from there. An optional Node test also proves flush order, never discard and wrong account never sent. This plan only builds the link; the `resend` landing is 04-22's.

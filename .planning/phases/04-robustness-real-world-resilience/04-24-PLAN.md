# Plan 04-24: Flag this report on every reading surface

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP slice S7 "flag and moderate", third plan, after the Flag API of 04-20 and the moderator API of 04-23):** a reader can tap "Flag this report" on a feed row, a popup and a Low data row, choose a reason in a locked dialog and see "Flagged", without the tap ever activating the row, and Escape closes the dialog first (D-09, P4-A5, P4-A6, P4-A7). Before this plan the flag route existed but no person could reach it; after it, any signed in reader can send a post to the moderators from wherever they read it, and the moderator screen of 04-26 has real flags to show.

<objective>
Build the one shared Flag control and the one Flag dialog, and mount the control on the three reading surfaces.

1. The control (D-09, P4-A6, P4-A7): `flag.js` exports `window.PinalertFlag` with `createFlagControl(report)` and `updateFlagControl(control, report)`; two `stopPropagation` listeners keep it from activating a row; hidden on the viewer's own report; "Flagged" from `viewer_flagged` or a page session record; `chrome.css` gives it the quiet underlined look.

## Decisions recorded for this plan (Claude's discretion and open item defaults)

- **`createRow(id)` passes `{ id: id }`.** The feed row builder has only the id (Phase 3 tests anchor its header), so the outline's `createFlagControl(report)` reads only `report.id` at build time, builds the control hidden, and every other field is applied by `updateFlagControl`, which `updateRow` runs right after creation. The popup and Low data row pass the full report.
- **Page session flagged record** (`flaggedHere`, filled on a 201 or a 409): a poll that left before the flag can still carry `viewer_flagged: false`; the control shows Flagged when either says so. After a 201 or 409 every `.flag-control` on the page for that id flips (the row and the popup can both be open).
- **Sending blocks closing.** The UI-SPEC Sending row lists no controls, so Escape, a backdrop click and Go back are ignored until the answer; the 10 second bound keeps that short.
- **Answer mapping.** No HTTP answer or a timeout gives the offline sentence (a timed out request had no answer). 400, 401, 403 and any 5xx give the generic sentence (403 cannot happen: the control is hidden on your own report). A 429 shows `body.error.message` (the server's `FlagLimitMessage`); a 429 without a readable message (a proxy) shows the generic sentence, so no client copy of the server sentence exists.

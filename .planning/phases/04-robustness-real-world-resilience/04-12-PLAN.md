## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (slice S1 "says it is unofficial", third and last plan):** after this plan a logged out visitor can read the public Disclaimer and Policy pages: no account data, a private short cache, the numbers block from the one setting with 112 as a tap to call link, the label glossary from the app's own sentences, and the contact blocks only when an address is configured (D-22, D-23, D-24, R-07, P4-A18). Before it, the bar's Disclaimer link (04-06) answers 404.

<objective>
Serve `GET /disclaimer` and `GET /policy` as public, cacheable only in the browser (`private, max-age=300`), account free full pages that carry the bar, the D-22 numbers block from the one numbers setting, a glossary built from the app's own trust sentences, honest Policy text drawn from what the app really does, and contact blocks that exist only when `CONTACT_EMAIL` is set. Also define the Activity held and removed strings that 04-16 renders.

Purpose: the ROBUST-08 and ROBUST-10 half of the phase goal that tells a reader what Pinalert is and is not, without logging in.
Output: `internal/service/moderationcopy.go` and its test, `internal/api/handlers/legal.go`, two templates, `legal.css`, the two public routes with the router doc comment, the amended `TestAccessGateExemptRoutes`, and two new test files.
</objective>

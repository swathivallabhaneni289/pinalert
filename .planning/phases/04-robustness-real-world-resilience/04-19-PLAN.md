# Plan 04-19: Post and vote from the Low data view

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP slice S5, after 04-10 and 04-18):** on the Low data view a person can post a report with their phone position or a typed place and Confirm or Dispute (never Mark resolved), forms lock the page behind them, and a rate limited vote shows the wait in the vote error everywhere (D-15, D-16, D-12 votes, P4-A15, P4-A26, P4-A27, U8, U10). Before this plan the Low data view could only be read; after it the whole reporting loop works there without a map.

<objective>
Make the Low data view a full participant in the reporting loop and close the vote side of D-12.

1. Votes (D-15, P4-A15, R-09, D-12): `createVoteBlock(reportId, options)` skips Mark resolved when `options.resolve === false`; `low.js` mounts the block with `{ resolve: false }` after the trust block and adds the Written line (P4-A8 Low row). A 429 on any vote is remembered per report and vote kind inside `castVote`, its message shows in `.vote-error`, and no request is sent while the wait runs. `Pinalert.formatWait` words the wait.
2. Page lock and Escape (P4-A26, P4-A5): `Pinalert.lockPage(backdrop)` and `Pinalert.unlockPage()` in `app.js`, called by the shared form's open and close; `modal.css` locks scrolling; one generic `trust.js` guard keyed on `html[data-modal-open]`.
3. Posting without a map (D-15, D-16, U8, U10, P4-A27): the Low page renders its own report dialog shell (as the main page does, 04-10) with the map free location fieldset around the shared search block, then includes the shared static form partial unchanged; `low.js` implements the map free location interface with the eight states through 04-10's optional `start` and `reset` hooks; `report-form.js` gains the Low variant behaviour and an 8 second search timeout.

Purpose: ROBUST-01. A person on a weak connection can post and confirm without loading a map.
Output: edits to 12 existing files and one new contract test file, `web/low_form_contract_test.go` (13 files, as the outline Sizing row now says; its first count of 14 included `report_form.html.tmpl`, which 04-10 made a static partial that each page wraps with its own shell and location block, so this plan does not touch it).
</objective>

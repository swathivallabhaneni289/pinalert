## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP slice S2 "abuse screened, held state", late verdicts):** after this plan a report held because the text check could not finish is retried in the background with backoff, jitter and `Retry-After`, released on a late pass, released as clear and listed for review (`needs_review`) on a late borderline, withdrawn as removed on a late reject (serious included) and never retried once expired. Before this plan a provider outage at submit left a non serious report held forever and a serious one published unscreened forever. A database test run on `p4_0414` proves each of these outcomes, and the running server starts the worker with the process.

## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to**
keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out
and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not
misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP slice S3 "limits", first plan):** after this plan a test run proves a keyed token
bucket that every Phase 4 limiter will use: it admits a burst, refills on an injected clock, keeps keys
apart, tells the exact wait in whole seconds, refunds a token for real (cancel at the reservation instant,
proven with the clock advanced), never evicts a bucket before it would have refilled, and never over admits
under concurrent load with `-race`. Before this plan, `internal/ratelimit` had only `PerIP.Allow`, which has
no wait time, no refund, no injected clock and a single ten minute idle window. Nothing is wired to a route
here: 04-09 (votes), 04-15 (reports) and 04-20 (flag) consume it.

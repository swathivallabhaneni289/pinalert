## Phase Goal

**As a** person reading or reporting during an emergency, often on a weak phone connection, **I want to** keep reading, posting and confirming reports when the signal is bad, knowing abusive posts are screened out and that the site says plainly it is unofficial, **so that** the reporting loop keeps working and I am not misled about what Pinalert is.

**This plan's step (MVP slice S7 "flag and moderate", the moderator API):** after this plan a moderator on the server list can read the three queues, find any report by id, and Keep or Remove it with an audit row, while everyone else gets a 404 that is byte equal to an unknown route on every method (D-08, R-03 Remove authority, correction 2). Before this plan nothing could act on a borderline post, a flagged post or a post whose check never finished, and a held report stayed held for as long as the provider was down. A database test run on `p4_0423` proves each outcome; the screen's behaviour on top of this API is 04-26.

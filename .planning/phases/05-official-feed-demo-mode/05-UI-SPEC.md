# Phase 5: UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 5 (Official feed and Demo): the Official alerts page filled with real alerts, the one line Nearby banner, the GDACS official pin and its read only card, the Demo, the sign in globe, and the Settings row for silent official alerts (the first-time card was dropped on 2026-10-27, see 6b). Built on `03.4-UI-SPEC.md` (the baseline) and `03.3-UI-SPEC.md` (the Notifications rows). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, to be verified by gsd-ui-checker. Nothing was put to the owner while writing this file: placement and look are judged at the viewing session, and every open choice is in "Defaults to confirm at the viewing session".

## Source of Truth and Scope

**Decisions treated as the contract:** D-01 to D-16 of `05-CONTEXT.md` (D-16 is the owner's answer on state only Severe alerts; OWNER DECIDED and LOCKED EARLIER are fixed; CLAUDE'S DISCRETION items stand on the defaults written here), the validated popup and trust block in `.claude/skills/sketch-findings-pinalert` (kept as built), and sketch `009-login-globe` placement A (owner, 2026-10-17: "i like A", and the realistic Earth).

**Tokens, type sizes, spacing, radii, the page frame, the bars, the places layout and every component named below come from `03.4-UI-SPEC.md` and the built CSS** (`main.css`, `places.css`, `app-bar.css`, `feed.css`, `phone.css`, `welcome.css`, `auth.css`). This file re-declares none of them. It adds only what a Phase 5 surface cannot do without: **two icon files, one stylesheet per new surface, and the tags and chips listed under "New components" and "Departures"**. No new colour hue, no new font size, no new weight, no new spacing value.

**Stack:** Go `html/template`, vanilla JS, hand written CSS, Leaflet 1.9.4 with the MapLibre basemap, maplibre-gl 5.24.0 (already pinned with SRI) for the globe. No framework, no build step, no new library. Tool: none. The shadcn gate does not apply (no `components.json`, no `package.json`).

**Sample data rule:** every name, number and sentence quoted from the feed or the sketches ("Krishna district", "IMD Chennai", "Lightning") is a sample. Real values come from the server. The browser authors no alert level, no area match, no freshness state and no trust figure: the server sends `state`, `age_seconds`, the groups and the banner pick, and the page draws them (research Pattern 2, the TRUST-02 rule).

**Not designed here (stays as built or belongs to another phase):** shading alert areas on the map, the rain outlook line, the replay slider, the explainer video (its slot beside Try the demo is reserved and not drawn), the report page (Phase 8), the sign in card itself (Phase 8 replaces it), the Low data view (it gets no banner, no Official alerts and no globe, see Defaults), push delivery mechanics (research G7), and the lock screen text beyond the one table in Surface 6.

**Dependencies on sessions that are still landing:** Phase 3.3 is being committed to main now and changes `welcome.html.tmpl` from four steps to five (every "Step n of 4" becomes "of 5", step 5 Alerts is added) and adds the Notifications rows to `settings.html.tmpl`. **Amended 2026-10-27:** at the owner's request, quick 261027-t5w (merged 17ee257) took step 5 out again; the flow is four steps counted "of 4", and the alerts ask is a pop-up after step 4 (`dialog#welcome-alerts-ask.logout-confirm`, "Get alerts for critical reports near you?", Not now and Allow). ## Color

No new hue. 60/30/10 as the app lays it out: `--color-bg` dominant, `--color-surface` and `--color-border` secondary (details panel, status notice, column head row, Demo strip, neutral chips), severity tokens as the accent.

**One level mapping, used everywhere (banner, page chip, details, GDACS chip).** This departs from 03.4 Surface 4 ("Severe in the critical tint, Moderate in the medium tint") because the owner decided in D-07 that Extreme is red and Severe is orange:

| Level | Chip fill | Chip and banner border | Icon (file) and its colour | Word |
|-------|-----------|------------------------|----------------------------|------|
| Extreme | `--color-severity-critical-bg` | 1px `--color-severity-critical` | `octagon-alert.svg`, `--color-tone-bad` | Extreme |
| Severe | `--color-severity-medium-bg` | 1px `--color-severity-medium` | `triangle-alert.svg`, `--color-tone-caution` | Severe |
| Moderate, Minor, Unknown | `--color-surface` | 1px `--color-border` | `circle-alert.svg`, `--color-text` | Moderate, Minor, Unknown |

Text on every tint is `--color-text` (never muted). The icon colours are the tone tokens because `--color-severity-medium` on its light tint measures about 2.9 to 1, under the 3 to 1 a graphic needs, while `--color-tone-caution` measures about 4.8 to 1; `--color-tone-bad` and `--color-tone-caution` clear 4.5 to 1 on their tints in both themes. The executor adds the six pairs to `TestPhase4ContrastPairs` or a sibling. The app's amber is the Severe "orange"; a truer orange is a viewing session choice and not a new token here.

**GDACS levels use the same chip family and words of their own.** Red uses the Extreme look, Orange the Severe look, Green the neutral look (never the Low green, which belongs to crowd reports). The chip words are Red, Orange, Green.

**Accent reserved for** (extends the 03.4 closed list by items 9 to 11):
1. to 8. as 03.4 (severity dot and tint, chosen severity box, the 4px edges, the Disputed line, the Call 112 box, the alert fill, the strip icon, the Resolved chip).

**Destructive:** none in this phase (no destructive action exists in the Demo, the page or the Settings switch).

The event phrase is built from the closed table (never the feed headline, which can be Telugu or long): Cyclone, Heavy rain, Flood, Lightning, Thunderstorm, Heat wave, Cold wave, Tsunami, Earthquake, each followed by " warning"; anything else reads "Weather alert". The area is our own name for the district ("Krishna district") or, for a state level Extreme alert, the state ("Andhra Pradesh").

**What shortens:** only the area, with an ellipsis. The icon, the event phrase, "+2" and the chevron never shrink. Fit at 320px (the worst case, phone flush row): 320 minus 32 padding leaves 288; icon and gap 28, chevron and gap 24, leaving about 236 for the text without a "+N"; "Cyclone warning · " takes about 125 and leaves about 110 for the area, so "Krishna district" shortens to "Krishna dist…" only with a "+N" or the longest phrase "Thunderstorm warning". ## Interaction and Accessibility

- **Focus order, Official alerts:** top bar, status line (not focusable), then each row or card in order; the details panel follows its button. The group label is a heading level 3 (`h3`) so a screen reader can jump between groups.
- **Names:** the disclosure button's name is the row content with the level word first ("Extreme. Cyclone warning ..."); `aria-expanded` follows the panel. The banner name is in Surface 2. The pin name is in Surface 3. Exit's name is "Exit demo" (amended 2026-10-29 by the Phase 5 review fix WR-T07, was "Exit the demo"). The authority badge reads "Official" or "GDACS" as text, the Demo tag reads "Demo".
- **Colour is never alone:** level is icon shape plus word plus tint; the GDACS pin is told apart by shape and word; Demo is told apart by a word and a dashed ring; the stale notice has an icon and words.
- **Live regions:** `#notice-live` for the banner announcement; `#feed-status` for "Practice report confirmed..."; the status line is not live. Errors use `role="alert"` as built.
- **Reduced motion:** the chevron turn, the pin growth and the globe fade are instant; the globe stands still on one frame, live. No scroll triggered animation, no crawl.
- **Targets:** every pressed control is 44px or taller. The 24px tags are not controls.
- **Text size:** the banner stays one line and grows in height; its area shortens first. Rows and cards wrap or grow; no horizontal page scroll at 320 and 200 percent text.
- **Escape:** closes the GDACS popup or sheet (as the crowd card); the Disagree box is cleared by the step change as built.
- **Forced colours:** see Color.

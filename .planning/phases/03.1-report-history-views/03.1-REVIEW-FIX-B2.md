## 3. The screen

**Commit:** 373cdcb (plus 16675d6, a one line follow up that spells the two dash
characters of the partial test as escapes)
**Files:** `web/static/js/feed.js`, `web/past_tab_contract_test.go`, `web/past_partial_contract_test.go` (new)

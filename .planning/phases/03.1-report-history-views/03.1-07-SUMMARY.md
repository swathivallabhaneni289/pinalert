# Phase 3.1 Plan 07: Activity page follows the undo limit and blurs voted finished Rescue Summary

The Activity page's Reopen now spends the same two undos as the take back and shows "You have used both undos on this report." instead of a button the server would refuse, and voted cards of someone else's finished Rescue report show the coarse report (Rescue needed, Approximate location, no text) through the shared coarsening.

## What was built

- **internal/service/auth.go**: `AuthQuerier.ResolutionUndoCounts`; `ActivityReport.UndosLeft` and `ActivityReport.Approximate`. `ActivityPageForAccount` collects the ids of own posts that are Retracted with an expiry after now, makes one `ResolutionUndoCounts` call for them (none when there are no such posts) and sets `UndosLeft` from `UndosLeft(UndosUsed(row, true))`. For voted cards, a `rescue_needed` card whose `ViewOf` is resolved or past gets `CoarsePoint` applied to latitude, longitude and geohash, its description emptied and `Approximate` true. The read order and count of the existing reads are unchanged.
- **internal/api/handlers/auth.go**: `profileReport.UndosUsed`; `CanReopen` is closed, unexpired and `UndosLeft > 0`; `UndosUsed` is closed, unexpired and `UndosLeft <= 0`; an Approximate card gets the title `Rescue needed` (C17) and ` · Approximate location` (C18) appended to Detail, in the same separator `profileDetail` uses.
- **web/templates/profile.html.tmpl**: `{{if .UndosUsed}}data-undos-used="true"{{end}}` beside `data-can-reopen`.
- **web/static/js/activity.js**: a fixed constant `UNDOS_USED_SENTENCE` (C52) and an `else if (row.dataset.undosUsed === 'true' && body)` branch that appends a `p.vote-note` through `Pinalert.setText`. The Reopen branch, the click listener, `onReopenClick` and its one `showToast` call are untouched.

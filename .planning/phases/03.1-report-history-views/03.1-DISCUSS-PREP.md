## 2. Open for the owner (highest impact first)

Q1 Phone: you are on the map and tap Past. How do you know the grey pins are old, not live?
- Today the selector would live in the list pane, which is hidden in phone map mode (main.css .pane--list). The old checkbox has the same flaw.
- Recommend: four plain text tabs (like Instagram's Posts, Reels, Tagged), always reachable, not a dropdown. Whenever the view is not Active, a one line label sits on the map ("Showing: Past, last 24 hours", tap to go back), and finished pins look different from live ones.
- If not: readers mistake yesterday's flooded road for a live one.

Q2 You search "Velachery", like Google Maps. What moves?
- Today the map never reloads when you pan (setCenter runs once, map.js line 221). distance_km measures from the search point, not from you, so every row there shows "Too far to vote".
- Recommend: only a search moves the list. A line says "Showing reports near Velachery. Back to my location". Distances read "from Velachery". Reading works anywhere, voting needs you within 1 km. The search box sits on the map. Press Enter, up to 5 results, no type ahead, so reporters keep the single geocoder lane.
- If a drag should also reload, that needs a "Search this area" button, which is more load. Deferred.

Q3 You set Category: Flood, close the app, and tomorrow's bookmark still has it. Can a filter hide a critical or rescue report?
- Today no filters exist. Phase 3.2 keeps urgent reports visible only for its Reliable filter.
- Recommend: filters narrow exactly as asked, with a visible "Filtered by Flood. Clear" line, and a fresh open without the filter starts clear. An emptied panel says "Nothing matches your filters", never the HIST-04 line.
- Fork: "urgent always shown" protects rescue requests but makes Severity: Low show Critical rows.

Q4 The poster taps Mark resolved by mistake, or two strangers close a report while water is rising. Where do people undo it?
- Today only the poster has Reopen, on the Activity page. Nearby non posters have no button anywhere. HIST-01 says no voting in Resolved.
- Recommend: the poster alone sees Reopen on their own Resolved row (existing control, no new power).
- Fork: no controls at all (literal roadmap), or nearby people can also reopen (new capability, deferred).

Q5 Finished rescue reports: how coarse?
- Today exact lat and lon is sent, and the text may hold a house address or phone number (04 D-05).
- Recommend: rescue needed only (the requirement text), pin at about 1 km accuracy marked "Approximate location", and no description on finished rescue rows.
- If all history is coarse: simpler privacy story, less useful road history.

Q6 A critical report was out disputed but stayed in Active. After it expires, Past says "Disputed". OK?
- Today ClassifyOutcome says contradicted. 03 R-06 says the owner never confirmed this.
- Recommend: keep "Disputed", so Past agrees with the reporter rating.
- Otherwise: show neutral "Expired", and R-06 must change too.

Q7 Time and order.
- Recommend: one shared chooser "24 hours, 3 days, 7 days" for Resolved and Past, default 24 hours. Newest first in history. Active keeps urgent first (feed.js sortReports). "Resolved today" means the last 24 hours, not the calendar day.
- If fixed at 7 days: simpler, but long lists of old news.

Plan for the session: ask Q1 to Q5 one at a time in plain words. Q6 last if the owner is still engaged. Q7 is decided as the recommended default and listed in CONTEXT.md for the owner to veto.

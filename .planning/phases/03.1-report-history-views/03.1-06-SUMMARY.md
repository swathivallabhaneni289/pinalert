# Phase 3.1 Plan 06: History reads in the service Summary

The service now composes the Past page (Resolved group then Expired group, 25 each, honest cut flags), the 24 hour counts for the empty Active tab, the single report read for links, and the viewer fields (view, your resolution, undos left) on every read path, all from the one resolver.

## What was built

- `internal/service/report.go`: Querier gains `ExpiredReportsInWindow`, `OpenReportsInArea`, `ReportForFocus` and `ResolutionUndoCounts`. `NearbyQuery.Category` filters in Go after Resolve (step 11). Nearby sets `View` (ViewOf) and `ViewerResolution` and ends with `attachUndos` (step 12, numbered in the doc comment). Steps 1 to 10 are unchanged.
- `internal/service/feed.go`: `ReportView` gains `View`, `History *HistoryFacts`, `ViewerResolution`, `UndosLeft` and `Approximate`, each documented.
- `internal/service/historyread.go` (new):
  - `attachUndos` sets `UndosLeft` to `UndoLimit` on every view and makes one `ResolutionUndoCounts` call only for unexpired views where the viewer has a resolution row or posted the report. An ordinary page makes zero calls. Viewer 0 never reads.
  - `History`: one `now`, both candidate reads (caps 500 and 1000) de duplicated by id, an empty pair returns at once, then reporter accounts, reporter history (only with accounts), one vote read and the signals. Per candidate: tally, Resolve, ViewOf. Resolved is kept when `ClosureOf.At` is after the window start, Past only from the expired set. Category filter, then sort (closure time or expiry, newest first, ties by id descending), then cap 25 per group with the cut flags, then `buildFinishedView`, then `attachUndos`.
  - `buildFinishedView`: base view, `BuildHistoryFacts` with `ClassifyOutcome` for Past and the tally's `ConfirmCells`, then `CoarsenFinishedRescue` with the query centre, setting `Approximate`. Every finished view on every path goes through it.
  - `RecentHistoryCounts`: window 24 hours, both candidate reads, one reporter read and one vote read, counts Resolved by closure time and Expired from the expired set. No reporter history and no undo read.
  - `Focus`: `ReportForFocus` (pgx.ErrNoRows gives unavailable, other errors returned), expiry at or before now minus 7 days gives too_old with no further read, then one report History style, none gives unavailable, a resolved report with an old closure gives too_old, finished views through `buildFinishedView`, live views through the same base fields as Nearby, then `attachUndos`.
- Tests: `TestCategoryFilterNearby` and `TestNearbyFillsViewerResolution` in feed_test.go (plus the four fake methods, argument capture and configurable rows); in historyread_test.go `TestHistoryGroupsAndOrder`, `TestHistoryResolvedOutsideWindowDropped`, `TestHistoryCapAndCut`, `TestHistoryCategoryFilterBeforeCap`, `TestHistoryOutcomeAndClosedBy`, `TestFinishedRescueCoarsenedOnEveryPath` (History and Focus subtests, asserting all four location fields and the description), `TestHistoryEmptyCostsNoBatchedReads`, `TestRecentHistoryCounts`, `TestFocusStatuses` and `TestUndoReadOnlyWhenNeeded`.

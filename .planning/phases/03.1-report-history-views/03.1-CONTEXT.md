# Phase 03.1: Report history views - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-13 (the discussion ran from the evening into the early hours of 2026-10-14, IST)
**Status:** Ready for planning. Three items wait on the owner (see "Waiting on the owner"): the row size pick from sketch 006, undoing a resolve, and finished rescue reports. Everything else is decided or a recommended default.

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A reader can see what happened to reports near them, not only what is live. One tab strip at the top of the list, Active | Disputed | Past, replaces the "Show disputed reports" checkbox on every screen size. The Past tab lists reports from other people that were closed or faded out in the last 7 days, clearly labelled as history and never mixed into Active or its counts. A reader can search a place to see reports for another area, and can narrow the list with category chips that only filter when tapped. The report rows in the list get smaller. Nothing else on the page is redesigned.

Not in this phase: the reader's own history (My votes, My disputes and the six tab Activity page are Phase 3.2), in app updates and trusted reporter alerts (3.2), official alerts (5), a page wide design pass (backlog 999.10), a "Search this area" button, any change to how trust is computed.

Requirements: HIST-01 to HIST-06, with the wording amendments listed under "Amendments to make".
</domain>

### The tab strip (HIST-01)
- **D-01 [OWNER DECIDED]:** One tab strip with three tabs, Active, Disputed and Past, replaces the "Show disputed reports" checkbox, at the top of the list on the laptop home page. The owner: "instead of having that show having like the show disputed in a checkbox we should have it like a like a tab where it shows you like the current issues going and then disputed ones and then the past um also those three tabs should be on the home page for the laptop version". Why: a checkbox hides a mode and the owner found it confusing on a phone, while three visible tabs show every set one tap away.
- **D-02 [OWNER DECIDED]:** The phone gets the same strip at the top of the Feed list, and the phone bottom bar stays Feed and Activity as built in quick task 261013-lrw. Asked whether the phone should have the same three tabs, the owner answered "Yes, yes." Why: one structure on every screen size means one `view` state and one set of tests. This replaces two earlier answers (see "Superseded answers").
- **D-03 [LOCKED EARLIER]:** The views are a disjoint partition: Active is Live and Provisional, Disputed is Hidden, Resolved is closed by people, Past is expired. A report closed and then expired stays Resolved, a hidden report that expires moves to Past, and critical and rescue needed reports are never Hidden so they never reach Disputed (02-CONTEXT D-06 and D-08, ROADMAP criterion 2). The Past tab shows Resolved and Expired as two groups. Why: no report may appear twice or change an Active count.
- **D-04 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** The server side classifier keeps four words (active, resolved, disputed, past) and the Past tab shows both resolved and past. `view=resolved` and `view=past` both open the Past tab (scrolled to the matching group), and the default Active view is never written into the address. Why: Phase 4's ModRow `view` and its planned `moderate.js`, and Phase 3.2's deep link `/?view=resolved&report=ID`, already name those words, and a main address with a query string is never sent to the Low data view.
- **D-05 [PLANNER]:** `show_disputed` becomes one `view` setting. Old `show_disputed=true` bookmarks map to `view=disputed`, the page keeps `replaceState` and the pageshow re read, and `web/static/js/app.js` keeps one `fetch(` for the list. Why: Phase 4 pins exactly two `fetch(` calls in `web/static/js/app.js` and one `show_disputed` "or Phase 3.1's built equivalent" (`.planning/phases/04-robustness-real-world-resilience/04-PLAN-OUTLINE.md`).
- **D-06 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** Tab labels are Active, Disputed and Past, with the heading inside the Past tab reading "Past reports". The owner's own word for the first tab was "current issues going", while the app and the roadmap say Active and the count line already reads "N active". UI-SPEC may choose another word but uses one word on every surface. Why: Phase 5 adds Alerts and Following tabs beside Nearby, so these words must stay short and consistent.

### The Past tab (HIST-02, HIST-03, HIST-04)
- **D-07 [OWNER DECIDED]:** The Past tab shows what happened to reports from other people, so people know what happened. It is not the reader's own history. The owner: "then maybe we can have that as a past report. What do you say? Or like give it a name. And then we can have those uh, in uh, like the reports from other people closed. In that page so we know what happened". The owner left the name to Claude: Past reports. Why: the Activity page already holds a person's own posts, and Past answers a different question, what happened near me.
- **D-08 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** The tab has two groups on one screen, RESOLVED then EXPIRED, for a fixed last 7 days with no time choice, newest first, and it includes the reader's own closed reports. Cards follow the owner's Past picture of 2026-10-13 (white cards, a bold title, one grey meta line) with the app's real strings: "hrs" not "h", and "faded after N hrs" because a confirmation does not extend a report's life (backlog 999.12). Why: the picture the owner chose draws exactly this, and 7 days is the roadmap's longest window.
- **D-09 [LOCKED EARLIER]:** Resolved rows say who closed the report (the poster alone, or independent confirmers) and show the Phase 3 counters, so self resolving cannot hide a dispute. Expired rows carry an outcome label from `ClassifyOutcome`, with the neutral "Expired" for critical and rescue needed reports that expire unconfirmed (HIST-02, HIST-03, 03-CONTEXT D-11 and D-12, R-05). The wording uses the Phase 3 people words ("N people nearby"), never "places". Why: history labels must not disagree with the reporter rating (`docs/TRUST-MODEL.md`, "Hand offs to later phases").
- **D-10 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** A critical or rescue needed report that was out disputed and then expired shows "Disputed" in the Past tab, in line with the reporter rating (03-CONTEXT R-06, which the owner never confirmed on its own). Why: showing neutral "Expired" instead would force R-06 to change as well.
- **D-11 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** Past rows are read only, with no Confirm or Dispute. Tapping a row opens that report on the map through `/?report=ID` (and `/?view=resolved&report=ID`), and the link must open a report outside the reader's radius and outside the 7 day window. Why: Phase 3.2's Updates and Phase 4's moderator links open a report by id, and a held, removed or fully aged report needs defined copy.
- **D-12 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** When the Active tab is empty its message says what happened recently using real counts only (for example "2 were resolved in the last 24 hours") and links to the Past tab; "today" means the last 24 hours, not the calendar day (HIST-04). Why: no invented numbers.
- **D-13 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** The map follows the list (ROADMAP criterion 1). While Disputed or Past is selected it shows only that tab's reports, a one line label on the map names the tab, finished pins look different from live ones and offer no vote, and official pins (Phase 5) ignore the tab. Why: otherwise yesterday's flooded road reads as live.

### Looking at another area (HIST-05)
- **D-14 [OWNER DECIDED]:** A search box on the map. The reader types a place and presses Enter (up to 5 results, no type ahead), the map and the list move there, and a line says "Showing reports near Velachery. Back to my location." Distances read "from Velachery". Reading works anywhere, voting still needs the reader within 1 km so far rows say "Too far to vote", and dragging the map does not reload the list. The owner: "Okay, yeah, let's add it." Why: it answers "what is happening over there" without a second app, and no type ahead keeps reporters' address search from being starved.
- **D-15 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** The Disputed and Past tabs follow the same area as Active, a searched place included. Why: one place for all three tabs.
- **D-16 [PLANNER]:** Reader searches get their own budget or a cache, and a failed search never blocks anything. Why: the geocoder allows 1 request per second for the whole process (`internal/geocode/client.go`, Phase 7 D-02), and Phase 7 D-04 says a failed search never gates reporting.

### Filters (HIST-06)
- **D-17 [OWNER DECIDED]:** Category chips (All, Flood, Rescue, Shelter, Power) over the map never hide anything by default. They only narrow the list when a reader taps one, All is the default, and rescue and critical reports are not specially hidden. The owner (22:14 IST, to the other session): "it won't hide anything or nor uh, the rescue reports" and "that's when they'll click it but other than that there's no hiding of anything". Why: the filter is the reader's own request, not the app's judgement.
- **D-18 [RECOMMENDED DEFAULT]:** A chip narrows exactly as asked. While one is on, a line says "Filtered by Flood. Clear", a fresh open starts clear (nothing is remembered), and an emptied list says "Nothing matches your filters" and never the HIST-04 "what happened recently" line. The phone has category chips only, as drawn; a severity filter is not built unless the owner asks. Why: a reader must always see why the list is short.
